# Netnography and Digital Corpus Protocol

Use for studies based on forums, blogs, social media, videos, web archives, online testimonies, or mixed grey literature.

## Required Corpus Fields

| ID | Region | Language | Platform | Source type | Date | Access date | Archive URL | Evidence features | Reliability | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Source Type Hierarchy

A: peer-reviewed / archival / academic book.
B: professional or institutional source with stable authorship.
C: detailed public testimony or video evidence.
D: forum/blog/social post, useful but weak.
E: single visual lead or unstable/unverifiable page.

## Ethics Checklist

- Is the platform public, semi-public, or private?
- Is the user identifiable?
- Are direct quotes traceable by search?
- Should usernames be omitted or paraphrased?
- Are screenshots/images reproduced? If yes, justify permission/fair use.
- Are deleted/archived pages handled responsibly?

## Method Checklist

- Search log exists.
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria are explicit.
- Coding manual exists.
- Translation method is declared.
- Weak evidence is not used as proof of strong historical claims.
