# Claim Support Audit

Use for citation checking and manuscript integrity checks.

## Claim Status

- `supported`: cited source directly supports the claim.
- `partially-supported`: source supports part of the claim, but scope is broader in manuscript.
- `overstated`: source is relevant but manuscript claim is stronger than evidence.
- `unsupported`: cited source does not support the claim.
- `needs-source`: claim has no source and should be sourced or softened.
- `unclear`: source unavailable or ambiguous.

## Procedure

1. Extract factual, causal, novelty, and evidence claims.
2. Bind each claim to its citation or mark uncited.
3. Inspect cited source where possible.
4. Judge support status.
5. Recommend delete / soften / add source / reframe.

## High-risk claims

- first / unique / no prior literature;
- proves / demonstrates / effective;
- causal mechanisms;
- safety claims;
- legal/ethical/compliance claims;
- clinical or health guidance.
