# Literature Review Protocol

## Purpose

Produce a grounded literature review, not a source dump.

## Steps

1. Define the research question and scope.
2. Specify databases/search surfaces and date/language limits.
3. Search by concepts and synonyms, not only by exact title terms.
4. Screen sources using explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria.
5. Grade each source by reliability and relevance.
6. Synthesize by themes, methods, populations/materials, and unresolved controversies.
7. Produce a gap map and next-search recommendations.

## Output

| Theme | Sources | What is established | Disagreement | Gap | Use in paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Do not cite a source you have not inspected.
- Separate source existence from claim support.
- Mark preprints and grey literature explicitly.
