# Editorial Decision Standards

## Accept

Use only when no major conceptual, methodological, evidence, ethics, or fit issues remain.

## Minor Revision

Use when the core contribution and evidence are sound and changes are local.

## Major Revision / Revise and Resubmit

Use when the paper is promising but needs substantial reframing, method transparency, evidence correction, safety/ethics additions, or structural revision.

## Reject with Invitation to Resubmit

Use when the topic is valuable but the current framing or method cannot support the central claim.

## Reject

Use when the manuscript is unsound, out of scope, unethical, or not recoverable without becoming a different paper.

## Decision Rule

The decision must follow the strongest unresolved major concern, not the average tone of the reviews.
