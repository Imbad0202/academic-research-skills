# Devil's Advocate Protocol

## Purpose

Identify the strongest reasons a serious journal, reviewer, editor, or ethics board could reject the manuscript.

## Required Output

For each objection:

1. Rejection reason.
2. Manuscript evidence or quote.
3. Why it matters.
4. Concrete fix.
5. Whether the fix is major, moderate, or minor.

## Common Attack Lines

- Novelty overclaimed.
- Evidence weaker than conclusion.
- Methods not reproducible.
- Grey sources treated as strong proof.
- Citations exist but do not support claims.
- Ethics/disclosure incomplete.
- Genre ambiguity.
- Safety, legal, or clinical risk.
- Theory name-dropping.
- Target journal mismatch.

## Rule

Be skeptical but fair. Do not invent fatal flaws; identify recoverable fixes where possible.
