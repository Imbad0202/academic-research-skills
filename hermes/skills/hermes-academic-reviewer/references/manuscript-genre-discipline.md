# Manuscript Genre Discipline

Do not review every manuscript as an empirical IMRaD article.

## Conceptual / methodological papers

Evaluate:
- recoverability of reasoning or reconstruction;
- adequacy of corpus/material;
- clarity of contribution;
- whether claims are framed as potential vs demonstrated effect;
- limitations and future validation.

## Netnography / digital corpus papers

Evaluate:
- search log;
- corpus inventory;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- source reliability hierarchy;
- coding protocol;
- translation verification;
- ethics of online material.

## Sport pedagogy / PE papers

Evaluate:
- safety and age adaptation;
- curriculum fit;
- lesson progression;
- teacher competencies;
- evidence status of pedagogical outcomes.
