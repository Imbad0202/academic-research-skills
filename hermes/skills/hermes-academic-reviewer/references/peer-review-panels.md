# Peer Review Panels

Select reviewers by manuscript genre.

## Completed conceptual/methodological paper

- Reviewer 1: domain specialist.
- Reviewer 2: methodology/design/reconstruction specialist.
- Reviewer 3: theory/source/implementation specialist.
- Devil's Advocate: rejection-risk reviewer.

## Netnography / comparative corpus

- Reviewer 1: folklore/traditional games/heritage.
- Reviewer 2: netnography/digital ethnography methods.
- Reviewer 3: comparative morphology/diffusion model.
- Devil's Advocate: overclaiming and source weakness.

## Sport pedagogy / physical education

- Reviewer 1: PE methodology/curriculum.
- Reviewer 2: motor learning/praxeology.
- Reviewer 3: safety/ethics/age adaptation.
- Devil's Advocate: school implementation risk.

## Rule

Independent reviewers should be prompted separately before editorial synthesis.
