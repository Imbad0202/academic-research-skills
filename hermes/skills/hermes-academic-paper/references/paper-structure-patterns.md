# Paper Structure Patterns

## Empirical IMRaD

Title, Abstract, Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Limitations, Conclusion, References.

## Conceptual / Theoretical

Problem, conceptual gap, framework, argument sections, counterarguments, implications, limitations.

## Methodological / Design Paper

Problem, material/corpus, reconstruction/design procedure, operational model, application pathway, limitations, validation agenda.

## Literature Review

Problem, search/scope, thematic synthesis, debates, gaps, future research.

## Policy Brief

Problem, evidence, options, recommendation, implementation risks.

## Rule

Match structure to genre. Do not force a conceptual/methodological article into empirical IMRaD.
