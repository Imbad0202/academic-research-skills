# Disclosure and Declarations Checklist

Check target venue requirements first.

## Common Declarations

- Author contributions / CRediT.
- Funding statement.
- Conflict of interest.
- Data availability.
- Ethics approval or exemption.
- Consent for images, minors, interviews, or personal data.
- AI-use disclosure.
- Acknowledgements.

## AI-use Disclosure Pattern

State:
- tool/provider if required;
- tasks performed (language editing, formatting, translation, brainstorming, code assistance);
- what was not delegated (study design, analysis, conclusions, final responsibility);
- human review and responsibility.

## Pitfall

Do not say "no human participants" if the manuscript reports pedagogical try-outs, interviews, photos of minors, or user testimonies without clarifying why these are not human-subjects research.
