# Example: Sport Pedagogy / Physical Education Review

Reviewer panel:

- PE methodology / curriculum;
- motor learning / motor praxeology;
- safety / ethics / age adaptation;
- devil's advocate.

Key checks:

- lesson progression;
- age scaling;
- safe surfaces and contact rules;
- teacher supervision;
- whether pedagogical effects are measured or hypothetical;
- ethics for minors/photos/practical try-outs;
- fit for school PE vs club/recreation context.
