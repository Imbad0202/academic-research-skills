# Example: Netnography / Comparative Corpus Review

Use for manuscripts based on web forums, blogs, videos, social media, and multilingual grey literature.

Reviewer panel:

- folklore / traditional games / heritage;
- netnography / digital ethnography methodology;
- comparative morphology / diffusion model;
- devil's advocate.

Key checks:

- search log;
- corpus inventory;
- source reliability levels;
- translation audit;
- inclusion criteria vs evidence of relatedness;
- ethics of online material;
- confidence levels for historical route claims.
