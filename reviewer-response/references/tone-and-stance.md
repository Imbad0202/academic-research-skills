# Tone And Stance

Preferred stance:

- respectful
- specific
- evidence-linked
- non-defensive

When disagreeing:

1. acknowledge the reviewer's concern
2. explain the scientific or scope-based reason
3. describe the clarification, revision, or evidence note added to reduce future misunderstanding

Avoid:

- combative wording
- sarcasm
- vague claims that changes were made "throughout"
- unexplained refusal to run a requested analysis, experiment, or rewrite
