## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer):** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its statistical machinery would survive a hostile re-analysis. As an adversarial challenger I read it as demanding that sampling, assignment, measurement, missing-data handling, effect estimation, uncertainty reporting, and reproducibility affordances be stated precisely enough that a skeptic could attempt replication and fail to find hidden degrees of freedom. It is not enough that a method be named; the design must actually license the inferences drawn from it, and any gap between what was measured and what is claimed to have been measured counts against the paper. I am not the owner of this dimension and will not score it, but I note that its findings bear on my own dimension whenever the argument leans on a statistical result.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer):** Here the challenge is whether the manuscript's factual substrate holds. Terminology, constructs, instruments, and cited prior work must be represented as the field actually understands them, not as a convenient paraphrase that flatters the paper's thesis. Adversarially, I would look for prior work recruited as support when it in fact reports something narrower or contrary, for domain terms used with drifting meanings, and for claims stated as settled when the evidence base is contested. This dimension is outside my scoring remit, though misrepresented sources are also an argumentative defect when the misrepresentation is load-bearing.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by me as devil's advocate):** This is the dimension I must adjudicate. The question is whether the central thesis survives internal pressure: do the stated premises actually entail the conclusion, does each claim's strength match the evidence offered for it, and does the paper avoid inferential moves that only look valid at reading speed. My adversarial posture is to reconstruct the paper's argument as a chain, then attack the weakest link — unwarranted causal language over correlational evidence, selection or survivorship effects treated as findings, conclusions in the abstract or discussion that exceed what the results section licenses, definitional slippage between the construct named in the hypothesis and the construct actually operationalised, unaddressed rival explanations, and circularity where the outcome is partly constitutive of the predictor. Local writing quality is irrelevant here; what matters is whether the argument would still stand if a determined opponent granted every datum but disputed every inference.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer):** The demand is that a competent reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper without the authors' tacit knowledge, and that any claim reaching beyond the home discipline is actually earned rather than asserted. Adversarially, the failure mode is a manuscript whose framing presumes insider definitions while advertising broad implications it never substantiates. I do not score this dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role):** This covers organisation, expositional clarity, the informativeness and honesty of figures and tables, and conformity to venue conventions. The adversarial reading is that presentation choices can conceal as well as reveal — truncated axes, tables without denominators, sections that bury the limitation that matters — but the dimension itself is presentational, and it is not mine to score.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role):** The question is whether this manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it adds something its readership does not already have. A hostile reading asks whether the contribution is genuinely novel or a restatement of an established result in new local clothing, and whether the scope matches the venue's remit. Outside my scoring remit.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the paper's thesis can be reconstructed as an explicit premise-to-conclusion chain in which the operationalised constructs match the constructs named in the claims, the inferential language (associative versus causal versus predictive) matches the design that produced the evidence, rival explanations including selection, survivorship, reverse causation, and confounding are named and addressed, and the abstract, results, and discussion assert claims of the same strength rather than escalating across sections.
what_triggers_block: The central conclusion depends on an inferential step the paper's own evidence cannot license — for example a causal or effectiveness claim resting on observational association, a predictor and outcome that are definitionally entangled, or a headline claim whose supporting result is absent, contradicted, or reversed elsewhere in the manuscript — such that the thesis fails even if every reported number is granted.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument survives but with identifiable weak links that are fixable in text — overreaching or hedge-free phrasing in the abstract or discussion relative to the results, one or more plausible rival explanations left unaddressed, mild construct drift between hypothesis and measure, or unsupported bridging assertions that are not themselves load-bearing for the main conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript has no coherent recoverable argument at all — the stated conclusion contradicts the reported evidence outright, mutually exclusive claims are asserted as jointly true, or the reasoning is circular at its foundation — so that no revision short of a different study or a different thesis could repair it.
