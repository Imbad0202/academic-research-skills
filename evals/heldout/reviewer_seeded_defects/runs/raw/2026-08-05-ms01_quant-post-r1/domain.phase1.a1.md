## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, analysis, and reporting would survive scrutiny from a competent methodologist in the manuscript's field, and whether enough procedural detail is disclosed that another team could in principle repeat the work. From where I sit as the domain reader, this is the dimension that governs whether the machinery producing the numbers is sound; I do not score it, but I will remain alert to cases where a domain claim is only as good as an undisclosed methodological choice, and I will route such observations to the methodology reviewer rather than adjudicating them myself.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This is my dimension. It requires that every substantive claim be consistent with the current state of evidence in the paper's field, that prior work be characterised as its authors actually characterised it, and that field-specific terminology, constructs, measures, instruments, and reported results be used correctly and without factual error. It covers three distinct failure surfaces: claims that contradict or overstate the established evidence base; citations and literature summaries that misattribute, misdate, or misrepresent what earlier studies found; and internal misuse of the field's technical vocabulary or misstatement of the paper's own or others' numerical results. Accuracy here is judged against the domain literature, not against the paper's internal logic, which belongs to D3.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, DA role with methodology eligibility).** This dimension governs whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated evidence actually licenses the stated conclusions, whether intermediate steps follow, and whether any reasoning defect is severe enough to undermine the main argument rather than merely a peripheral remark. It is distinct from my dimension because a paper can be internally impeccable while resting on domain facts that are wrong, and can be domain-accurate while drawing conclusions its own evidence does not support. I do not score it.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension checks that a reader from a neighbouring discipline can follow the framing, that key definitions are supplied rather than assumed, and that any claim reaching beyond the home field is actually substantiated rather than asserted. It concerns accessibility and the warrant for out-of-field claims; the truth of in-field claims remains mine. I do not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role).** This dimension covers manuscript organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and legibility of figures and tables, and conformity to the target venue's conventions of presentation. It is a presentation judgement, not a truth judgement, and belongs to the editor-in-chief role. I do not score it.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and significant enough to matter to that venue's readership. Originality is partly a domain question — knowing what is already established is what makes novelty assessable — so I may supply factual observations about prior art, but the scoring judgement is the editor's, not mine.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether every domain claim, cited prior finding, technical term, construct definition, measure, and reported result is consistent with the established evidence base of the field; whether references are represented as their originals actually state; whether numerical and statistical results are stated correctly and consistently wherever they recur; and whether hedging matches the strength of the evidence actually available in the literature.
what_triggers_block: A domain claim that is materially wrong or contradicts well-established field evidence, or a substantive misrepresentation of cited prior work, such that a specific stated conclusion cannot stand as written without correction to the underlying facts or citations.
what_triggers_warn: Localised inaccuracies that do not overturn any stated conclusion, such as loose or nonstandard use of a technical term, an imprecise or dated characterisation of the literature, an unhedged claim that outruns the evidence, or a minor inconsistency in a reported figure.
what_triggers_fatal: Pervasive or foundational domain error, including fabricated, non-existent, or systematically misattributed references, or a core factual premise so wrong that the paper's substantive contribution is void regardless of any revision short of a new study.
