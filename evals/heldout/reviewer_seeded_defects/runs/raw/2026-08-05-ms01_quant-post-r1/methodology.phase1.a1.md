## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** This dimension asks whether the study's design can actually bear the weight of the inferences drawn from it. From a methodology standpoint I read this as four linked questions: is the design (experimental, quasi-experimental, or observational) explicitly stated and appropriate to the causal or associational claim being made; is the data provenance, sample construction, inclusion/exclusion, and attrition documented well enough that I can tell who is in the analysis and who dropped out; are the statistical procedures reported completely enough to be evaluated — estimator, model specification, effect sizes with uncertainty intervals, handling of missing data, clustering or nesting structure, and any multiplicity correction; and are there reproducibility affordances such as available code, data or synthetic data, measure definitions, and analytic decisions fixed before rather than after seeing outcomes. The bar is the field's normal peer-review bar, not perfection, so ordinary limitations disclosed honestly are acceptable while undisclosed or unrecoverable design problems are not.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension governs whether the substantive content is factually correct against current evidence in the field: whether cited prior work is characterised as its authors would recognise, whether field-specific terminology and constructs are used in their accepted senses, and whether reported results from other studies are transcribed and interpreted without distortion. I am not scoring it; my role is to leave judgements about the state of domain evidence to the reviewer who owns it, and to confine myself to how the study was conducted and analysed rather than whether its subject-matter claims match the literature.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, methodology eligible).** This dimension concerns the internal logic connecting the manuscript's thesis, its evidence, and its conclusions. My angle on it is narrower than the owner's: I look at whether the inferential chain from the reported measurements to the stated claims holds, whether the strength of the language in the conclusions is calibrated to the strength of the design and the estimates, and whether any step smuggles in an assumption the data cannot support — for example treating an association as a mechanism, generalising beyond the sampled population, or letting a result that failed to reach the stated criterion be narrated as if it had. Fallacies that would still stand even if every number were correct fall under this dimension rather than D1.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This asks whether a competent reader from an adjacent field can follow the framing, definitions, and stated implications, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually backed rather than asserted. I do not score it. Where I encounter methodological vocabulary that may be opaque outside the field, I will note it as observation only and leave the accessibility judgement to the owning reviewer.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This covers organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and legibility of figures and tables, and conformance to venue formatting conventions. I do not score it. My interest in tables and figures is instrumental only: whether they carry the numerical detail needed to evaluate the analysis. Any presentational comment I make is subordinate to that and is not a score on this dimension.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and significant for that readership. I do not score it. Methodological weakness can constrain how large a contribution a paper can legitimately claim, but the decision about fit and significance rests with the owning role, and I will not substitute a design critique for a contribution judgement.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design statement matched to the strength of the claim; documented sampling frame, unit of analysis, exclusions and attrition; complete statistical reporting including model specification, effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, missing-data handling and any nesting or multiplicity adjustment; stated measurement definitions for exposure and outcome variables; and reproducibility affordances such as available code, data or synthetic data, and pre-specification of the analysis.
what_triggers_block: A design or analysis defect that invalidates the paper's principal quantitative inference and cannot be repaired from the reported information — for example a causal or effectiveness claim resting on a self-selected comparison with no adjustment strategy, an outcome measured only on participants who by construction had to persist to be measured, or inferential statistics reported with no denominators, no uncertainty, and no recoverable specification.
what_triggers_warn: Reporting or design gaps that leave the main inference plausible but under-supported and that a revision could close — for example missing effect sizes or intervals alongside reported p-values, unstated missing-data or attrition handling, an undescribed engagement or exposure measure, unaddressed clustering, or no reproducibility artefacts while the underlying analysis remains legible.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported analysis is not the analysis performed or that the numbers cannot be true as printed — internally contradictory sample sizes, degrees of freedom, or subgroup totals that cannot be reconciled; statistics arithmetically impossible given the stated design; outcome or comparison definitions that shift between sections; or indications of undisclosed outcome switching or repeated analysis presented as a single confirmatory test.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each stated conclusion is entailed by the reported estimates under the design actually used, whether hedging in the abstract and discussion matches the hedging warranted by the evidence, whether the population and setting to which conclusions are generalised match those sampled, and whether alternative explanations for the observed pattern are acknowledged and addressed rather than ignored.
what_triggers_block: The central thesis depends on an inferential leap the reported evidence cannot license and the paper's argument does not survive without it — for instance conclusions or recommended actions stated in causal, mechanistic, or policy-directive terms while the evidence base is purely associational, or a headline claim built on a comparison the paper elsewhere concedes is confounded.
what_triggers_warn: Localised overreach or logical slippage that a revision can correct without dismantling the argument — abstract or discussion language stronger than the results section supports, generalisation modestly beyond the sampled population or setting, a plausible rival explanation left unaddressed, or a limitations section that names a threat and then argues as if it had been resolved.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript asserts conclusions that directly contradict its own reported findings, or two sections state mutually incompatible claims about the same result, such that no single coherent thesis can be identified for review.
