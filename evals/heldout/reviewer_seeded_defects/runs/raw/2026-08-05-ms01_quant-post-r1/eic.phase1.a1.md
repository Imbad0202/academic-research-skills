## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and analytic execution would survive scrutiny by a competent specialist reviewer in the field: whether the design actually supports the inferences drawn, whether data provenance, sample construction, exclusions, and missingness are documented, whether statistical procedures are correctly chosen and fully reported rather than gestured at, and whether a reader has enough procedural detail to reproduce or at least audit the analysis. It is owned by the methodology reviewer and is not mine to score; my interest is only that a fatal or blocking judgement here propagates into the panel-level decision arithmetic.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension governs factual fidelity to the substantive literature: whether assertions about the state of evidence are current and defensible, whether cited prior work is characterised as its authors would recognise, and whether field-specific terminology, constructs, instruments, and reported quantities are used correctly rather than loosely. The domain reviewer owns it. Editorially, I treat it as the guardrail against a manuscript that reads well and reasons cleanly yet misstates what the field already knows.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, discourse-analysis role with methodology eligibility).** Here the question is internal: does the central thesis hold together across the manuscript, do the stated conclusions follow from the evidence actually presented rather than from evidence the authors wish they had, and are there reasoning defects — equivocation, circularity, causal overreach, unacknowledged alternative explanations — serious enough to undermine the core claim rather than merely blemish a paragraph. Owned by the DA reviewer; I do not score it, but coherence failures typically co-occur with the venue-fit judgement I do own.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension tests portability of the argument beyond its home specialty: whether framing and definitions let an adjacent-field reader follow the stakes without insider vocabulary, and whether any claims that reach across disciplinary boundaries are actually supported rather than asserted as rhetorical scope. It is the perspective reviewer's call. As chair I note it carries high rather than mandatory weight, so a block here triggers major revision but cannot by itself force rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC-owned).** This is mine. It covers whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can locate the question, the method, the evidence, and the claim without reconstruction work; whether exposition is clear at sentence and section level; whether figures and tables are legible, self-describing, and genuinely informative rather than decorative or redundant; and whether the submission observes the venue's structural and formatting conventions, including proportionality of section lengths to the work reported. Its normal priority means presentational defects alone should drive revision requests, not rejection.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC-owned).** Also mine, and the heavier of my two. I must judge whether the manuscript belongs in this venue at all given its stated scope and readership, and whether it advances something original and consequential for that readership rather than restating settled material, reporting an uncontested local exercise, or offering a contribution too thin to justify the space. Because this dimension is mandatory, a fatal finding here maps to outright rejection under F1 and a block maps to major revision under F2, so my scoring must be calibrated rather than impressionistic.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A locatable research question, sections that follow venue convention and are proportionate to the evidence reported, clear paragraph-level exposition, defined terms and consistent notation, and figures/tables with self-sufficient captions, labelled axes or columns, stated units, and reported sample sizes.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or presentation defects severe enough that a competent reader cannot reliably determine what was measured, on whom, or what the reported quantities mean — for example unlabelled or uninterpretable exhibits carrying the paper's main evidence, missing essential sections, or numbers appearing in text that no table or figure supports.
what_triggers_warn: Recoverable clarity and structure problems that add reader effort without hiding the argument — inconsistent terminology, misordered or bloated sections, captions that require hunting through the body text, uneven citation formatting, or exhibits that duplicate text without adding information.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of what is new relative to named prior work, a contribution whose scope and evidentiary weight match the venue's readership and article type, framing that engages the venue's ongoing conversation, and honest calibration between the strength of the claim and the setting from which it was drawn.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is real but underweight or misaligned for this venue as submitted — novelty asserted rather than positioned against prior work, generalised claims resting on a single narrow deployment, or scope that would better suit a different article type or outlet without substantial reframing.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are adequate but imprecisely argued — thin positioning against the closest prior studies, implications stated more broadly than the evidence licenses, or an unclear statement of intended readership that leaves the increment over existing work partly for the reader to infer.
what_triggers_fatal: The submission is outside the venue's scope or offers no discernible increment to knowledge — a restatement of established findings, an internal or operational report with no transferable claim, or a contribution so slight or so misdirected that no revision within the manuscript's design could make it appropriate here.
