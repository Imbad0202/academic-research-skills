## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor.** The contract asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive the ordinary scrutiny of a competent peer reviewer in this field. Read adversarially, this dimension is a challenge to the paper's evidentiary machinery: could a skeptic reconstruct what was done, from whom data were collected, how cases were included or dropped, and which statistical procedures were applied — and would that reconstruction support the numbers actually reported? Vagueness, undisclosed choices, missing uncertainty estimates, or analyses that cannot in principle answer the question posed are the failure modes this dimension exists to catch. Ownership sits with the methodology reviewer, so my own reading of design flaws is only relevant here insofar as it bears on argument structure.

**D2 — domain_accuracy.** This dimension demands that every substantive claim be consistent with what the field currently knows, that cited prior work be represented as it actually stands rather than as convenient, and that domain terminology and reported results contain no factual errors. The adversarial reading is that a paper can be internally tidy while still misdescribing the literature it leans on or misusing a technical construct in a way that quietly inflates its contribution. Ownership sits with the domain reviewer.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence.** This is the dimension I own. It asks whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated evidence actually licenses the stated conclusions, whether the paper's premises remain stable from abstract through discussion, and whether any fallacy sits close enough to the core argument to bring it down. Adversarially, I am looking for the seam where the paper's rhetoric outruns its warrant — conclusions phrased more strongly than the evidence supports, constructs that shift meaning between where they are measured and where they are interpreted, causal or prescriptive language resting on non-causal or non-prescriptive evidence, and conclusions that would be equally compatible with the opposite finding. A paper can be methodologically clean and factually accurate and still fail here, because the failure is one of inference rather than execution.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance.** The contract requires that framing, definitions, and implications be legible to readers in adjacent fields, and that any claim reaching across disciplinary boundaries be substantiated rather than gestured at. The adversarial test is whether an informed outsider could follow what is being claimed without importing assumptions the paper never states, and whether cross-field claims are backed or merely asserted for reach. Ownership sits with the perspective reviewer.

**D5 — writing_and_structure.** This dimension concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue conventions. Read adversarially, poor structure is not merely cosmetic: it hides gaps, lets unsupported claims migrate between sections without challenge, and makes tables assert things the text never establishes. Ownership sits with the editor-in-chief role.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution.** The contract asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it offers something original and significant enough to matter to that readership. Adversarially, this is the question of whether the paper adds anything beyond confirming what the field already assumed, and whether its scale and ambition match the venue's bar rather than falling below it. Ownership sits with the editor-in-chief role.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated conclusions are entailed by the stated evidence — tracking each headline claim back to the specific result offered for it, checking that constructs keep the same meaning between measurement and interpretation, that the strength of causal, directional, or prescriptive language matches the design's inferential reach, that limitations acknowledged in one section are not contradicted by claims elsewhere, and that the argument would not survive unchanged had the results come out differently.
what_triggers_block: The central thesis rests on an inference the evidence cannot license — for example a causal, temporal, or prescriptive conclusion drawn from evidence that can only establish association or coexistence, a construct silently substituted for a different one between measurement and conclusion, or a headline claim with no traceable supporting result — such that fixing the argument requires retracting or substantially reframing the paper's main claim.
what_triggers_warn: Localised overreach or internal inconsistency that does not reach the core thesis — a discussion or abstract sentence stronger than the corresponding result, an unacknowledged alternative explanation left unaddressed, a limitation stated in one section but contradicted by phrasing in another, or a secondary claim asserted without support — all repairable by rewording and hedging while the central argument stands.
what_triggers_fatal: The argument is not merely overreaching but self-defeating or vacuous at its core — mutually contradictory central claims that cannot both be held, a thesis that is circular or true by construction of its own measurement so that no possible result could disconfirm it, or a conclusion that directly contradicts the paper's own reported evidence, leaving no coherent argument to revise.
