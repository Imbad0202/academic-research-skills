## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** This dimension asks whether the study was built and executed in a way that a competent peer reviewer in the field would accept: is the design appropriate to the question, is the sampling and data-handling chain defensible, are statistics reported completely rather than selectively, and could another researcher plausibly re-run the work from what is written. It is owned by the methodology reviewer, not by me, so from the editorial chair I read that reviewer's finding rather than substituting my own verdict; but because it is flagged mandatory, a fatal or blocking judgement there drives the decision rule for the whole panel.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** Here the question is factual fidelity to the field: whether the assertions match what current evidence in the domain supports, whether cited or characterised prior work is described as its authors would recognise it, and whether field-specific terms, instruments, constructs, and reported quantities are used correctly. The domain reviewer owns this call. As chair my role is to ensure their finding is registered and weighted, since a mandatory-tier failure here has the same escalation force as a design failure.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, devil's-advocate role with methodology as secondary):** This concerns the internal logic of the manuscript: does the central thesis hold together without contradicting itself, does the evidence actually bear the weight the authors place on it, and are there inferential leaps — causal language on correlational footing, question-begging, equivocation between constructs — that would collapse the main argument. Ownership sits with the devil's-advocate reviewer, and methodology may also score it. It is mandatory-tier, so I treat any blocking finding as decision-determining.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role):** This asks whether a reader from an adjacent field could enter the paper: are framings and definitions supplied rather than assumed, are implications stated in terms that travel beyond the immediate specialty, and where the paper reaches across disciplinary lines, are those reaching claims actually backed rather than gestured at. The perspective reviewer owns it. It is high but not mandatory, so a block here escalates to major revision without triggering outright rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role — mine):** This is my own dimension and covers craft-level presentation: whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow the argument from framing through method to interpretation, whether the exposition is clear rather than padded or elliptical, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and non-redundant with the text, and whether the submission observes the conventions the venue expects (sectioning, reporting format, referencing, length norms). It is normal priority, so problems here shape revision requests rather than acceptance in principle.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role — mine):** Also mine, and the heavier of my two. I must judge whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue at all — subject scope, expected readership, expected depth — and whether it advances something original and consequential rather than restating what the literature already establishes. Because this dimension is mandatory, a blocking or fatal judgement from me alone is sufficient to drive a major-revision or reject outcome, which imposes a duty to state the fit and contribution reasoning explicitly rather than by assertion.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether sectioning follows a legible arc from problem to method to results to interpretation, whether prose states claims plainly with defined terms, whether every figure and table is self-labelled, readable, and adds information beyond the text, and whether venue conventions for structure, reporting format, and referencing are observed.
what_triggers_block: Presentation is broken to the point of impeding review — results reported without the tables or statistics they refer to, sections missing or so disordered that method cannot be separated from findings, or figures/tables unlabelled, uninterpretable, or contradicting the narrative text.
what_triggers_warn: Presentation is followable but flawed — uneven organisation, redundancy between text and exhibits, undefined terminology on first use, inconsistent reporting formats, or deviations from venue conventions that a copy-editing and restructuring pass would resolve.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, framing, and depth match the configured venue's scope and readership, and whether the manuscript articulates a specific advance — new evidence, new construct relation, new setting, or new synthesis — that is positioned against what the existing literature already establishes.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is not established as advancing on prior work — findings restate what the cited literature already reports, or the novelty claim is asserted without positioning — such that substantial reframing, added evidence, or scope expansion is required before the venue could carry it.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are real but underspecified or marginal — the advance is incremental, its significance for the venue's readership is left implicit, or positioning against prior work is thin enough that the editorial value proposition needs explicit strengthening.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is categorically outside the venue's remit or has no identifiable contribution to offer its readership — wrong field or genre entirely, or content that duplicates already-published work without added value, leaving nothing that revision could bring into scope.
