## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** From an editorial oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's design and execution would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the manuscript's field. That covers how the study was set up, how data were collected and cleaned, whether statistical procedures are reported with enough detail to be understood and checked, and whether the manuscript gives readers what they would need to reproduce or at least audit the work. It is designated mandatory, so a fatal-level defect here can drive the whole submission to rejection regardless of other strengths. The methodology reviewer owns it; I do not score it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This concerns whether the manuscript is factually right about its own subject matter: are the substantive claims consistent with what the field currently knows, is prior literature described faithfully rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and are technical terms, instruments, and reported results used correctly. Errors here are not stylistic quibbles; they mislead readers who take the paper at face value. As a mandatory dimension it carries reject-level exposure. Ownership sits with the domain reviewer, outside my scoring remit.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, devil's-advocate role with methodology eligibility).** The question is whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: do the stated conclusions actually follow from the evidence presented, are there internal contradictions between sections, and are there reasoning defects — overreach from correlation to cause, unsupported generalisation, circularity — serious enough to hollow out the main argument. Coherence is judged against the paper's own claims, not against what a different paper might have argued. Mandatory priority, owned by the devil's-advocate reviewer; not mine to score.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension tests whether a reader from an adjacent discipline can enter the paper without being stranded: whether framing and definitions are explained rather than assumed, whether jargon is unpacked where it matters, and whether any claim the paper makes about relevance beyond its home subfield is actually backed by argument or evidence rather than asserted in a closing paragraph. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a block here escalates the decision without triggering rejection on its own. Owned by the perspective reviewer.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role — mine).** As editor I assess whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow it: sensible section sequencing, a clear line from research question through method and results to interpretation, prose that communicates rather than obscures, tables and figures that are legible, self-describing, and consistent with the text, and conformity to the venue's expectations for length, format, and reporting conventions. This is normal priority, meaning problems here shape revision requests rather than acceptance in principle, but severe disorganisation can still make a paper unreviewable in practice.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role — mine).** This is the editorial gatekeeping judgement: does this manuscript belong in this venue, and does it add something its readership does not already have. I look at whether the topic and treatment match the venue's declared scope and readership, whether the contribution is genuinely novel relative to existing published work rather than a restatement, and whether its significance is proportionate to the space it occupies. Because it is mandatory, a fundamental mismatch or an absent contribution is grounds for rejection rather than revision.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Logical section ordering and navigable structure; a traceable line from research question to method, results, and interpretation; unambiguous prose; figures and tables that are self-describing, correctly labelled, and consistent with the narrative; conformance to the venue's formatting, length, and reporting conventions.
what_triggers_block: Disorganisation severe enough to obstruct evaluation — for example, results or methods that cannot be located or distinguished, conclusions presented without any traceable supporting section, or figures and tables whose content contradicts or cannot be reconciled with the text.
what_triggers_warn: Structure and exposition are followable but flawed — redundant or misplaced passages, chronically unclear sentences, under-labelled or poorly captioned exhibits, or departures from venue formatting and reporting conventions that a revision round could fix.
what_triggers_fatal: NOT_APPLICABLE

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Explicit alignment between the manuscript's topic, framing, and depth and the configured venue's scope and readership; a clearly articulated contribution stated against identified prior work; evidence that the finding is new rather than a restatement; significance proportionate to the manuscript's length and claims.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is stated but not substantiated — no clear positioning against prior work, or a claimed advance that on inspection replicates well-established findings without added value — such that the paper's originality would need to be rebuilt before publication is defensible.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are real but underspecified — the novelty claim is implicit, the readership implications are thin or asserted rather than argued, or the scope is marginal for the venue while remaining plausibly within it.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript falls outside the configured venue's scope altogether, or offers no identifiable contribution to its readership — for example, it presents only routine descriptive material with no advance over existing published work, leaving nothing that revision could supply.
