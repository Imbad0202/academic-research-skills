## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, and statistical reporting would survive scrutiny by a competent referee in the paper's own field, and whether enough procedural detail exists for another team to repeat the work. It is a mandatory dimension, so a fatal finding here triggers rejection under F1 and an ordinary block triggers major revision under F2. I do not score it — the methodology reviewer owns it — but I must weigh its verdict when assembling the panel decision.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This covers whether the substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence in the manuscript's discipline, whether prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and whether technical vocabulary and reported results are factually correct. It is mandatory and outside my scoring remit; my editorial role is to ensure the domain reviewer's finding is registered and propagated correctly through the failure conditions.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the devil's-advocate reviewer, also open to methodology).** The question is whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated evidence actually licenses the stated conclusions, whether inferential leaps are flagged and defended, and whether any reasoning defect is severe enough to hollow out the paper's main claim. Mandatory status means warnings here interact with F3 and blocks with F2. Not mine to score.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This asks whether a reader one field over can follow the framing, whether key definitions are supplied rather than assumed, and whether any claim reaching beyond the home discipline is actually supported rather than asserted. It is high priority, not mandatory, so a block here escalates only through F4 to major revision. I do not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as editor-in-chief).** This is my dimension. It concerns whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can navigate it, whether the exposition is clear at sentence and section level, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission observes the conventions the configured venue expects. Priority is normal, so the highest consequence available from this dimension alone is a minor-revision trigger under F5; it cannot by itself force rejection.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as editor-in-chief).** Also mine. This asks two linked questions: does the manuscript belong in this venue at all, given its scope and readership, and does it advance something original and consequential enough to warrant the space it occupies. Because this dimension is mandatory, a fatal finding here produces rejection under F1 and a block produces major revision under F2 — this is the strongest lever I personally hold, and I should apply it on evidence rather than impression.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether sections follow a logical and signposted sequence, whether abstract/introduction/methods/results/discussion each do their assigned job without redundancy, whether prose is unambiguous at the sentence level, whether every figure and table is self-explanatory, numbered, captioned, referenced in text and worth its space, and whether reference formatting, length, and structural conventions match what the configured venue requires.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken badly enough that a competent reader cannot reliably determine what was done or what was found — for example results presented with no interpretable labelling, missing or unreadable core exhibits, absent essential sections, or prose so unclear that key claims admit multiple incompatible readings.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but has real presentational defects — uneven section balance, undefined shorthand on first use, figures or tables that are legible but under-captioned or duplicative of the text, inconsistent referencing, or departures from venue formatting conventions that a copy-editing pass would need to fix.
### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, method, and framing sit inside the configured venue's declared scope and speak to its actual readership; whether the paper states a specific contribution rather than a generic one; whether that contribution is genuinely new relative to the work the paper itself cites; and whether its significance is proportionate to the space and the claims made, judged on the evidence presented rather than on topical fashion.
what_triggers_block: The stated contribution is materially thinner than claimed, or the fit with the venue is doubtful — for instance a study that replicates well-established findings without adding new leverage, that overstates novelty relative to its own cited literature, or that addresses a readership meaningfully different from the venue's while still being salvageable through reframing and added substance.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but under-argued — the novelty claim is asserted rather than positioned against close prior work, the implications for the venue's readers are left implicit, or scope is marginal at the edges of the venue in a way an explicit framing paragraph could resolve.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript offers no identifiable original contribution at all, or is plainly outside the venue's subject domain, such that no revision short of a different study or a different journal could make it publishable here.
