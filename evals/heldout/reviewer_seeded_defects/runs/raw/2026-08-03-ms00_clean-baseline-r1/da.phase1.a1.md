## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of data would survive an unsympathetic reading by a specialist in the field: whether sampling, measurement, and analysis choices are stated precisely enough to be checked, whether the statistics reported are the ones the design actually licenses, and whether enough procedural detail exists for another team to repeat the work. As an adversarial reader, my posture would be to assume nothing is adequate until it is documented — missing denominators, undefined instruments, unreported response rates, or analyses whose assumptions are never examined are all live faults rather than stylistic omissions. I note that this dimension is not open to my role, so I will read it only as context for the argument I do own.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** Here the challenge is whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence in its field, whether the literature it invokes is characterised as that literature actually reads rather than as the authors would like it to read, and whether field-specific constructs, instruments, and results are named and used correctly. An adversarial reading looks for misattributed findings, constructs used loosely as though they were interchangeable, and confident assertions about "what the research shows" that outrun what the cited sources support. This dimension is likewise outside my dispatch role, so I will not score it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, my owned dimension).** This is the dimension I am accountable for: whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms, whether the evidence actually presented does the work the paper says it does, and whether any reasoning defect is severe enough to undercut the main claim. My adversarial job is to reconstruct the argument as a chain — premises, inferential steps, conclusion — and then attack each link: does a correlational design get written up as if it established a causal or directional relationship; does the abstract or conclusion assert something the results section never demonstrates; are constructs redefined mid-paper so that a weak finding can be reported as support for a strong claim; are alternative explanations for the observed pattern acknowledged and then quietly dropped; does the paper generalise beyond the population, setting, or time frame it sampled; do self-report measures get treated as behavioural facts without argument; are contradictory numbers or statements left standing between sections. I will not credit hedged language in one section as repair for an overclaim in another, and I will treat an unsupported load-bearing inference as more serious than several unsupported peripheral remarks.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** The question is whether a competent reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper without insider knowledge: whether framing and definitions are supplied where needed, and whether any claim that the work matters beyond its home subfield is actually substantiated rather than asserted in a closing paragraph. An adversarial reading treats gestures toward broad significance as claims requiring evidence. Not my dimension to score.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This concerns organisation, expository clarity, the quality and self-sufficiency of tables and figures, and conformity to the target venue's conventions. Adversarially, the test is not whether prose is pleasant but whether structure ever hides a gap — sections that promise content they do not deliver, tables whose numbers cannot be reconciled with the text, or missing standard components. Not my dimension to score.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something genuinely new and consequential for that readership, as opposed to restating an established result in a new local setting without added insight. An adversarial reading asks what the field would lose if this paper did not exist, and whether scale and depth are commensurate with the venue's bar. Not my dimension to score.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A reconstructable chain from research question through stated evidence to conclusion, with the strength of every conclusion (associational vs. causal vs. predictive), the population it is applied to, and the construct each measure stands for all matching what the reported evidence can bear, and with abstract, results, discussion, and conclusion mutually consistent on both numbers and claim strength.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing inference for the central thesis is unsupported or invalid — for example the headline conclusion asserts a causal, directional, or effect-producing relationship that the presented evidence cannot establish, the conclusion generalises to a population or setting the evidence never covers, or a key construct is silently substituted for a different one so that the finding appears to support the thesis.
what_triggers_warn: The central thesis survives scrutiny but the argument has repairable defects — peripheral overclaims, an inconsistency confined to non-central statements or figures, an obvious rival explanation for the observed pattern left unaddressed, or hedging that is present in one section and dropped in another without changing what the paper mainly concludes.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's argument fails irrecoverably as an argument — the stated conclusion contradicts the evidence the paper itself reports, the thesis is circular or unfalsifiable as formulated so no evidence could bear on it, or the results presented are not connected to the central claim at all, such that no revision short of a different study or a different thesis could rescue it.
