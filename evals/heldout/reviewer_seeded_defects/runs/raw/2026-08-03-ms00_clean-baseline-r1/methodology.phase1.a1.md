## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by methodology).** From a methods-rigor standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's design actually licenses the inferences drawn from it: whether the sampling frame, recruitment, and unit of analysis are specified; whether measurement instruments are described well enough to be evaluated and reused; whether data handling (exclusions, missingness, cleaning decisions) is transparent and pre-specified rather than reconstructed after the fact; whether the statistical reporting includes the quantities a reviewer needs to judge precision and uncertainty rather than only point estimates or significance verdicts; and whether the manuscript provides enough reproducibility affordances (instrument text, analysis specification, data or code availability, or a clear statement of why not) that an independent reader could in principle repeat the work. The bar is the field's ordinary peer-review standard, not perfection: the question is whether a competent reader can tell what was done and whether what was done supports what is claimed.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by domain).** This dimension concerns substantive correctness within the paper's own field: whether the empirical and theoretical claims are consistent with the current state of evidence, whether cited prior work is characterised faithfully rather than flattened into a convenient foil, and whether field-specific terminology, constructs, and reported results are used and stated correctly. It is owned by the domain reviewer, and I do not score it; my only legitimate interaction with it is that methodological findings may be relevant evidence for the domain reviewer's own judgement.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis role, with methodology eligible).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together internally and whether the evidence presented is of the right kind and strength to carry the claims made. From the methodology seat, my contribution is the inferential-warrant half of coherence: whether the conclusions stay within what the design can support, whether causal, directional, or generalising language is licensed by the data structure, whether constructs invoked in the argument are the same constructs actually measured, and whether any argumentative step depends on a fallacy or an unstated assumption that the methods cannot underwrite. Because the DA reviewer owns this dimension, my score is a co-signed methodological reading of coherence, not a rhetorical or stylistic verdict.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by perspective).** This dimension evaluates whether the framing, definitions, and stated implications are intelligible and useful to readers in adjacent fields, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually substantiated rather than asserted. It is owned by the perspective reviewer; it is not in my eligible set and I will not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension covers manuscript craft: organisation and signposting, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's conventions for reporting and formatting. It belongs to the EiC role and lies outside my scoring mandate.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether its contribution is original and significant enough for that readership. It is an editorial judgement owned by the EiC; I do not score it, though the rigor findings I report may bear on how much contribution the work can credibly claim.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit sampling frame and recruitment procedure, response and completion rates, sample size with any justification, instrument provenance and full item wording or a citable source, reliability and validity evidence for each measured construct, handling of missing data and exclusions, the exact analytic model with assumptions checked, effect sizes with confidence intervals alongside any test statistics, and reproducibility affordances such as available instrument, data, code, or an explicit availability statement plus ethics/consent reporting.
what_triggers_block: A core reported result cannot be evaluated or reproduced because a load-bearing methodological element is absent or internally inconsistent — for example undisclosed sample size or analytic sample definition, an unspecified or mismatched statistical model, numbers that do not reconcile across text and tables, or inferential statistics reported with no measure of uncertainty or effect magnitude.
what_triggers_warn: Methods are recoverable and the analysis is defensible, but specific rigor affordances are thin or missing in ways a revision could fix — for instance partially described instrument items, absent reliability coefficients, unreported missing-data handling, no confidence intervals though effect sizes are given, unstated ethics approval, or no data/code availability statement.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported design or data could not have produced the reported results at all, or that the analysis is invalid beyond repair by revision — such as fabricated, impossible, or mutually contradictory data, an analysis applied to a design that structurally cannot yield the reported quantities, or undisclosed selective reporting that changes the study's conclusions.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated research questions, the measured variables, the analyses run, and the conclusions drawn form a single closed loop; whether the strength and direction of claim language match what the design can license; whether the constructs named in the argument are the constructs actually operationalised; whether generalisation is bounded to the sampled population and setting; and whether limitations are used to qualify the claims rather than as a disclaimer that the conclusions then ignore.
what_triggers_block: The central conclusion asserts something the design cannot support — for example a causal or temporal-ordering claim drawn from data that cannot establish it, generalisation well beyond the sampled population presented as established, or a headline claim about a construct that was never measured — such that the thesis as written is not underwritten by the reported evidence.
what_triggers_warn: The main argument holds, but individual inferential steps overreach or are loosely joined — hedged findings restated more strongly in the abstract or discussion, an implication asserted without a stated warrant, a limitation acknowledged and then not carried through into the claim, or an unexamined alternative explanation that a revision could address.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's core thesis is self-contradictory or rests on a circularity that no revision short of a different study could repair — for instance a conclusion that directly contradicts the paper's own reported results, or an argument whose premise is the conclusion it purports to establish.
