## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor.** This dimension asks whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, analysis, and reporting would survive scrutiny from a competent methodologist in the field, and whether enough procedural detail exists that another researcher could in principle repeat the work. As an adversarial reader I would treat this as a demand that the manuscript prove its inferential machinery rather than assert it: an unexplained sampling frame, an undisclosed analytic procedure, or statistics reported without the quantities needed to interpret them are all failures, not stylistic omissions. It is a mandatory dimension, so a severe defect here is fatal to the paper regardless of how interesting the topic is. I am not the owner of this dimension and will not score it, but I note that argumentative claims resting on a broken method are also argumentative failures.

**D2 — domain_accuracy.** This dimension demands that every substantive claim about the field be consistent with what the field currently knows, that cited or paraphrased prior work be represented as its authors actually stated it, and that technical vocabulary and reported results be factually correct. The adversarial reading is that misdescribing a prior finding, or using a term of art in a non-standard sense without flagging it, is a defect even when it is convenient for the paper's narrative. It is mandatory, so a serious misrepresentation of the evidence base is disqualifying. I do not score this dimension, though I would flag any place where an argument I am evaluating depends on a domain claim that looks contested.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence.** This is my dimension. It asks whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated conclusion is the conclusion the presented evidence actually licenses, whether intermediate steps follow from what precedes them, whether definitions stay stable across the manuscript, and whether any reasoning defect is large enough to undermine the core claim rather than merely blemish a side remark. Adversarially, I am not looking for whether the thesis is plausible or agreeable; I am looking for the shortest path by which a hostile reader could show that the paper's own text does not get from its premises to its conclusion. Overreach from a limited evidentiary base to a general prescription, circular support in which the conclusion is smuggled into a definition or an instrument, unacknowledged alternative explanations that fit the same evidence, and internal contradictions between sections are the canonical failures. Because the dimension is mandatory, a fallacy at the load-bearing centre of the argument is not a revision request but a rejection trigger.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance.** This dimension asks whether a competent reader from a neighbouring field can follow the framing, understand the definitions without insider knowledge, and see why the implications matter beyond the paper's home subfield, and whether any claim that reaches into another discipline is actually backed rather than gestured at. The adversarial version is that borrowed authority from an adjacent field counts against the paper unless the borrowing is substantiated. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a block here forces major revision but is not by itself fatal. I do not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure.** This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and legibility of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's conventions. Read adversarially it means prose that obscures rather than conveys, sections that do not do the work their headings promise, or exhibits that cannot be read independently are genuine defects, even though the dimension carries normal priority and cannot on its own drive anything worse than minor revision. I do not score it.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution.** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue at all and whether it adds something original and consequential for that readership rather than restating settled material or reporting an increment too small to matter. Adversarially, "interesting to the author's institution" is not the same as "a contribution to the venue's literature," and the paper carries the burden of showing the difference. It is mandatory, so a paper that is out of scope or that contributes nothing new is disqualified on that ground alone. I do not score it.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated central thesis is the one the presented evidence can support — tracing each load-bearing claim back to its stated warrant, checking that key constructs keep one stable definition throughout, that intermediate inferences follow from what precedes them, that conclusions and recommendations do not exceed the scope, population, or design the paper itself describes, that rival explanations of the same evidence are acknowledged and addressed rather than ignored, and that no section contradicts another.
what_triggers_block: A reasoning defect that the central thesis depends on but that the paper could in principle repair without new data — for example, a conclusion generalised well beyond the described evidence base, a load-bearing causal claim asserted from purely associational or self-reported material, a plausible alternative explanation that is never confronted, or a direct contradiction between two sections that both bear on the main claim.
what_triggers_warn: Localised reasoning weaknesses that leave the main argument standing — for example, an unhedged claim in a discussion or implications passage that outruns the supporting section, a term used in two slightly different senses in different places, a supporting sub-argument that is under-warranted while the primary chain remains intact, or a rhetorical inference offered where an evidential one is expected.
what_triggers_fatal: The core argument is irreparably circular or vacuous as presented — the conclusion is presupposed by the definition, framing, or instrument used to establish it, or the thesis is stated in a form no presented or obtainable evidence could bear on, such that no revision short of a different study and a different claim could rescue it.
