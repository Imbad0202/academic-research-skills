## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of evidence would survive scrutiny by a competent specialist in the field. That means the sampling or case-selection logic is stated and defensible, the data collection and analysis procedures are described in enough detail that another researcher could follow them, any statistical or coding claims are reported with the accompanying uncertainty and assumptions, and the manuscript offers whatever reproducibility affordances the field expects (instruments, protocols, coding schemes, availability statements). It is a mandatory dimension, so a fatal defect here ends the manuscript's candidacy regardless of how attractive the topic is. I do not score it; I read the methodology reviewer's finding and let it drive the failure-condition arithmetic.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension governs whether the manuscript's substantive claims are true to the current state of knowledge in its field. It covers correct use of field-specific terminology, faithful representation of the prior literature it cites or leans on, and absence of factual errors in reported results or in characterisations of other people's findings. Misattribution, straw-man renderings of existing scholarship, or confident assertions that contradict settled evidence all live here. It is mandatory, and it is not mine to score; the domain reviewer owns it and I inherit the verdict.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, with the methodology reviewer also eligible).** The question is whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the evidence actually presented supports the conclusions actually drawn, whether the chain from premises to claims is free of gaps and equivocations, and whether there are logical fallacies or unacknowledged leaps large enough to undermine the argument rather than merely blemish it. A paper can be methodologically clean and still fail here if the interpretation overshoots the data. Mandatory priority, and outside my scoring remit.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension tests whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper without insider tutoring: whether framing and definitions are made explicit rather than presumed, whether jargon is glossed, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually substantiated rather than gestured at. High priority means a blocking finding here escalates the decision even though the dimension is not mandatory. The perspective reviewer scores it; I do not.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as EiC).** This is one of my two scoring responsibilities. It concerns craft rather than content: whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can navigate it, whether the exposition is clear at sentence and paragraph level, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, self-explanatory, and genuinely referenced in the text, and whether the manuscript honours the venue's conventions for sectioning, referencing, abstract, and length. Normal priority means problems here shape revision requests rather than existentially threaten the paper, but a warn or block still pulls the decision away from outright acceptance under F5.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as EiC).** My second scoring responsibility, and the mandatory one. Two joined questions: does this manuscript belong in this venue and before this readership, and does it add something the readership does not already have? Fit covers topic, scope, method tradition, and register relative to the venue's remit. Contribution covers originality relative to existing published work and significance — whether the increment is large enough to justify the space it occupies. Because the dimension is mandatory, my judgement here can trigger reject (fatal), major revision (block), or feed the multi-dimension warn threshold, so I must ground it in what the manuscript actually demonstrates rather than in the promise of its title.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A stated and followed organisational logic (clear sections with a purpose each), prose that a first-time reader can parse without rereading, figures and tables that are legible, labelled, captioned, self-contained and cited in the text, consistent and complete referencing, and compliance with venue conventions for abstract, sectioning, terminology use, and length.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition so deficient that the manuscript cannot be read as a scholarly article without restructuring — for example missing or unusable core sections, figures/tables referenced but absent or uninterpretable, systematic reference breakdown, or prose whose meaning is indeterminate at points that carry the argument.
what_triggers_warn: Locally fixable craft problems that survive a full read — uneven section proportions, buried or hedged thesis statement, undefined terms on first use, thin or inconsistently formatted captions and labels, repetition across sections, or venue-convention deviations correctable by copyediting and modest reorganisation.
### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of what is new and for whom, positioned against identifiable prior work; a topic, scope, method tradition, and register that match the configured venue's remit and readership; and a contribution whose demonstrated increment (not merely asserted increment) is proportionate to the space and prominence requested.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is real but presently under-demonstrated or under-positioned for this venue — the novelty claim is asserted without engagement with the closest existing work, the scope is materially broader or narrower than the venue serves, or the stated implications outrun what the manuscript actually delivers, such that substantive rewriting and repositioning are required before publication can be contemplated.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but imperfectly argued — the novelty framing is vague or oversold in the abstract relative to the body, the target readership is left implicit, the significance argument leans on generic appeals rather than concrete uptake, or the positioning against neighbouring literature is present but shallow.
what_triggers_fatal: There is no identifiable contribution to this venue's readership at all — the manuscript restates already-established knowledge, falls plainly outside the venue's remit with no reconcilable reading, or its claimed contribution collapses because the work it purports to advance is absent, making no revision short of a different paper capable of rescuing it.
