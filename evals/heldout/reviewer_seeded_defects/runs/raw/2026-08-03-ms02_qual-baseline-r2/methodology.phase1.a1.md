## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** From a methods standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study was actually designed to answer the question it poses, and whether a competent reader could verify or repeat the work. That means the research design is named and justified, the sampling or recruitment logic is explicit and matched to the inferential claims, data collection instruments and procedures are described in enough detail to be reconstructed, and any analytic strategy — statistical, qualitative-coding, or otherwise — is reported with the accompanying uncertainty, reliability, or trustworthiness evidence that the field expects. Reproducibility affordances (instrument availability, coding frames, data or transcript access conditions, ethics and consent handling) count as part of the rigor bar rather than as optional extras. My job is to judge the reported procedure against the field's peer-review standard, not against an ideal study, but silence on a load-bearing procedural element is itself a finding.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain role).** This dimension governs whether substantive assertions about the field's evidence base are correct: whether cited prior work is characterised faithfully, whether technical vocabulary is used in its accepted sense, and whether reported results of others' studies are accurately transmitted. It is a factual-fidelity check on domain content rather than a check on how the present study was executed. I am not eligible to score it and will not do so; where a methodological weakness happens to touch a substantive claim, I confine my own scoring to the procedural side and leave the evidential-accuracy verdict to the owning reviewer.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis role, methodology eligible).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together and whether the evidence presented actually licenses the conclusions drawn. My eligible contribution is the inferential-warrant angle: whether the strength of the stated conclusions is proportionate to what the design and data can support, whether causal or generalising language exceeds what a descriptive or non-inferential design permits, whether stated limitations are reconciled with the claims made elsewhere, and whether the argument avoids internal contradictions between what was measured and what is asserted. I score the fit between evidence and claim, not rhetorical polish.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective role).** This concerns whether readers from adjacent fields can enter the paper: are constructs defined rather than assumed, is jargon unpacked, and are claims that reach beyond the home discipline actually backed rather than asserted by analogy. It is an accessibility-and-substantiation judgement about framing. I am not eligible to score it and will not assign it a score; any methodological observation that also bears on transferability will be reported under my own dimensions only.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension covers organisation, expositional clarity, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's formatting and reporting conventions. It is a presentation judgement distinct from whether the underlying procedure was sound. I am not eligible to score it; if a table omits information I need for a rigor judgement, that deficiency surfaces in my D1 reasoning rather than as a D5 score from me.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and consequential for that readership. Judging novelty and audience fit is an editorial function that sits outside my dispatch role, so I will not score it. My reports may note whether the evidence base is strong enough to support whatever contribution is claimed, but the fit-and-significance verdict rests with the owning reviewer.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: An explicitly named and justified design; stated population, sampling/recruitment procedure, response or participation accounting, and unit of analysis; described instruments and data-collection protocol with enough operational detail to reconstruct; a specified analysis procedure with the field-appropriate uncertainty or trustworthiness evidence (effect sizes and intervals, or coder agreement, saturation logic, audit trail); ethics, consent, and confidentiality handling; and stated availability of instruments, codebooks, or data enabling independent scrutiny.
what_triggers_block: One or more load-bearing procedural elements that the conclusions depend on is absent or unrecoverable from the text — for example no described sampling frame or participant selection logic, no described analysis procedure, statistics reported without denominators or dispersion, or qualitative claims with no account of how data were coded or by whom — such that a reader cannot evaluate whether the reported results follow from the stated method.
what_triggers_warn: The method is recoverable in outline and the core procedure is sound, but specific reporting gaps or design limitations weaken verification — such as thin instrument description, missing reliability or agreement figures, unreported missing-data or non-response handling, unspecified ethics approval, or absent reproducibility affordances — all of which are fixable by adding detail rather than by new data collection.
what_triggers_fatal: The reported methodology is internally incoherent or invalidating rather than merely underspecified — the described procedure could not have produced the reported results, the analysis is applied to data that cannot support it, sample and reported figures contradict each other, or human-subjects data appear to have been handled without any consent or approval basis — so that no amount of added description could rehabilitate the reported evidence.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether conclusion strength is calibrated to design capacity — descriptive or single-site evidence framed descriptively rather than causally or universally; consistency between the limitations section and the claims made in abstract, discussion, and implications; explicit reasoning connecting each headline claim to specific reported evidence; and absence of contradictions between what was measured and what is asserted about mechanism, effect, or transferability.
what_triggers_block: A central claim the paper is built on is not warranted by the evidence presented — causal, comparative, or generalised conclusions drawn from a design incapable of supporting them, or a headline finding with no traceable evidentiary basis in the reported data — so that the main argument would have to be restated to match the study.
what_triggers_warn: The thesis stands and its principal support is traceable, but localised overreach or inconsistency weakens it — overstated wording in abstract or implications relative to the results, limitations acknowledged yet not carried through into the claims, or a secondary inference asserted without shown reasoning — remediable by hedging and by making the inferential chain explicit.
what_triggers_fatal: The argument is self-defeating: the manuscript's own reported evidence contradicts its central thesis, or two mutually exclusive core claims are advanced and relied upon, leaving no coherent position that revision of wording could recover.
