## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive an unsympathetic reading by a competent peer reviewer in the field. Adversarially, I would treat the burden as resting entirely on the manuscript: sampling frame, response rate, instrument provenance, measurement reliability, analytic choices, and the availability of materials sufficient for another team to repeat the work must be stated rather than assumed. Silence about an important design decision is not neutral — it is an unmet obligation. I do not score this dimension, but I note that unstated design choices frequently reappear as unsupported inferential leaps, which is my territory.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** The test is whether every substantive claim is consistent with the current state of evidence in the relevant literature, whether prior work is represented as its authors would recognise it, and whether domain terminology and reported results are factually correct. An adversarial reading looks for constructs used loosely, citations that support a weaker proposition than the one they are attached to, and terminology imported from adjacent traditions without acknowledging that the definitions differ. I do not score this dimension, though misrepresented prior work is also an argumentative defect when the manuscript's thesis depends on it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by me).** My charge is to determine whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence actually presented is capable of supporting the claims made from it, and whether any fallacy or inferential gap undermines the core argument. This is the dimension where I am most hostile: I will trace each headline claim backwards to the specific evidence offered for it, check that the strength of the language matches the strength of the warrant, and look for the classic failure modes — correlational evidence narrated causally, self-report treated as behaviour, a conclusion broader than the population or design supports, conflation of distinct constructs, circular definitions, contradictions between abstract and discussion, unfalsifiable framing, and limitations sections that name a problem and then proceed as if naming it had solved it. Hedged prose does not repair a claim that the design cannot license.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This asks whether framing, definitions, and stated implications are intelligible and useful to readers one field over, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home discipline is actually substantiated rather than asserted. The adversarial version of this test is that borrowing authority from a neighbouring field while omitting its evidentiary standards is a rhetorical move, not a contribution. I do not score this dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** Here the question is organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue conventions. Adversarially, prose problems matter when they conceal reasoning: undefined referents, results introduced in the discussion, figures whose captions do not say what was measured. I do not score this dimension, but I treat structural confusion as a place where argumentative gaps like to hide.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** The manuscript must belong in the configured venue and must offer something original and significant to that readership. An adversarial reading resists the assumption that a competently executed study is thereby a contribution: replication, incremental confirmation, and descriptive reporting each carry their own justification burden, and the manuscript must state what a reader now knows that they did not know before. I do not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A traceable chain from each headline claim (abstract, discussion, conclusion) back to specific reported evidence, with claim strength, causal language, construct definitions, and population scope all matching what the presented design and data can license, and with no internal contradictions between sections.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing claim central to the manuscript's thesis rests on an inference the evidence cannot support — for example causal or effectiveness language derived from purely associational or single-timepoint data, self-report treated as verified behaviour, or a conclusion generalised well beyond the described sample — such that removing the unsupported step would leave the paper's main contribution without a warrant.
what_triggers_warn: Localised inferential overreach, drifting or inconsistently used construct definitions, hedged-then-unhedged restatement of the same claim, or limitations that are acknowledged but not reflected in how conclusions are worded, where the central argument still stands once the affected sentences are qualified.
what_triggers_fatal: The core argument is incoherent as constructed rather than merely overstated — the thesis is circular or unfalsifiable as defined, the stated conclusion contradicts the manuscript's own reported evidence or an earlier section, or the design is structurally incapable of addressing the research question posed, so that no revision short of a different study or a different thesis could repair it.
