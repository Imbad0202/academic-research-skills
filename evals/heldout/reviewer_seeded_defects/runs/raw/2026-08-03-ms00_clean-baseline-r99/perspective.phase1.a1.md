## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility supports would survive scrutiny at the field's normal peer-review threshold. Viewed from my cross-disciplinary seat, the relevance of methodological rigour is that a reader from an adjacent field cannot independently reconstruct what was done unless design and analysis are stated plainly enough to travel outside the authors' own subfield; but the substantive judgement here is not mine and I will not score it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension covers whether the manuscript's claims match current evidence in its home domain, whether prior work is characterised faithfully, and whether domain-specific terms and reported results are factually correct. From a cross-disciplinary standpoint, domain accuracy matters because terminology that is misused internally will also mislead outsiders who take the definitions at face value — yet verifying the domain literature is the domain reviewer's task, and I will not produce a score for it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer).** This dimension concerns whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence actually licenses the stated claims, and whether any reasoning defect undermines the core argument. My interest as a cross-disciplinary reader is adjacent: an argument that only coheres if you already share the authors' assumptions will read as a non-sequitur to an outside reader. Still, the coherence verdict belongs to the DA and methodology roles and is outside my scoring scope.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by me).** This is my dimension. I must judge whether the framing, the operational definitions, and the stated implications are intelligible and usable to readers in adjacent fields rather than only to specialists in the manuscript's immediate niche, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually substantiated instead of merely asserted. Concretely this means checking that constructs are defined before they are used, that jargon and instruments are glossed or cited, that the scope of the population and setting is stated so an outside reader knows to whom the findings could plausibly transfer, and that claims of relevance to other domains rest on evidence presented in the paper rather than on rhetorical gesture. Over-generalisation beyond the design's reach and unexplained borrowing of theory from other fields both count against this dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension covers manuscript organisation, clarity of exposition, quality of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's conventions. Poor structure is one of the most common reasons an adjacent-field reader gives up, so I have an indirect stake here; but presentation quality is the EiC's call and I will not score it.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it offers a contribution that is original and significant enough for that readership. Cross-disciplinary reach can bear on perceived significance, but the fit-and-contribution determination sits with the EiC role and I will not assign it a score.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether key constructs and instruments are explicitly defined and glossed before use, whether the population, setting, and design boundaries are stated clearly enough for an adjacent-field reader to judge transferability, whether borrowed theory or terminology from other disciplines is cited and explained, and whether every claim of relevance beyond the immediate subfield is tied to evidence actually presented in the manuscript.
what_triggers_block: Central interdisciplinary or generalising claims are asserted without supporting evidence in the manuscript, or core constructs are used throughout without any definition or citation, such that an adjacent-field reader cannot determine what was measured or to whom the conclusions are meant to apply.
what_triggers_warn: The work is broadly followable but relevance is weakened by localised gaps — undefined jargon in places, implications stated more broadly than the described design supports, or borrowed cross-field concepts introduced without a citation or a brief gloss — none of which alone destroys an outside reader's ability to interpret the study.
