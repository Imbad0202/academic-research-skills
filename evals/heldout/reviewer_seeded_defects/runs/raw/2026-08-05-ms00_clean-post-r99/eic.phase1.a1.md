## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** As editor I read this dimension as asking whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, and statistical reporting would survive scrutiny from a competent specialist in the field, and whether enough procedural detail is present that another team could repeat the work. It is a gatekeeping dimension: a manuscript whose inferential machinery cannot be audited cannot be published regardless of how appealing its topic is. Ownership sits with the methodology reviewer, so my editorial job is to weight and reconcile that reviewer's verdict rather than substitute my own.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension tests whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence in its field, whether prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than conveniently, and whether field-specific terms, constructs, and reported quantities are used correctly. Misattribution of earlier findings or misuse of an established construct counts here even when the statistics are clean. As editor I treat sustained domain error as a threat to the journal's credibility, not a copy-editing matter.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the devil's-advocate reviewer, also open to methodology).** Here the question is internal: does the central thesis hold together, does the evidence actually license the conclusions drawn from it, and are there reasoning defects — overreach, circularity, causal language on correlational footing, unacknowledged alternative explanations — that would collapse the argument if pressed? A paper can be methodologically tidy and factually accurate yet still fail because the chain from data to claim is broken. This is a mandatory dimension, so a fatal defect here ends the manuscript's path to acceptance.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This asks whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper without insider tutoring: are framing, definitions, and stated implications intelligible and explicitly grounded, and where the manuscript reaches across disciplinary lines, is that reach evidenced rather than asserted? It is high priority but not mandatory, so a serious deficiency drives revision rather than outright rejection under this contract's failure conditions.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as EiC).** This is my own dimension. It covers how the manuscript is organised, whether exposition is clear and proportionate, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative, and whether the submission observes the venue's conventions for structure, length, and apparatus. It is the lowest-stakes dimension in the contract, but it is the one where editorial judgement is primary and cannot be delegated to specialist reviewers.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as EiC).** Also mine, and mandatory. I must decide whether this manuscript belongs in the configured venue at all, and whether it advances something original and consequential for that venue's readership rather than restating settled knowledge or reporting an increment too small to occupy journal space. Because it is mandatory, a fatal judgement here forces rejection, so I have to apply it with discipline: genuine absence of contribution, not mere absence of excitement.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether sections follow a coherent and conventional progression, whether prose is clear and proportionate to the claims, whether every figure and table is captioned, self-explanatory, and referenced in text, whether numbers reported in text match those in exhibits, and whether length, referencing style, and required apparatus conform to venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken badly enough that a qualified reader cannot reliably reconstruct what was done and what was found — for example missing or unlabelled exhibits carrying central results, internally contradictory numbers between text and tables, or absent sections the venue requires.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is comprehensible but has recurring presentational defects — vague or bloated passages, unexplained abbreviations, thin or poorly captioned exhibits, inconsistent citation formatting, or sequencing that forces the reader to re-read to follow the argument.
### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, method, and audience match the configured venue's declared scope; whether the manuscript states a specific claim to novelty and locates it against existing work; whether the reported increment is substantive enough to matter to this readership; and whether the stated implications are ones this venue's readers could act on.
what_triggers_block: Scope or contribution is seriously deficient — the subject falls outside the venue's remit, or the novelty claim is unsupported because the work substantially duplicates readily available prior findings without adding analytical or evidentiary value.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but underdeveloped — the novelty claim is asserted rather than positioned against named prior work, the increment is modest, or the implications are stated too generically to guide this readership.
what_triggers_fatal: There is no identifiable contribution to evaluate for this venue at all — the manuscript neither advances a claim of its own nor reports findings capable of supporting one, so that no revision short of a different study could make it publishable here.
