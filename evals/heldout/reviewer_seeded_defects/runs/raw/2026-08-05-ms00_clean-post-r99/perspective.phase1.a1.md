## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, statistical reporting, and the affordances it offers for reproduction would survive scrutiny by a competent peer reviewer in its home field. From where I sit as the cross-disciplinary reader, methodological rigour matters because a reader from an adjacent field cannot independently audit the numbers; they inherit whatever confidence the reporting earns. I do not score this dimension, but I note that its outcome constrains how much an outside-field reader should be told to trust the results.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension covers whether the claims are consistent with current evidence in the field, whether prior work is characterised faithfully, and whether domain-specific terms and reported results are factually correct. It is adjacent to my own concern in a specific way: outside readers are the least equipped to detect a mischaracterised construct or a misused technical term, so accuracy failures propagate further across disciplinary boundaries than within them. Scoring here belongs to the domain reviewer.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, also open to methodology).** This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together, whether the evidence presented actually supports the claims made of it, and whether any reasoning defects hollow out the main argument. My interest is that incoherence and interdisciplinary inaccessibility are easy to confuse: a chain of reasoning that is merely unstated for insiders reads to an outsider as a gap. I will keep that distinction clean and leave the coherence verdict to its owners.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by me as the perspective reviewer).** This is my dimension. It asks whether the framing, the operative definitions, and the stated implications are usable by a reader trained in a neighbouring discipline rather than only by specialists in the paper's home subfield, and whether any claim the manuscript makes about reaching beyond its own field is actually substantiated rather than asserted. Two distinct failure modes live here: opacity, where the paper is defensible but unreadable outside its niche because constructs, instruments, and abbreviations go undefined or the scope of the finding is never stated in field-neutral terms; and overreach, where the paper exports its conclusions to populations, settings, institutions, or disciplines that its own evidence base does not cover. I read for whether an informed outsider could state, in one sentence and without error, what was measured, in whom, and what the finding does and does not license.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue conventions. It overlaps my concern at the surface but is not the same thing: prose can be tidy and conventionally formatted while still being sealed off from adjacent-field readers, and prose can be rough while remaining perfectly legible across fields. I will not score presentation quality as such.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it makes an original and significant contribution for that readership. It touches my territory because a venue with a broad or mixed readership raises the accessibility bar, and because breadth of relevance is sometimes offered as a substitute for originality. Judging fit and contribution is nevertheless the editor's call, not mine.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether key constructs, instruments, and abbreviations are defined at first use in field-neutral language; whether the population, setting, and unit of analysis are stated explicitly enough for an adjacent-field reader to bound the finding; whether stated implications for other disciplines, institutions, or practice are backed by the paper's own evidence rather than asserted; and whether the design's inherent limits on inference are declared in terms an outsider can act on.
what_triggers_block: Core constructs or the measured outcome are never operationally defined for a non-specialist, or the manuscript exports conclusions to populations, settings, or disciplines its evidence base does not cover, such that an adjacent-field reader would predictably misstate what the study shows.
what_triggers_warn: The paper is broadly followable but leaves specific jargon, instrument names, or scale conventions unglossed, or asserts interdisciplinary implications in a closing passage without tying them to reported results, so an outside reader can reconstruct the meaning only with extra effort.
