## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my dimension):** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its execution can actually bear the weight of the inferences drawn from it. From a methodology standpoint I read this as covering the match between the research question and the chosen design, how the sample was obtained and described, how measures were constructed and validated, how missing and excluded data were handled, whether the statistical procedures reported are appropriate and completely specified (estimates, uncertainty, assumptions, and any correction for multiple comparisons), and whether a reader could re-run the analysis given the instruments, data, and code affordances described. The bar is the ordinary peer-review standard of the field, not perfection: identified limitations that are honestly scoped are acceptable; undisclosed choices that could reverse the reported result are not.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, not my dimension):** This dimension concerns whether substantive claims are consistent with the current evidence base of the field, whether prior literature is represented faithfully rather than selectively, and whether domain-specific constructs, instruments, and reported quantities are named and used correctly. I will not score it. Where a methodological observation touches it — for instance a measurement instrument whose established psychometric interpretation is being stretched — I will keep the observation inside my own dimensions and flag it as adjacent rather than adjudicating the domain question.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, shared with me as a non-owner):** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together and whether the evidence actually presented licenses the claims actually made. My methodological angle on this is narrower than the discourse-analysis owner's: I look for inferential slippage between what the design can support and what the prose asserts — correlational evidence narrated causally, sample-bounded findings generalised to populations never sampled, effect sizes described as substantial without a magnitude referent, or conclusions that quietly rest on an untested auxiliary assumption. Internal contradictions between abstract, results, and discussion also fall here when they concern what the data show.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, not my dimension):** This dimension evaluates whether framing, definitions, and stated implications are intelligible and defensible to readers in adjacent fields, and whether any interdisciplinary claim is backed rather than gestured at. It belongs to the perspective reviewer and I will not assign it a score, even if I notice unexplained jargon while reading for method.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, not my dimension):** This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity with venue formatting conventions. It is the editor-in-chief's to score. My interest in the same artefacts is different in kind: a table matters to me when its contents are the only record of a statistical result, not because of its typography, and I will confine such remarks to D1.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, not my dimension):** This dimension judges whether the manuscript suits the configured venue and offers an original contribution of appropriate significance to that readership. Novelty and fit are outside my dispatch; a methodologically sound but unoriginal study is not mine to penalise, and I will not let perceived low stakes soften or harden my methodological reading.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design statement and its match to the research question; sampling frame, recruitment route, achieved sample size and response/completion rate; construction and psychometric justification of each measure including item wording, response scale, and reliability evidence; treatment of missing data, ineligible responses, and outliers; complete inferential reporting with point estimates, uncertainty intervals or exact test statistics, degrees of freedom, assumption checks, and handling of multiple comparisons; whether self-report constructs and any behavioural measure are distinguished as separate data sources; and reproducibility affordances such as instrument appendix, ethics/consent statement, and data or analysis-script availability.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing analysis cannot be evaluated or reproduced as described — for example the reported inferential results lack the statistics needed to verify them, the sample's provenance is unspecified so no target population can be identified, or an analytic decision such as exclusion or scale construction is asserted without rule and could plausibly reverse the headline finding.
what_triggers_warn: Design and analysis are recoverable and broadly appropriate, but rigor gaps remain that a revision should close, such as reliability evidence reported for only some scales, uncertainty given without assumption checks, missing-data handling mentioned only in passing, no instrument appendix, or a single-source self-report design whose common-method vulnerability is not discussed.
what_triggers_fatal: The reported procedure is internally impossible or invalid at its foundation — for instance the stated analysis cannot be run on the stated design or measurement level, reported numbers are mutually inconsistent to the point that no coherent dataset could produce them, the sample or units described contradict themselves across sections, or human-subjects survey data are reported with no consent or ethics basis at all — such that no revision short of re-collecting or re-analysing the data could repair the inference.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Traceability from each stated conclusion back to a specific reported result; consistency of the claim's strength across abstract, results, and discussion; whether cross-sectional or correlational evidence is described in associational rather than causal language; whether generalisation targets stay inside the sampled population and setting; whether effect magnitudes are interpreted against a stated referent rather than adjectivally; and whether unmeasured auxiliary assumptions on which the argument depends are named as assumptions.
what_triggers_block: The central thesis rests on an inference the presented evidence cannot license — a causal or effectiveness claim drawn from a design that only establishes association, a conclusion about a population or outcome that was never measured, or a headline claim in the abstract that the results section does not actually support.
what_triggers_warn: The main argument is defensible but locally overreaches or wobbles — occasional causal phrasing in an otherwise associational account, effect sizes characterised as strong or meaningful without a comparison benchmark, or a discussion claim that outruns its result yet is elsewhere correctly hedged.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's core argument is self-contradictory in a way no revision preserves — the stated conclusion is directly opposed by the direction or sign of its own reported evidence, or the thesis simultaneously requires two mutually exclusive premises about the data, so that abandoning the contradiction means abandoning the paper's claim.
