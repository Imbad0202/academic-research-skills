## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of data would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in this field. That covers how participants or cases were obtained, whether the analytic procedures match the design, whether statistics are reported completely enough to be checked rather than merely asserted, and whether a reader could in principle reproduce or re-run what was done. It is a mandatory dimension, so a fatal defect here forces rejection rather than revision, and the methodology reviewer owns the call.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** This dimension governs whether the substance of the manuscript is factually right within its own field. The domain reviewer checks that claims are consistent with the current state of evidence, that cited prior work is characterised the way that work actually reads rather than the way it would be convenient to read it, and that field-specific terminology, constructs, and reported results are used correctly. Because it is mandatory, misrepresentation of the literature or a clear factual error in domain content carries escalation weight beyond ordinary criticism.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, discourse-analysis role with methodology as secondary):** Here the question is whether the paper holds together as an argument independently of whether its individual facts are correct. The central thesis must not contradict itself across sections, the evidence actually presented must be the evidence the conclusions rest on, and inferential leaps or fallacies must not be doing load-bearing work. The discourse-analysis reviewer owns this, but a methodology reviewer may also score it, which reflects how often coherence failures and design failures are the same failure seen from two angles.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role):** This dimension tests whether a competent reader from a neighbouring field can enter the paper without being locked out. Framing must situate the work, definitions must be given rather than assumed as insider vocabulary, and implications must be stated at a level that adjacent disciplines can evaluate. Where the manuscript reaches across disciplinary lines and claims relevance elsewhere, that reach must be substantiated rather than asserted. It is high priority but not mandatory, so a block here routes to major revision rather than to rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, editor-in-chief role):** This is my own dimension as editor. It concerns the craft layer: whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can follow the line of development, whether exposition is clear rather than merely present, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and actually informative, and whether the submission observes the conventions the venue expects. It is normal priority, so problems here shape revision requests rather than acceptance in principle, and no fatal escalation path exists for it.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, editor-in-chief role):** Also mine, and the one where editorial judgement is least delegable. I must decide whether this manuscript belongs in this venue at all, and whether what it adds is original and consequential enough to justify the readership's attention. That means weighing the increment over existing work, the significance of that increment for this specific audience, and whether the topic and treatment match the journal's declared scope. Being mandatory, a fatal verdict here ends the manuscript's path rather than sending it back for repair.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Section-level architecture that carries the reader from problem to method to result to interpretation; unambiguous prose; self-contained and correctly captioned figures and tables; consistent notation and terminology; conformity to venue formatting, citation, and reporting conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken enough that a qualified reader cannot reliably determine what was done or what the results are without reconstructing the manuscript, or key figures/tables are unreadable, mislabelled, or contradict the text.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable overall but has localised defects such as an underdeveloped or disproportionate section, unclear passages, inconsistent terminology, thin or poorly captioned exhibits, or departures from venue conventions that copyediting and one revision round can fix.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of what is new relative to prior work, evidence that the increment matters to this venue's readership, topical and methodological alignment with the journal's declared scope, and a contribution whose scale is commensurate with the manuscript's length and claims.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is stated but not substantiated as an advance over existing work, or the fit with the venue is marginal enough that acceptance would require the authors to reframe scope, audience, or claimed novelty rather than simply clarify them.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is genuine and the venue fit is defensible, but novelty is oversold in places, positioning against the nearest prior work is incomplete, or the significance to this readership is asserted rather than argued, all correctable within the present study.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript falls outside the venue's scope altogether, or it offers no identifiable original contribution because it duplicates already-published work, restates established findings without increment, or rests on a contribution claim that the reported work cannot support at any level of revision.
