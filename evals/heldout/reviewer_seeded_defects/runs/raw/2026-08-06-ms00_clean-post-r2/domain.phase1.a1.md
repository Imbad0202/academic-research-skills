## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether constructs, instruments, and technical vocabulary from educational technology and higher education are used with their established meanings; whether cited prior work is characterised as it actually reads and supports the claim attached to it; whether reported quantities, scales, and results are internally consistent and consistent with what the field has found; whether contested or context-dependent findings are presented with appropriate qualification; and whether any body of contrary domain evidence bearing on a central claim is acknowledged.
what_triggers_block: One or more substantive domain errors that a knowledgeable reader could not accept as written and that cannot be repaired by rewording alone, for example a core construct conflated with a distinct one throughout, a central claim attributed to cited literature that does not support it, or domain-specific results that are mutually inconsistent in a way that changes their interpretation.
what_triggers_warn: Domain imprecision or overreach that is correctable by revision without disturbing the paper's substance, for example loose or nonstandard use of a technical term, an overstated generalisation from a bounded finding, a citation that is thematically apt but not the appropriate source for the specific claim, or an unqualified presentation of a finding the field treats as context-dependent.
what_triggers_fatal: Domain misrepresentation so extensive or so central that the manuscript's substantive contribution cannot survive correction, for example a defining construct or measurement model misunderstood at its foundation, systematically fabricated or unrecoverable attributions to prior work, or headline findings that contradict well-established domain evidence with no engagement of that evidence at all.
