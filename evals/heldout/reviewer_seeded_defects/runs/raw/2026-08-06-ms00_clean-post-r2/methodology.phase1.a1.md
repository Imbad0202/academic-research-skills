## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its execution can bear the inferential weight the manuscript places on them. From a methodology standpoint I read this as: is the design named and appropriate to the question; is the sampling frame defined with response and completion rates; are the measures documented (item wording, response scales, provenance of any adapted instrument, reliability evidence); is the analytic path stated before results rather than reconstructed after; are estimates reported with uncertainty rather than as bare point values or bare p-values; and is there enough procedural and data detail that an independent analyst could re-run the same steps and land in the same place. Missing-data handling, exclusions, and any deviation between planned and reported analyses fall here too.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension governs whether the substantive claims are true to the current state of evidence in the field, whether cited prior work is characterised as its authors would recognise, and whether field-specific constructs and terminology are used in their accepted sense. It is not mine to score, but I note that a methodological reading treats definitional drift in a construct as a measurement problem as well as a domain problem, and I will confine my own commentary to the measurement side of any such overlap.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by discourse analysis, eligible to me).** This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together internally and whether the evidence actually presented licenses the conclusions actually drawn. My angle on it is inferential rather than rhetorical: does the strength of the stated conclusion match the strength of the design that produced it; are associational results narrated in causal or directional language; do the abstract, results, and discussion assert the same thing at the same confidence level; and are limitations acknowledged in a way that genuinely constrains the claims rather than being listed and then ignored. Overclaiming that a design cannot support is the paradigm case I am watching for.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, perspective role).** This dimension concerns whether readers from adjacent fields can follow the framing, definitions, and stated implications, and whether any claim reaching beyond the paper's own discipline is actually substantiated. I do not score it. Methodologically, the only adjacent concern is that accessibility should not be achieved by simplifying away the qualifications that the design requires.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, EiC role).** This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and self-sufficiency of tables and figures, and conformity to the venue's conventions. I do not score it. Where a table or figure omits the information needed to interpret an estimate, I treat that as a reporting defect under D1 rather than as a presentation issue, and I will say so explicitly to avoid double-counting.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and consequential for that readership. I do not score it. The methodological adjacency is that a contribution claim rests on the evidential base; I will report on the strength of that base under D1 and D3 and leave the fit and significance judgement to the owning role.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design label and rationale; defined population, sampling frame, recruitment procedure, achieved N with response and completion rates; full instrument documentation including item wording, response scale, source or adaptation history, and reliability or validity evidence; pre-specified analysis plan matching what is reported; effect estimates with confidence intervals or comparable uncertainty alongside any test statistics; stated handling of missing data, exclusions, and non-response; ethics or consent statement; and reproducibility affordances such as available instrument, data, code, or a documented availability rationale.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing methodological component is absent or unverifiable — for example no reported sample size or sampling procedure, an undocumented measure with no wording or reliability evidence, an analysis whose specification cannot be reconstructed from the text, or results reported with no uncertainty at all — such that the reported findings cannot be evaluated or reproduced as written.
what_triggers_warn: The core design and analysis are recoverable and defensible, but specific reporting gaps weaken verification — for instance response rate omitted while N is given, reliability reported without item wording, missing-data handling unstated though the pattern appears minor, or no data or instrument availability statement — and each gap is fixable by adding information the authors plainly already hold.
what_triggers_fatal: The evidentiary chain is broken rather than merely underreported — reported numbers are mutually inconsistent or arithmetically impossible, the described procedure could not have produced the reported data, the analysis is invalid for the data structure in a way that voids every substantive result, or human-subjects data are used with no ethical basis — so that no revision short of collecting or re-analysing the data could rescue the conclusions.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated conclusions are calibrated to what a cross-sectional self-report design can support; consistency of the claim across title, abstract, results, and discussion; associational findings described in associational language; alternative explanations such as common-method variance, reverse causation, and selection considered rather than dismissed; limitations that actually restrict the claims that follow them; and no reliance on a null or non-significant result as positive evidence.
what_triggers_block: The central thesis rests on an inference the design cannot yield — causal or predictive claims drawn from cross-sectional associational data, or a headline conclusion that the reported analyses do not test — and this overreach is carried into the abstract or conclusion rather than confined to a single incidental sentence.
what_triggers_warn: The overall argument holds but is locally miscalibrated — scattered causal or directional verbs where association is meant, a discussion that mentions a limitation and then argues past it, or a modest asymmetry in confidence between abstract and results — correctable by rewording without altering the substantive finding.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript asserts conclusions that contradict its own reported evidence, or the thesis is internally self-cancelling — two load-bearing claims cannot both be true — so the argument cannot be repaired by rewording or by softening the claim and no coherent contribution survives.
