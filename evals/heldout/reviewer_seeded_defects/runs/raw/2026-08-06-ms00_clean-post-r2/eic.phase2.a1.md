## Review Body

I read this manuscript first for the question I always ask on page two: what does a mixed researcher–practitioner readership do with it? The honest answer is that the paper contains one usable number, r = .42 between perceived usefulness and self-reported LMS use in a single undergraduate sample, reported with a confidence interval, a robustness check, and unusually well-calibrated language about what it does not show. That is a real asset and I want to say so plainly before I say anything else, because the manuscript's restraint is the best thing about it and revision should not sand it off.

But restraint is not the same as contribution, and my editorial job is to keep those two apart. The manuscript's own contribution claim is that it supplies "an incremental data point, comparable with prior work." Comparable to what? Section 2 cites Song (2018) explicitly as a multi-campus study whose association strengths varied by institution, which is precisely the anchor a reader needs, and then never reports a single number from it, or from any other cited source. Without any prior estimate on the page, the reader cannot tell whether .42 sits at the centre, the top, or the bottom of what the field already believes. The paper's one asserted reason for existing is therefore stated rather than demonstrated, and that is what moves D6 to block rather than warn: the fix is not a clarifying sentence but a rebuild of what Section 2 does. Coupled with a seven-section full-article architecture carrying a single bivariate result, acceptance in the present form would require reframing the article's class and its claimed weight, not merely tightening its prose. I would put the reclassification option to the authors directly — as a Short Paper or Research Note with a genuine comparison table, this becomes a clean, citable, replication-grade contribution rather than a thin full article.

Two further matters. First, on D5 I am at warn, not block: the exposition is clear, the sections do what their headings say, and a competent reader never has to reconstruct the study to know what was done. The defects are proportion and exhibits, both fixable in one round. Second, and separately from the accept/revise calculus, the reference list needs verification before this manuscript proceeds at all. All six entries carry DOIs under a reserved test prefix with perfectly sequential registrant numbers, and the journal titles read as near-variants of well-known outlets. I could not resolve the identifiers as part of this review and I am not asserting fabrication as established fact; I am asserting that the on-page evidence is sufficient to require an integrity check by the editorial office. I have deliberately not bound this to a fatal classification on D6, because my declared fatality criterion for that dimension concerns scope and duplication, not source verification. The correct route is a verification step running alongside, and logically prior to, the ordinary decision path — if the sources do not resolve, no amount of revision to Sections 2 through 7 is relevant.

I note two blind spots in my own reading, so the panel weights my scores accordingly: I did not audit the statistics, and I did not evaluate the measurement properties of the single-item use measure or the sample's composition. Those belong to the methodology and perspective seats, and my D5/D6 scores should not be read as clearing them.

### S1: Claim language is calibrated to the design throughout
The abstract, discussion, and conclusion all decline the causal reading that this literature routinely invites, and the reverse-pathway argument is stated in the authors' own voice rather than buried in limitations. For a practitioner readership that regularly over-reads correlational LMS work, this is a model of proportionate reporting.
**Evidence Anchor**: text: Abstract "should not be read as causal, given the cross-sectional design"

### S2: Limitations anticipate the objections rather than deflect them
Section 6 names the single-site ceiling, the self-report-versus-log divergence, the cross-sectional constraint, and volunteer bias without hedging any of them into meaninglessness. Reviewers usually have to supply this list; here the authors supplied it themselves.
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §6 "students who engage more with institutional channels may be overrepresented"

### S3: Topical fit with the venue is unambiguous
Institutional LMS adoption among undergraduates, framed around onboarding practice, is squarely inside this journal's declared scope and its readership's working concerns. Nothing in the framing requires the audience to travel to reach the paper.
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §1 "Learning management systems have become a standard part of the undergraduate experience"

### S4: Reporting conventions for the headline estimate are observed
The single inferential result arrives with an interval, an n, a p value, and a non-parametric robustness check in the same paragraph, which is what allows a later synthesist to reuse the estimate at all. This is the specific property that makes the reclassification path viable rather than a demotion.
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §4 "The Spearman robustness check yielded a comparable coefficient"

### W1: Reference list carries markers of non-resolving sources and requires editorial integrity verification
All six references use DOIs under the 10.5555 prefix, which is reserved for testing and examples rather than for registered scholarly content, and the six suffixes run in an unbroken sequence (2050001 through 2050006). The journal titles are near-miss variants of established outlets. If these sources do not resolve, then the literature review, the comparability claim, the methodological cautions in Section 2, and the provenance of the adapted perceived-usefulness instrument are all unsupported, and no revision to the body text repairs that. Uncorrected, this alone makes acceptance impossible regardless of the paper's other merits, which is why it stands as Critical on its own rather than as an aggravating factor on the contribution finding. I could not resolve the identifiers within this review; my claim is that the on-page pattern obliges the editorial office to verify before any further processing.
**Severity**: Critical
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §References "https://doi.org/10.5555/2050001", "https://doi.org/10.5555/2050006"
**Confidence**: 4 — routine editorial handling of DOI provenance and reference verification, discounted because live resolution was outside this review.

### W2: The comparability claim that carries the whole contribution is never anchored to a prior estimate
Section 2 positions the study as an incremental data point "comparable with prior work," and Sections 5 and 7 repeat that the result is "consistent with prior technology-acceptance research." No prior coefficient, range, or pooled estimate appears anywhere in the manuscript, including from Song (2018), which is cited precisely for reporting cross-institution variation. Incrementality is therefore asserted, not shown, and the reader cannot judge whether .42 confirms, extends, or sits oddly against the existing answer. The repair is substantial but bounded: report the actual estimates from the cited studies, state the range, and locate .42 within it. The estimate itself survives, which is why this is Major rather than Critical; its joint effect with W3 on the dimension score is registered at D6, not by inflating this finding's band.
**Severity**: Major
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §2 "It is intended as an incremental data point, comparable with prior work, rather than as a test of a theoretical model."
**Confidence**: 5 — direct editorial assessment of positioning claims against manuscript content.

### W3: Full research-article architecture is disproportionate to a single bivariate result
The manuscript runs seven sections with separate Discussion, Limitations, and Conclusion sections, and Section 4 delivers two descriptive statistics plus one correlation with its robustness check. There are no subgroup contrasts, no covariates, no secondary outcomes. The structural overhead signals a scale of contribution the results cannot fill, which makes a genuinely disciplined study read as thin. This is a format-class mismatch rather than a defect in the work: as a Short Paper or Research Note the same content reads as appropriately scoped, and the reclassification is the cheapest available repair. It requires substantial restructuring rather than clarification, hence Major.
**Severity**: Major
**Evidence Anchor**: absence: §4 Results — expected reported analyses beyond the single bivariate coefficient commensurate with full research-article format; checked §3.4 Analysis, §4 Results, §5 Discussion, §7 Conclusion
**Confidence**: 5 — sustained editorial experience triaging article-class fit on submission.

### W4: No tables or figures, including a scatterplot the text says was inspected
Section 3.4 reports that scatterplot inspection established approximate linearity and the absence of extreme bivariate outliers, and Section 4 reports a mean, an SD, and a median category in running prose. The reader is asked to accept a visual diagnostic never shown and to hold the descriptives in memory. One small descriptives table and the scatterplot would let a practitioner reader see the spread rather than take it on trust, and would cost half a page. Quality and verifiability improve without any core claim changing.
**Severity**: Minor
**Evidence Anchor**: absence: whole manuscript — expected at least one table or figure presenting the descriptives and the inspected scatterplot; checked abstract, §3 Methods, §4 Results, §5 Discussion
**Confidence**: 5 — direct inspection of the submitted exhibits.

### W5: Quantities available to the authors are reported as adjectives
Section 4 describes the shared variance as "accordingly modest" instead of giving the value, and Section 3.4 refers to "a conventional significance threshold" where the alpha level is already stated a sentence earlier. For a readership that will want to place this estimate beside others, replacing numbers with qualitative descriptors adds a small but unnecessary friction. Substituting the figures is a copyediting-scale fix.
**Severity**: Minor
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §4 "The proportion of variance shared by the two measures was accordingly modest"
**Confidence**: 4 — editorial reading of reporting clarity, with statistical adequacy left to the methodology seat.

### W6: The paper's actual selling point is never claimed as a contribution
The manuscript's distinctive value to this readership is that it supplies a transparently reported, design-bounded, non-inflated single-site estimate of a kind that later syntheses and replications can reuse. That case is nowhere made. The closest the text comes is a passive framing in the conclusion which describes what the finding is not, rather than what it is for. Stating the contribution affirmatively — as a replication-grade input with full reporting, offered against a named prior range — would convert the manuscript's honesty from an apologetic register into a reason to publish. This changes framing, not findings.
**Severity**: Minor
**Evidence Anchor**: text: §7 "is offered as an incremental, design-bounded contribution rather than a causal claim"
**Confidence**: 4 — editorial judgement of readership value framing.
