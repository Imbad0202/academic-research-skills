## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and execution would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the manuscript's field: whether the sampling and data-collection strategy support the inferences drawn, whether data handling (cleaning, exclusions, missingness) is disclosed rather than assumed, whether statistical reporting includes the quantities a reader needs to judge the result rather than only those that flatter it, and whether enough procedural detail exists that another team could attempt a replication. As editorial oversight I do not score this dimension, but I note that it is mandatory, so a fatal finding here forces rejection and a block forces major revision regardless of how the manuscript reads.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** Here the question is factual correctness within the substantive literature: are the claims consistent with what is currently known, is prior work characterised as its authors would recognise, and are field-specific terms, instruments, and reported results used correctly rather than loosely. Misattribution of a prior finding, an inverted result, or a misused technical term all belong to this dimension rather than to writing quality. It is mandatory and outside my scoring remit.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the devil's-advocate reviewer, with methodology also eligible).** This concerns the logical spine of the paper: whether the central thesis stays consistent from abstract through conclusion, whether the evidence actually offered supports the strength of the claims made, and whether any inferential leap — conflating association with causation, generalising past the sampled population, or arguing from an assumption smuggled in as a premise — undermines the argument the paper is built on. Coherence failures are distinct from prose problems; a beautifully written paper can fail this dimension outright. Mandatory, and not mine to score.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension tests whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper: whether framing and definitions are supplied rather than presumed, whether jargon is glossed at first use, and, critically, whether claims that reach across disciplinary boundaries are actually substantiated rather than asserted as rhetorical scope-widening. It is high priority but not mandatory, so a block here triggers major revision without being fatal. It is outside my scoring remit.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as EiC).** This is my dimension for the manuscript as a made object: whether the organisation serves the argument, whether the exposition is clear enough that a reader does not have to reconstruct the authors' meaning, whether figures and tables are legible, self-describing, and actually referenced in the text, and whether the manuscript observes the conventions of the configured venue — section structure, abstract form, citation practice, length expectations. It carries normal priority, so failings here escalate the decision but cannot on their own be fatal.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as EiC).** This is the editorial gatekeeping judgement proper: does this manuscript belong in this venue and before this readership, and does it add something the readership does not already have. Fit concerns topic, method-type, and audience alignment with the venue's scope; contribution concerns originality relative to the existing record and significance relative to what the readership already accepts. A paper can be methodologically clean, factually accurate, and well written and still fail here by being a competent restatement of settled knowledge or by being addressed to a different community. Because this dimension is mandatory, a fatal finding here forces rejection and a block forces major revision.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether section order and internal signposting track the argument, whether claims are stated in prose a non-specialist editor can follow without reconstruction, whether every figure and table is numbered, captioned self-sufficiently, referenced in the text, and readable without the caption doing the analysis, and whether abstract, structure, citation format, and length match the configured venue's conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken badly enough that the manuscript's own claims cannot be reliably identified or traced to their supporting material — for example results reported nowhere in the text, figures or tables absent, unnumbered, or never referenced, missing or contentless method/results sections, or a length so far off venue norms that required components are simply not present.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but flawed in ways a copy-editing or restructuring pass must fix — redundant or misordered sections, undefined terms at first use, figures with unreadable labels or captions that do not stand alone, inconsistent citation formatting, numbers that disagree between text and table, or venue-convention deviations that do not remove required content.
what_triggers_fatal: NOT_APPLICABLE — omitted per contract; D5 is non-mandatory.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, method type, and intended audience match the configured venue's stated scope; whether the paper states a specific claim of novelty and locates it against the existing record rather than against a strawman gap; whether the contribution is of a size and kind the venue's readership would find worth the page count; and whether the stated implications are ones this readership can actually act on.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is real but too thin or too narrow for this venue — a single-site descriptive report offered without any generalisable claim, an incremental variation whose novelty over cited prior work is not articulated, or a topic that sits at the edge of scope and would need substantial reframing to address this readership.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but under-argued — the novelty claim is present yet vague, positioning against the closest prior work is thin or missing, or implications are stated at a level of generality that leaves the readership's actual use of the result unclear.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is outside the venue's scope altogether or offers no contribution to accept — no identifiable new claim, evidence, or synthesis beyond restating established knowledge, or duplication of work already in the record such that publication here would serve no readership.
