## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by methodology).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its analytic execution would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the paper's own field. From a rigor standpoint I read this as covering four linked things: whether the design can in principle support the inferential claim being made (sampling frame, assignment or comparison structure, temporal ordering of measures, control for obvious confounding); whether the data were handled defensibly (provenance, inclusion/exclusion rules, missingness, measurement definitions, unit of analysis, any derived or aggregated variables); whether the statistical reporting is complete and honest (effect sizes with uncertainty, not just significance stars; model specification stated; assumptions checked; multiplicity and researcher degrees of freedom addressed); and whether a reader could reproduce or at least audit the work (analysis code, data availability or a stated reason for withholding, software versions, preregistration status where applicable). It is mandatory, so a fatal defect here reaches the reject action under F1.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by domain).** This dimension concerns substantive correctness against the current state of evidence in the paper's field: whether the cited prior work is characterised faithfully rather than strawmanned or over-claimed, whether field-specific terminology is used in its accepted technical sense, and whether reported empirical results are consistent with what is known. It is not my dimension to score; the domain reviewer owns it. I note only that methodological and domain errors can be entangled — a misdescribed construct is both — and that when I encounter such an overlap I should confine my own scoring to the design/analysis face of it and leave the substantive-evidence face to the domain reviewer.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, eligible to both discourse-analysis and methodology roles; owned by da).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated claims actually follow from the evidence offered, whether the chain from data to conclusion has missing links, and whether any reasoning defect is serious enough to undermine the paper's core argument rather than merely a peripheral remark. My methodology-role contribution here is the inferential-warrant angle: whether the strength of language in the claims is licensed by the strength of the design, whether causal vocabulary is used where only association was established, whether limitations sections retract in the fine print what the abstract asserts, and whether alternative explanations that the design cannot rule out are acknowledged. Because I share eligibility rather than ownership, my score should be a methodology-informed reading of coherence, not an attempt to substitute for the discourse analyst's rhetorical assessment.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by perspective).** This dimension evaluates whether readers from adjacent fields can follow the framing, whether key definitions are supplied rather than assumed, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually backed by evidence from those boundaries rather than asserted. It is outside my dispatch role, so I will not score it. It matters to me only as context: a high-priority dimension scoring block triggers major revision under F4, which is a reminder that non-mandatory does not mean inconsequential.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by eic).** This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and legibility of figures and tables, and conformance to the venue's presentational conventions. It is the editor-in-chief role's to score. I flag only that figure and table quality has a rigor-adjacent face — a figure that omits error bars or an axis that hides scale is a reporting defect, not merely a presentation defect — and where that occurs I would treat it under D1 reporting completeness rather than poaching D5.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by eic).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge in a way that is original and significant for that readership. It is mandatory but not eligible to my role, so I will not score it. From a methodology perspective the relevant adjacency is that novelty claims are themselves empirical claims: if the contribution rests on a design or dataset being new, the evidence for that novelty falls partly under my rigor assessment even though the fit judgment does not.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of design type and unit of analysis; how the sample/population was defined and how cases entered or left it; operational definitions of every construct including any composite or engagement-style measure; treatment of missing data and attrition; the exact model specification with covariates named; effect estimates reported with uncertainty intervals rather than significance markers alone; assumption checks and sensitivity or robustness analyses; handling of multiple comparisons; and reproducibility affordances such as data/code availability, software versions, ethics or IRB approval, and any preregistration.
what_triggers_block: A core inferential claim rests on an analysis whose specification, comparison group, or outcome definition is not recoverable from the text, or a confound structurally built into the design (e.g. self-selection into the measured exposure) is neither controlled nor addressed, such that the reported estimates cannot be interpreted as the authors interpret them.
what_triggers_warn: The design and analysis are interpretable and broadly sound, but reporting is incomplete in ways a reader can work around — missing uncertainty intervals or effect sizes, unstated assumption checks, vague missing-data handling, absent code/data availability statement, or unreported sensitivity analysis for a discretionary modelling choice.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence of an error that invalidates the empirical basis wholesale — analysis applied to a unit of analysis inconsistent with the sampling structure in a way that makes standard errors meaningless, outcome measured before or contemporaneously with the exposure it is claimed to result from, undisclosed post-hoc redefinition of the outcome or sample to obtain the reported result, or internally contradictory numbers (Ns, degrees of freedom, subgroup totals) that cannot be reconciled and would change the reported conclusion.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the abstract, results, and discussion make claims of the same strength; whether causal, predictive, and associational language is used where the design licenses it; whether each stated conclusion traces back to a specific reported estimate; whether plausible alternative explanations the design cannot exclude are named and engaged; whether limitations genuinely constrain the claims rather than being ritually listed and then ignored; and whether the practical or policy recommendations follow from what was actually measured.
what_triggers_block: The central conclusion is stated in causal or prescriptive terms that the reported design cannot support, and the manuscript does not qualify it — i.e. the paper's headline claim exceeds its inferential warrant in a way that reshapes what a reader would take away.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument holds, but there is localised slippage — abstract phrasing stronger than the results section, an alternative explanation left unaddressed, a recommendation that outruns the measured outcome, or a limitation acknowledged in one place while a claim elsewhere proceeds as if it did not exist.
what_triggers_fatal: The thesis is self-contradictory or circular at its foundation — a stated conclusion is directly negated by the paper's own reported evidence, or the argument's premise presupposes the conclusion it purports to establish, so that no revision short of a different study could repair the reasoning.
