## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** From an editorial oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study was actually built in a way that can bear the weight of its conclusions: whether the design suits the question, whether data collection and cleaning are described well enough to be trusted, whether the statistical reporting is complete and correctly interpreted, and whether another researcher would have enough material to attempt a replication. It is a mandatory dimension, so a fatal defect here forces rejection rather than revision. I am not the scorer for it, but I will read its verdict as a load-bearing input to the final decision.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This covers whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with what the field currently knows, whether prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than conveniently, and whether domain-specific terms, measures, and reported results are used correctly. Misstating a well-established finding or mislabelling a standard construct counts here, not as a writing problem. Being mandatory, it carries the same escalation weight as methodology.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer with methodology as a second eligible role).** The question is whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether each claim is actually supported by the evidence offered, whether the chain from data to interpretation to implication is unbroken, and whether any reasoning defects are severe enough to hollow out the main argument. Local infelicities of phrasing belong elsewhere; this dimension is about the logical spine.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This assesses whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper without insider knowledge — whether framing, definitions, and stated implications travel — and whether any claim reaching beyond the home discipline is actually substantiated rather than asserted. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a block here escalates to major revision but cannot on its own trigger rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as Editor-in-Chief).** This is my direct responsibility: how the manuscript is organised, whether the exposition is clear enough for the argument to be followed on a single careful reading, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission follows the venue's conventions for sectioning, referencing, and reporting. It is a normal-priority dimension, so problems here shape revision requests rather than acceptance in principle.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as Editor-in-Chief).** Also mine: whether this manuscript belongs in this venue at all, and whether it advances something its readership does not already have. That means judging scope alignment with the configured venue, originality relative to existing work, and whether the significance of the contribution is proportionate to the space and prominence requested. Because it is mandatory, a fatal deficiency here — a manuscript with essentially no contribution or plainly outside scope — ends the process at rejection.

**Failure-condition awareness.** I also register that the decision arithmetic is contract-driven, not discretionary: a fatal block on any mandatory dimension yields reject; a block on any mandatory dimension, or two or more mandatory warns across the panel majority, or a block on the high-priority dimension, yields major revision; any single warn yields minor revision; and a unanimous clean sheet yields accept.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether sections follow a coherent order with a locatable research question, methods, and results; whether prose is unambiguous at the sentence and paragraph level; whether every figure and table is numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, referenced in text, and reports units, sample sizes, and uncertainty; and whether reference style, section headings, abstract, and reporting conventions match the venue's stated requirements.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is disordered enough that a competent reader cannot reliably determine what was done, in what order, or what the reported numbers refer to — for example missing or uninterpretable figures and tables that carry primary results, or absent methods/results sections.
what_triggers_warn: The paper is followable overall but has localised defects — unclear passages, under-captioned or cluttered exhibits, inconsistent terminology or notation, misordered subsections, or departures from venue formatting and referencing conventions — that require revision without obscuring the substance.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, method, and intended readership match the configured venue's scope; whether the manuscript states a specific novel claim and positions it explicitly against the closest prior work; whether the demonstrated increment is substantive rather than incremental repackaging; and whether the length and prominence sought are proportionate to what is delivered.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is stated but thin or poorly differentiated — overlap with existing work is substantial and unacknowledged, or the significance claimed to the readership materially exceeds what the manuscript actually establishes — so that acceptance would require a rebuilt contribution case.
what_triggers_warn: Scope and contribution are legitimate but under-articulated — novelty is implied rather than argued, positioning against neighbouring work is incomplete, or the implications for this venue's readers are asserted without development — fixable by targeted revision of framing and related-work discussion.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is outside the configured venue's scope altogether, or presents no identifiable original contribution — for example a restatement of established results, or content whose readership is plainly served by a different venue — such that no revision within this venue could remedy it.
