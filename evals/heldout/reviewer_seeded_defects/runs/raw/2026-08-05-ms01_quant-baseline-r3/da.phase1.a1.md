## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** The challenge here is whether the study's design and data handling would survive a hostile replication attempt rather than merely reading as competent. An adversarial reading asks whether the sampling frame, measurement instruments, analytic model, and statistical reporting are specified tightly enough that a skeptical outsider could rebuild the analysis and get the same numbers, and whether the manuscript quietly relies on choices (exclusions, transformations, model selection) that were never justified. Anything that makes the reported results non-checkable — missing effect sizes, undisclosed data availability, unstated assumptions — is a rigor deficit even if the prose sounds authoritative. This dimension is not mine to score, but its weaknesses often surface as gaps I must treat as evidence shortfalls under D3.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** The adversarial question is whether the manuscript's factual scaffolding actually holds: are the cited prior findings represented as their authors would recognise them, are field-specific terms used in their standard technical sense rather than a loosened colloquial sense, and are contrary results in the literature acknowledged rather than silently omitted. A paper can be internally tidy and still be wrong about the state of the field, and selective or stale citation is a way of manufacturing apparent novelty. I do not score this dimension, but a misrepresented literature base directly weakens any argument built on it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, my dimension).** My job is to attack the load-bearing chain of reasoning: does the stated thesis survive when I grant the authors their data but refuse to grant them their inferences? I will test whether every headline claim is entailed by the evidence actually presented, whether the conclusion drifts beyond what the design can license, whether alternative explanations were considered and dispatched or merely ignored, and whether definitions stay constant from abstract to discussion. Fallacies that matter here are the structural ones — conflating association with production of an outcome, treating absence of a detected difference as evidence of equivalence, circular definitions, motte-and-bailey shifts between a defensible narrow claim and a broad marketed one, and selective emphasis that lets a weak result carry a strong abstract. Internal contradictions between sections, and claims in the abstract or conclusion that no section substantiates, are the sharpest signals of failure.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** The adversarial framing is whether a competent reader from an adjacent field could actually use this paper, or whether it is written for a small in-group and merely gestures at broader significance. Claims of interdisciplinary reach are cheap to assert and expensive to substantiate; unexplained jargon, undefined constructs, and implications asserted for populations or settings the study never touched are the failure modes. I do not score this dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** The question is whether the organisation, exposition, and display items serve the reader or obscure the argument. Adversarially, poor structure is rarely neutral — buried methods, figures that do not support the claims made about them, and inconsistent reporting conventions frequently conceal substantive problems rather than merely inconveniencing the reader. I do not score this dimension.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** The challenge is whether the manuscript offers something the target readership does not already have, and whether the claimed originality survives comparison with what is already published. Incremental restatement dressed as novelty, or a contribution mismatched to the venue's scope and depth expectations, fails here regardless of how clean the execution is. I do not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the central thesis stated in the abstract and conclusion is entailed by the evidence actually presented, with stable construct definitions across sections, explicit consideration of rival explanations, causal or generalisation language calibrated to what the design can support, and no internal contradictions between reported results and their interpretation.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing headline claim is unsupported by or contradicted by the paper's own presented evidence — for example a causal or effectiveness conclusion drawn from a design that can only license association, an equivalence claim resting on a null result, or a stated finding that conflicts with the numbers reported elsewhere in the manuscript — such that removing the unsupported inference would collapse the paper's stated contribution.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument survives scrutiny but is overreached or under-defended at identifiable points — unhedged generalisation beyond the studied population or setting, an obvious alternative explanation raised nowhere, a construct whose definition shifts subtly between sections, or emphasis in the abstract that outruns the strength of the corresponding result — where targeted rewriting and explicit limitation statements would repair the chain of reasoning.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript has no coherent recoverable argument at all — the thesis is circular or unfalsifiable as stated, the reasoning chain contradicts itself at its foundation rather than at its edges, or the conclusions are so disconnected from the presented evidence that no revision short of a different study and a rewritten claim set could reconcile them.
