## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of data would survive scrutiny from a competent peer reviewer in the manuscript's own field. That covers how the sample was obtained, whether the instrument and measures are defensible, whether statistical procedures are reported completely enough for a reader to judge them, and whether another researcher could plausibly reproduce the work from what is on the page. It is owned by the methodology reviewer and is not mine to score; my role as editor is to register that a fatal problem here is grounds for outright rejection rather than revision.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension concerns factual correctness within the substantive field: whether the claims the manuscript advances are consistent with what the current evidence base actually supports, whether cited prior work is represented faithfully rather than stretched, and whether field-specific terminology, constructs, and reported results are used correctly. Misstating a construct or misattributing a finding to a prior study falls here. The domain reviewer owns it; I do not score it, but its mandatory status means a fatal finding drives the panel decision regardless of how well the paper reads.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, discourse-analysis role with methodology as a second eligible reviewer).** This dimension tests whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the evidence actually presented does the work the conclusions ask of it, whether inferential steps are licensed, and whether any reasoning defect is severe enough to hollow out the main argument. Overreaching from correlational evidence to causal or prescriptive claims would be the archetypal problem here. Owned by the DA reviewer; outside my scoring remit.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension asks whether a reader from an adjacent discipline could enter the paper without insider knowledge — whether framing, key definitions, and stated implications are made legible outside the immediate specialty, and whether any claim the paper makes about relevance beyond its own field is actually substantiated rather than merely asserted. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a block here forces major revision but cannot on its own trigger rejection. The perspective reviewer owns it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, my dimension as editor).** This is my first scoring responsibility. It covers whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow the argument from problem statement through method, results, and discussion without backtracking; whether prose is clear and precise rather than padded or ambiguous; whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, self-explanatory, and genuinely informative rather than decorative or redundant with the text; and whether the submission observes the conventions expected at the configured venue, including structural completeness such as abstract, sectioning, and reference formatting. Its normal priority means problems here shape the revision request but do not by themselves force a major-revision outcome unless they reach block level under the general warn rule.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, my dimension as editor).** This is my second scoring responsibility and the heavier of the two. It asks two linked questions. First, is this manuscript the kind of work the configured venue publishes for the audience it serves — right scope, right level of ambition, right genre. Second, does it make a contribution that is both original and significant enough to warrant space in that venue: does it tell the readership something it did not already have, or does it restate settled knowledge, replicate without adding, or report a descriptive exercise whose findings carry no consequence for research or practice. Because this dimension is mandatory, a fatal judgement here means rejection and a block-level judgement means major revision, so I must hold the threshold honestly rather than deferring to the paper's competence on other dimensions.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript follows a coherent and complete structure for its genre (framing of problem, method, results, discussion, limitations, references), whether prose is precise and free of ambiguity or padding, whether section proportions match their argumentative load, and whether figures and tables are correctly labelled, self-contained, legible, and non-redundant with the running text.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken enough to obstruct comprehension — for example essential sections absent or unrecognisable, results reported without any interpretable structure, figures or tables uninterpretable or inconsistent with the text, or writing so unclear that the reader cannot reliably reconstruct what was done or found.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is comprehensible but carries fixable craft defects — awkward or repetitive passages, disproportionate or thin sections, missing or generic figure and table captions, inconsistent reference or heading conventions, or minor deviations from venue formatting expectations.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, scope, methodological ambition, and framing match what the configured venue publishes for its readership, and whether the paper states a contribution that is genuinely new relative to existing work and consequential enough to matter to that readership rather than confirmatory restatement of what is already established.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is too slight or too derivative for the venue as submitted — findings restate well-established results without new evidence, theoretical extension, or population-specific value, or the scope and depth fall materially below what the venue's readership expects — yet a plausible path to sufficiency exists through added analysis, reframing, or expanded scope.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but underdeveloped — the novelty claim is stated vaguely or oversold relative to what is delivered, positioning against prior work is thin, or the implications for the venue's readership are asserted rather than argued, while the underlying contribution remains real.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is categorically outside what the venue publishes, or offers no identifiable contribution at all, such that no revision within the paper's existing data and scope could make it publishable here.
