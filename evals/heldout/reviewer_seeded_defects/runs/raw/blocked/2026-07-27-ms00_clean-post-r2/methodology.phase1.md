## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my owned dimension).** From a methodology standpoint, this asks whether the study's design can actually support the inferences drawn from it: whether the sampling frame and recruitment are described well enough to know who was measured and who was missed, whether the measurement instruments are specified with enough detail (item wording, response scales, reliability evidence) to be evaluated and reused, whether data handling decisions (exclusions, missingness, transformations) are stated rather than implied, whether the statistical reporting includes the quantities a reader needs to judge magnitude and uncertainty rather than significance alone, and whether reproducibility affordances (analysis specification, data or instrument availability, software details) meet the ordinary standard of the field. The test is not whether the design is ambitious but whether it is transparently reported and internally adequate to the claims it is asked to carry.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory).** This dimension asks whether the substantive content is correct on its own terms: whether assertions about the state of knowledge in the field are supportable, whether cited prior work is characterised as its authors would recognise, whether technical vocabulary is used in its established sense, and whether reported results from other studies are reproduced faithfully. This is owned by the domain reviewer, not by me.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, methodology-eligible).** This asks whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together as an argument: whether the stated conclusions follow from what was actually measured, whether the strength of language matches the strength of the evidence, whether alternative explanations that the design cannot rule out are acknowledged rather than quietly excluded, and whether the paper avoids reasoning moves — inferential overreach, circularity, equivocation between constructs, treating absence of evidence as evidence — that would undermine the core claim. My angle on this dimension is specifically the inference chain from design to conclusion.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority).** This asks whether a competent reader from a neighbouring discipline can enter the paper: whether framing, definitions, and constructs are explained rather than assumed, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home field is actually backed by evidence rather than asserted by analogy. Owned by the perspective reviewer.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority).** This concerns craft and presentation: whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can follow the argument, whether exposition is clear, whether figures and tables are legible and self-contained, and whether the submission respects the venue's formatting and reporting conventions. Owned by the editor-in-chief role.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge enough to justify publication for that readership — originality, significance, and audience match. Owned by the editor-in-chief role.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit sampling frame and recruitment procedure with response/participation rate, instrument specification including item wording and response scale plus reliability/validity evidence, stated handling of missing data and exclusions, statistical reporting that includes effect sizes and uncertainty intervals alongside test statistics, correct alignment between the analysis model and the measurement level and dependence structure of the data, and reproducibility affordances such as an analysis plan, instrument appendix, or data/code availability statement.
what_triggers_block: Core analytic quantities are unreportable or unverifiable as written — for example inferential claims stated without the corresponding test statistics, sample sizes, or uncertainty measures; undisclosed exclusions or missing-data handling that could materially change the result; or an analysis applied to data whose structure violates its stated assumptions with no diagnostic or robustness treatment.
what_triggers_warn: The design and analysis are recoverable and defensible but reporting is thin in ways that limit independent evaluation — for example reliability evidence for a scale is omitted or cited only from another population, response rate is unstated, effect sizes are absent while p-values are present, or no instrument/data availability information is provided.
what_triggers_fatal: The reported methodology cannot have produced the reported results — internal contradictions in sample sizes, degrees of freedom, or reported statistics that are arithmetically impossible; measurement of a construct that is definitionally distinct from the one analysed; or evidence of a procedure that invalidates the dataset entirely, such that no revision short of re-collecting data could repair the inference.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A traceable chain from research question to design to measured quantity to stated conclusion, with hedging calibrated to what the design can support, explicit acknowledgement of the inferential limits imposed by the study's structure, alternative explanations for the observed pattern addressed rather than ignored, and consistent use of each key construct across abstract, results, and discussion.
what_triggers_block: The central conclusion asserts something the design cannot establish — for example a causal, directional, or longitudinal claim resting on evidence that supports none of these — or the discussion and abstract state a conclusion that the reported results do not sustain, such that the paper's headline claim would have to be rewritten.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument survives but contains local reasoning defects — overreaching phrasing in isolated passages, a limitation named in one section and then disregarded in another, or an unaddressed rival explanation that weakens but does not overturn the stated conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The thesis is self-defeating as argued — the paper's own reported evidence contradicts its central claim, or the argument depends on a circularity or equivocation between two distinct constructs so foundational that no revision preserving the thesis could resolve it.
