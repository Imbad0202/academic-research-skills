## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its execution would survive the scrutiny of a competent peer reviewer in the manuscript's field: whether sampling, instrumentation, data handling, and analysis are described in enough detail to be judged, whether statistical reporting is complete and correctly applied, and whether the paper offers enough procedural detail (materials, instruments, data, analysis steps) for another researcher to attempt reproduction. From my vantage point as the domain-accuracy reviewer, I do not score this, but I note that methodological detail is also the substrate on which domain claims rest, so I will read it for context without assigning it a grade.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role — mine).** This is my owned dimension. It asks whether every substantive claim the manuscript makes is consistent with the current state of evidence in its field; whether prior literature is described as that literature actually reads rather than as the authors would like it to read; whether field-specific constructs, instruments, and technical vocabulary are used with their established meanings; and whether the reported results are stated accurately and interpreted within the limits that the field's evidence base allows. Misattributed findings, constructs invoked under the wrong name, superseded evidence presented as current, and conclusions that outrun what the domain literature supports all fall here.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, DA role with methodology eligibility).** This dimension checks the internal logic of the manuscript: whether the central thesis stays the same claim from abstract to conclusion, whether the evidence actually presented supports the inferences drawn from it, and whether any reasoning defect — circularity, equivocation, unwarranted causal leaps, ignoring disconfirming cases — undercuts the paper's core argument. I am not eligible to score it, and I will keep my domain findings distinct from coherence judgments so that the assigned reviewers' scoring is not pre-empted.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension evaluates whether readers from adjacent fields can enter the paper: whether framing, definitions of specialised terms, and stated implications are intelligible outside the immediate subfield, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is backed by evidence rather than asserted by analogy. It is outside my scoring role.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role).** This dimension covers craft: how the manuscript is organised, how clearly the exposition proceeds, whether figures and tables are legible, well-labelled, and informative, and whether the submission follows the target venue's formatting and reporting conventions. It is outside my scoring role.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue at all and whether it advances something original and significant enough to matter to that venue's readership, rather than restating settled knowledge or reporting an increment too small to warrant the page space. It is outside my scoring role.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy

dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Whether field-specific constructs, instruments, and terminology are used with their established definitions; whether cited prior work is characterised as it actually reads and supports the claim it is attached to; whether reported results are stated accurately and interpreted only as far as the domain's current evidence base permits; whether known contrary or superseding evidence in the field is acknowledged rather than omitted.
what_triggers_block: One or more central claims — those the paper's headline contribution depends on — are contradicted by, or materially misrepresent, established domain evidence or a cited source, such that the stated contribution cannot stand as written without substantial correction and re-analysis of the literature.
what_triggers_warn: Localised domain inaccuracies that do not reach the paper's core claim: imprecise or non-standard use of a field construct or instrument name, a citation that only partially supports the sentence it is attached to, an outdated reference where current evidence exists, or an interpretation that modestly overreaches the reported result but is repairable by rewording.
what_triggers_fatal: Pervasive misrepresentation of the domain evidence base — for example, fabricated, non-existent, or systematically misattributed sources, results reported inconsistently with the described data, or a conclusion that inverts the established finding it claims to build on — such that no revision short of a different paper could make the domain content trustworthy.
