## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** This dimension asks whether the study's design, its handling of data, its statistical reporting, and the affordances it offers for reproduction would survive scrutiny by competent peer reviewers in the manuscript's own field. From an editorial-oversight standpoint it is the load-bearing dimension: a manuscript whose inferential machinery cannot support the weight placed on it cannot be repaired by good prose. It is owned by the methodology reviewer and is not mine to score, so my role is to respect that reviewer's verdict as authoritative and to fold it into the aggregate decision rather than to second-guess it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** This dimension covers whether the manuscript's assertions are consistent with the current state of evidence in its subject area, whether the prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than conveniently, and whether field-specific terminology and reported results are free of factual error. Editorially, this is the dimension that protects the record from propagating mistakes that non-specialist readers cannot detect. It belongs to the domain reviewer, and I neither plan nor issue a score for it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, da and methodology roles):** This dimension examines whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms, whether the evidence actually adduced supports the claims actually made, and whether any reasoning defects are severe enough to undermine the paper's core argument rather than merely to blemish it. It is a mandatory dimension owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer with the methodology reviewer as a second eligible scorer. As editor-in-chief I do not score it; I read its verdict as the test of whether the paper's argument survives contact with its own data.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, perspective role):** This dimension asks whether the framing, the operative definitions, and the stated implications remain intelligible and useful to readers one field over, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually substantiated rather than merely gestured at. It carries high rather than mandatory priority, which means a block here escalates the decision but cannot by itself trigger rejection. It is owned by the perspective reviewer and is outside my scoring remit.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, eic role):** This dimension is mine. It concerns how the manuscript is organised, how clearly the exposition carries a reader from problem to method to result to interpretation, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the manuscript observes the conventions of the venue it is submitted to. Its priority is normal, so deficiencies here shape revision requests and cannot on their own force a major-revision or reject outcome; they can nonetheless surface a minor-revision requirement under the aggregate failure conditions.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, eic role):** This dimension is also mine and is the one I own with the highest stakes. It asks two joined questions: whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue given that venue's scope and readership, and whether it advances something original and consequential enough to justify the space it would occupy. Because it is mandatory, a block here drives the decision to major revision and a fatal defect here drives outright rejection, independent of how strong the methodology or domain assessments turn out to be. Editorially this is where I must resist both the temptation to wave through competent-but-inconsequential work and the temptation to punish modest scope that is honestly declared.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript has a coherent section architecture with a discernible through-line from research question to method to results to interpretation; whether claims are stated in prose a reader can parse without re-reading; whether every figure and table is numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, referenced in the text, and reporting units and sample sizes; whether abstract, keywords, headings, citation style, and length observe the venue's stated conventions; and whether the length of the manuscript is proportionate to what it attempts.
what_triggers_block: The organisation actively obstructs assessment — for example results reported without any corresponding method description, findings that appear only inside a figure with no textual account, tables or figures whose axes, units, denominators, or captions are missing or contradict the text, or exposition so unclear that a competent reader cannot reconstruct what was done.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but structurally or presentationally untidy — misplaced material between sections, discussion that restates results without advancing them, inconsistent terminology or notation, uncaptioned or redundant exhibits, citation-format or heading-convention deviations, or an abstract that misdescribes the body's emphasis.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript's topic, method, and register match the configured venue's scope and the expectations of its readership; whether the paper states a specific contribution rather than a general area of interest; whether that contribution is positioned against the existing literature so its increment is visible; whether the significance claimed is proportionate to the evidence and scope actually delivered; and whether the paper offers something a reader of this venue could not get from already-published work.
what_triggers_block: The contribution as claimed is not delivered, or is delivered so thinly that publication in this venue would not be warranted — for example a restatement of well-established findings presented as new, an absent or nominal engagement with prior work such that no increment can be identified, or stated implications and generalisations that the study's design and scope cannot support.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real but narrow or imperfectly positioned — an incremental result whose novelty is asserted rather than demonstrated, framing pitched at a readership adjacent to rather than identical with this venue's, overstated significance language that could be corrected by rewriting, or a contribution statement that must be inferred by the reader instead of being declared.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is categorically outside the venue's scope, or the purported contribution is void — duplicate or substantially overlapping publication of the authors' own prior work, a contribution that rests on results the paper does not in fact contain, or a central claim of novelty that is demonstrably false against the cited record.
