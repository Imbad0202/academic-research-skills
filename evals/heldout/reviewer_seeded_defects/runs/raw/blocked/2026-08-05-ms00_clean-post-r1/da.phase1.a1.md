## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** The demand here is that the study's design, sampling, measurement, data handling, and statistical reporting would survive an unsympathetic replication attempt, and that a reader has enough procedural detail to attempt one. Adversarially, I would press on whether the design can support the inferences drawn from it at all, whether the analytic choices were fixed in advance or fitted to the data, and whether the absence of instruments, item wording, response rates, or analysis scripts makes the reported numbers unauditable. Convenience of the design is not an excuse for inferential overreach; the question is whether the reported machinery is strong enough that a skeptic must accept the numbers as produced.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with what the field currently holds, whether cited prior work is characterised as its authors would recognise, and whether domain terminology and reported quantities are used correctly. The adversarial stance is to assume that convenient citations may have been mischaracterised, that a construct name may have been borrowed without its established operationalisation, and that a claim presented as settled background may be contested or superseded. A single confidently stated factual error about the domain, or a mis-attributed finding used as a load-bearing premise, is a real defect rather than a cosmetic one.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by me as devil's advocate).** The requirement is that the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: premises are stated, the evidence actually offered supports the conclusions actually drawn, the scope of the claim matches the scope of the warrant, and no inferential move is doing work it cannot bear. My adversarial job is to reconstruct the argument as a chain and then attack each link — to look for correlational evidence carrying causal conclusions, conclusions that reappear later in stronger form than when they were first justified, definitions that shift between sections, alternative explanations that are named in the limitations and then ignored in the abstract, and rhetorical hedging that lets an unsupported claim survive by being unfalsifiable. Internal contradiction between abstract, results, discussion, and limitations is a coherence failure regardless of how sound the underlying data may be.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, owned by the perspective reviewer).** Here the paper must be readable and usable by competent readers in adjacent fields: framing that explains why the problem matters outside its own niche, definitions that do not presuppose insider vocabulary, and any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries backed by evidence from those disciplines rather than by assertion. Adversarially, I would suspect that interdisciplinary implications are decorative — asserted in the introduction and discussion to inflate reach without any corresponding evidence or engagement with the borrowed field's own literature.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, owned by the editor-in-chief).** The manuscript must be organised so that a reader can locate the claim, the evidence, and the caveats without reconstruction work; exposition must be clear; figures and tables must be self-explanatory, correctly labelled, and consistent with the text; and venue conventions must be respected. The adversarial reading treats structural disorder as a possible concealment mechanism — results introduced only in the discussion, tables whose numbers do not reconcile with the prose, or missing standard sections that would have exposed weaknesses.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief).** The paper must belong in the configured venue and must add something its readership does not already have. Adversarially, the test is whether removing this manuscript from the literature would cost anyone anything: whether the finding is already well established, whether the contribution is merely a local instance of a general result, and whether the scale and depth of the work match what the venue's readers expect. Novelty claimed in the abstract must be traceable to something specific in the results, not to the mere fact that this particular sample had not previously been surveyed.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A reconstructable argument chain in which the stated thesis, the evidence actually presented, and the strength and scope of every conclusion match — including consistency of the same claim across abstract, results, discussion, and limitations, stable definitions of central constructs, explicit handling of rival explanations, and inferential language calibrated to what the design can license.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing inferential gap that the paper cannot repair without new evidence or a substantially weakened thesis — for example the central conclusion asserts a causal, generalisable, or predictive relationship that the presented evidence cannot support, or a key premise is asserted without any supporting evidence anywhere in the manuscript.
what_triggers_warn: Localised coherence defects that are fixable by rewriting rather than by new evidence — overreaching or unhedged phrasing in the abstract or discussion relative to a more careful results section, a rival explanation acknowledged in limitations but ignored where the conclusion is stated, mildly drifting terminology, or a conclusion that outruns its warrant in degree while remaining recognisably supported.
what_triggers_fatal: Irreparable internal incoherence — the manuscript states mutually contradictory conclusions on the same question without resolution, or the conclusion is contradicted by the paper's own reported evidence, such that no revision short of retracting or replacing the central thesis can rescue the argument.
