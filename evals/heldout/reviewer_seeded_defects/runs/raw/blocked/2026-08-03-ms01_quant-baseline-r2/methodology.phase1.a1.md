## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my owned dimension).** From a methods-rigor standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's architecture would survive the ordinary scrutiny of a peer reviewer in its field: whether the design chosen can actually support the inferences drawn from it, whether the data were obtained, cleaned, and bounded in a way that is disclosed rather than assumed, whether the statistical or analytic reporting is complete enough that a reader can judge uncertainty rather than merely accept point estimates, and whether enough procedural detail exists that an independent party could re-run or at least re-specify the analysis. It is the dimension I own and must adjudicate against a fatal threshold, not merely comment on.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension governs whether the manuscript's substantive assertions are consistent with the current state of evidence in its field, whether prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than conveniently, and whether field-specific terms, constructs, and reported results are used correctly. Methodologically I recognise it as adjacent to my work — mis-stated constructs often signal measurement problems — but adjudication belongs to the domain role, and I am not scoring it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the devil's-advocate role, but methodology is eligible).** This dimension concerns whether the paper's central thesis holds together internally: whether each claim is actually licensed by the evidence adduced for it, whether the chain from data to conclusion contains unstated leaps, and whether reasoning defects — overreach, circularity, conflation of association with mechanism, selective emphasis — are severe enough to undermine the core argument. As a methodologist my angle on this is the inferential warrant question: does the analytic apparatus described actually entitle the conclusions asserted?

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension evaluates whether the framing, definitions, and stated implications are intelligible and useful to readers from neighbouring fields, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home discipline is properly substantiated rather than merely gestured at. It is not in my dispatch scope and I will not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and legibility of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's presentational conventions. Although unclear reporting can shade into methodological opacity, the presentational judgement here is the EiC's, and I will not assign it a score.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it offers a contribution that is original and significant enough for that readership. Novelty and fit are editorial judgements outside my dispatch role, so I am not scoring this dimension despite its mandatory status.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Whether the design (sampling frame, unit of analysis, comparison or control strategy, time structure) is stated explicitly and matches the inferential target; whether data provenance, inclusion/exclusion rules, attrition, and missing-data handling are disclosed; whether measures are operationally defined with evidence of reliability/validity; whether estimates are reported with uncertainty (intervals, standard errors, exact test statistics, effect sizes), with pre-specification versus exploratory analysis distinguished and multiplicity acknowledged; whether confounding and selection pressures are identified and addressed rather than named and dropped; and whether reproducibility affordances (analysis code, data availability or a stated reason for withholding, software versions, ethics/consent approval) are present.
what_triggers_block: A core inferential gap that no amount of rewording repairs without new analysis — e.g. the reported design cannot support the estimand claimed, a decisive confound or selection mechanism is left unmodelled and unaddressed, key statistical results are given without any uncertainty quantification or test detail, or the analysis pipeline is described so incompletely that its correctness cannot be assessed at all.
what_triggers_warn: Recoverable reporting deficits that leave the inference plausible but under-documented — e.g. missing exact test statistics or effect sizes alongside reported significance, unstated attrition or missing-data procedure, thin measurement validation, absent code/data availability statement, or robustness checks asserted but not shown.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported methodology is not merely weak but unsound or misrepresented — e.g. internally contradictory sample sizes or degrees of freedom that cannot be reconciled, an analysis that is invalid for the data structure as described, outcome or model specification apparently chosen after seeing results and reported as planned, or absence of required ethical/consent authorisation for human-subjects data.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated thesis is the same thesis defended in the results and conclusion sections; whether each substantive claim traces to specific evidence presented in the manuscript rather than to citation or assertion; whether the strength of hedging tracks the strength of the warrant; whether alternative explanations are entertained and adjudicated; and whether the abstract, discussion, and any policy or practice recommendations stay within the inferential reach of what the analysis can license.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing step in the central argument fails — the headline conclusion is stated at a strength the presented evidence cannot sustain, or a defect such as conflating correlation with causation, circular reasoning, or an unexamined alternative explanation directly undercuts the paper's main claim so that the thesis as written cannot stand.
what_triggers_warn: Localised reasoning slippage that does not topple the thesis — e.g. occasional overstated phrasing in the abstract or discussion, a secondary implication asserted without support, uneven hedging, or an alternative account acknowledged but dismissed without argument.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's argument is self-defeating or self-contradictory at its core — e.g. the conclusion asserts the opposite of what the reported results show, the thesis shifts to a different and unsupported claim between sections such that no coherent position is defensible, or the recommendations rest on a premise the paper's own evidence refutes.
