## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** As editorial oversight I read this dimension as the demand that the study's design, sampling, data handling, analytic choices, and statistical reporting be defensible to a competent specialist, and that enough procedural detail exist for another team to reproduce or at least audit the work. It is mandatory, which means a fatal defect here alone can sink the manuscript regardless of how attractive the topic is. It is not mine to score — the methodology reviewer owns it — but I must respect its verdict when I assemble the final decision.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This asks whether the substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence in the manuscript's own field, whether prior work is characterised fairly rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and whether field-specific terms, constructs, and reported results are used correctly. Errors here are not stylistic; they mislead readers who trust the venue. Scoring authority sits with the domain reviewer alone.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, with methodology also eligible).** The question is whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated evidence actually licenses the stated conclusions, whether intermediate steps follow, and whether any reasoning defect — overreach, circularity, equivocation, causal language on correlational footing — undermines the spine of the argument rather than a peripheral remark. Mandatory status means incoherence at the core is decision-determining.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension protects the adjacent-field reader: framing, definitions, and stated implications should be intelligible without insider vocabulary, and any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries must be backed rather than asserted. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a blocking finding drives revision rather than outright rejection, but it cannot be waved through as mere presentation.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as editor-in-chief).** I judge whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow it — sections that do the work their headings promise, an exposition that introduces before it uses, figures and tables that are legible, correctly labelled, and actually referenced — and whether it observes the conventions the venue expects (abstract, structure, citation practice, length discipline). This is a normal-priority dimension: it shapes the revision burden and can trigger a minor-revision outcome, but it carries no fatal escalation of its own.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as editor-in-chief).** Here I ask two linked questions: does this manuscript belong in this venue for this readership, and does it add something the literature did not already have? Fit covers topic, scope, audience, and the kind of evidence the venue publishes; contribution covers originality and significance proportionate to the venue's bar, not merely competence. Because this dimension is mandatory, a fatal-level failure — nothing new, or plainly wrong venue — is a rejection condition under F1, and a blocking failure escalates to major revision under F2.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether sections deliver what their headings promise, terms and measures are defined before use, the abstract and introduction state the question and finding plainly, figures and tables are legible, self-labelled, and cited in text, and the manuscript observes venue conventions for structure, citation, and length.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or presentation is broken enough to obstruct assessment — for example key results reported only in prose with no usable table or figure, undefined core constructs, missing or unlabelled figure axes and units, absent or non-functional method/results separation, or a word count far outside venue norms for the claimed article type.
what_triggers_warn: Locally fixable clarity and formatting defects that do not obstruct assessment — inconsistent terminology, redundant or misordered subsections, overlong unbroken passages, uncited or cosmetically weak exhibits, uneven citation formatting, or an abstract that omits the headline result.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, scope, evidence type, and intended readership match the configured venue, and whether the manuscript states a contribution that is genuinely additive — a new result, dataset, setting, or synthesis — with its novelty positioned explicitly against the closest prior work rather than merely asserted.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is real but underspecified or oversold relative to what is delivered — novelty asserted without positioning against nearest prior work, incremental findings framed as a breakthrough, or partial venue mismatch in framing and audience that requires substantive reorientation rather than reject.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution stand, but the case for them is thin in presentation — the contribution statement is buried or vague, the readership implications are gestured at rather than argued, or related-work coverage is sparse enough that the increment is harder to see than it should be.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript offers no discernible increment over existing published work, restates a known result as if new, or addresses a subject and audience outside the venue's remit such that no revision within scope could make it publishable here.
