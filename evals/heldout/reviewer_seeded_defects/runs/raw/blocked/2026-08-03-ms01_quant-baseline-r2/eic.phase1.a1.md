## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of data would survive scrutiny from a competent methodologist in the manuscript's own field. That covers how the sample was obtained and bounded, whether the analytic strategy actually matches the question being asked, whether statistics are reported completely enough for a reader to judge them rather than merely trust them, and whether enough procedural detail exists that another team could attempt the same study. It is flagged mandatory, so a determination that the evidentiary machinery is unsound is not a stylistic quibble — it is grounds to halt the paper.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This asks whether the manuscript tells the truth about its own field. Substantive claims should be consistent with the current state of evidence, cited predecessors should be characterised as they actually argued rather than as convenient foils, and field-specific vocabulary, instruments, and reported quantities should be used correctly. Misdescribing prior literature or misusing a technical term is treated here as a factual defect, not a presentational one, and because the dimension is mandatory, serious errors of this kind carry decision-level weight.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, with methodology also eligible).** The question is whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated conclusion is the conclusion the presented evidence licenses, whether the intermediate steps connect, and whether any inferential move — overreach from association to cause, quiet redefinition of a key term mid-argument, conclusions that outrun the design — undercuts the spine of the paper. Coherence failures are distinct from methodological ones: a technically clean study can still argue for something its own results do not support.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** Because the venue serves readers beyond the authors' immediate specialty, this dimension checks whether an intelligent adjacent-field reader can enter the paper: are the framing and motivation legible without insider assumptions, are key constructs defined rather than presumed, and are the implications drawn for other fields actually supported rather than asserted as decoration. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so failures here are serious enough to force revision but do not by themselves terminate the manuscript.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as editor-in-chief).** This is the craft layer of the manuscript: whether the sections are ordered so the argument accumulates rather than doubles back, whether prose is precise enough that a reader is not reconstructing the author's intent, whether figures and tables are self-sufficient, correctly labelled, and consistent with the text they support, and whether the submission observes the venue's structural and formatting conventions. It carries normal priority, which reflects that presentational problems are usually remediable — but severe disorganisation can still make substantive assessment impossible.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as editor-in-chief).** This is the gatekeeping judgement proper: does this manuscript belong in this journal, addressed to this readership, and does it add something that readership does not already have. Fit concerns scope, register, and the kind of contribution the venue exists to publish; contribution concerns originality relative to existing work and whether the increment is significant enough to warrant the space. Being mandatory, a determination that the paper is either out of scope or substantively redundant is a decision-controlling finding rather than advisory commentary.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the section architecture lets the argument build in one direction, whether claims are stated in prose precise enough to be checked, whether every figure and table is numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, and numerically consistent with the body text, and whether the submission observes the venue's conventions for abstract, structure, reporting, and citation.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is disordered enough that a reader cannot reliably determine what was done or what is being claimed — for example results introduced with no corresponding method, or figures/tables whose reported values contradict the narrative — such that substantive review cannot proceed without a rewrite.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but carries localised presentational defects — undefined terms on first use, captions that need the body text to be intelligible, redundant or misordered passages, inconsistent notation, or venue-convention lapses — that a targeted revision pass could fix without restructuring the argument.
what_triggers_fatal: NOT_APPLICABLE_REMOVED

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, register, and evidentiary genre match what this venue publishes for its stated readership, and whether the manuscript articulates a specific increment over existing work that is both original and large enough to justify publication rather than being a restatement, a local replication with no added interpretation, or an internal report repackaged as research.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is materially thinner than the venue requires or the fit is strained — the stated novelty collapses into what is already established, or the scope sits at the edge of the journal's remit — so that acceptance would require substantial reframing or additional evidence of significance.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but under-argued — the novelty claim is asserted rather than positioned against the nearest prior work, or the relevance to this specific readership is left implicit — and can be repaired by sharpening the framing and situating the increment explicitly.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is outside the venue's scope altogether or offers no identifiable original contribution to its readership, such that no revision within the paper's current premises could make it publishable here.
