## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** From a methods-rigor standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's design actually licenses the inferences drawn from it: is the research design named and matched to the question, is the sampling frame and recruitment path specified, is data collection instrumentation documented, is the analytic procedure traceable from raw material to reported result, and are the reporting conventions of the field honoured (effect sizes or equivalent qualitative rigor criteria, uncertainty, missingness, non-response, coding reliability where applicable)? It also covers reproducibility affordances — whether an independent researcher could re-run or re-audit the analysis from what is printed, including instrument availability, coding frames, ethics/consent approval, and any data or material access statement. I treat this as the load-bearing dimension for my role and the one where silence in the manuscript counts as evidence of absence, not as a neutral gap.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension concerns whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence in its field, whether cited prior work is characterised faithfully rather than conscripted, and whether field-specific vocabulary, frameworks, and previously reported results are stated without factual error. Methodologically it is adjacent to my remit — mischaracterised prior findings often signal a flawed evidence base — but scoring authority sits with the domain role, so I will not issue a score here.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer; I am an eligible co-scorer).** Here the question is whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms: do the stated claims follow from the evidence actually presented, is the strength of each claim calibrated to the strength of its warrant, are there internal contradictions between sections, and are there inferential fallacies — most importantly for my lens, over-generalisation beyond the sampled population, conflation of association with causation, or conclusions that quietly exceed what the chosen design can support. My contribution is the inference-validity half of coherence: whether the argumentative chain from data to conclusion contains an unsupported link.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension tests whether the framing, key definitions, and stated implications are intelligible and useful to readers from adjacent fields, and whether any claim of interdisciplinary reach is substantiated rather than asserted. Assessment belongs to the perspective role; I will not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This covers manuscript organisation, clarity and economy of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's formatting and structural conventions. Poor exposition can obscure methodological detail, but the scoring authority is editorial, so I withhold a score.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and consequential for that readership rather than restating settled ground. It is an editorial judgement about significance and fit, not a methods judgement, and I do not score it.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: An explicitly named design matched to the research question; specified population, sampling frame, recruitment route, and achieved sample with non-participation accounted for; documented instrumentation and data-collection protocol; a traceable analytic procedure (coding frame, analytic steps, or statistical model specification) linking raw material to each reported result; appropriate reporting of uncertainty, variability, missingness, or qualitative trustworthiness criteria such as coder agreement, member checking, audit trail, and negative-case handling; ethics approval and consent statement; and reproducibility affordances such as instrument appendices, coding schemes, or data/material availability statements.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing reported result cannot be traced to any described procedure, or the design is structurally incapable of supporting the manuscript's primary claim (for example inferential or comparative claims are reported with no specification of how cases were selected, how data were coded, or how the comparison was constructed), such that the finding cannot be evaluated or re-derived as printed.
what_triggers_warn: The core procedure is discernible and the primary claim is supportable in principle, but material rigor details are thin or missing in ways that weaken confidence without invalidating the result — for example unreported non-response or attrition, absent reliability or trustworthiness checks, an unavailable instrument or coding frame, missing ethics/consent statement, or no data-availability affordance.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence of a methodological defect that cannot be cured by revision of the existing dataset — for instance reported analyses that are internally impossible given the stated sample or design, results contradicted by the manuscript's own reported figures, undisclosed reuse or fabrication indicators, or a research procedure conducted without required ethical authorisation over human participants.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A single stated thesis that remains constant from abstract through conclusion; every substantive claim paired with an identifiable piece of the presented evidence; claim strength calibrated to design strength, with hedged language where the design permits only description or association; scope of conclusions confined to the actual study population and setting; explicit treatment of rival explanations and limitations; and internal consistency between the stated aims, the reported findings, and the recommendations or implications drawn.
what_triggers_block: The central conclusion rests on an inferential leap the presented evidence cannot bear — causal, prescriptive, or generalising claims asserted from a design that supports none of them — or the manuscript contradicts itself on a load-bearing point between sections, so that the main argument fails as stated rather than merely being under-qualified.
what_triggers_warn: The main argument survives scrutiny but contains repairable weaknesses — occasional over-reach in framing or abstract language relative to the findings, an unaddressed rival explanation, a limitations section that omits a limitation visible in the methods, or a secondary claim asserted without pointing to specific supporting evidence.
what_triggers_fatal: The thesis is incoherent at its foundation in a way no revision of argument can repair — for example the conclusion asserts the opposite of what the reported evidence shows, the argument is circular with the claim used as its own warrant, or the manuscript advances mutually exclusive core positions as simultaneously true.
