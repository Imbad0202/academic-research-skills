## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** As editor, I read this dimension as the demand that the study's design and evidentiary machinery survive scrutiny by a competent specialist in the field: that the sampling or case-selection logic is stated and defensible, that data collection and handling are described in enough detail to be audited, that any quantitative or qualitative analysis is reported with the conventional accompanying detail rather than bare conclusions, and that a reader could in principle re-run or re-trace the work. It is owned by the methodology reviewer and is not mine to score, but it constrains my editorial arithmetic because a fatal finding here forces rejection and a block forces major revision regardless of how well the manuscript reads.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript tells the truth about its own field: whether its factual assertions match the current state of evidence, whether prior scholarship is characterised as its authors would recognise rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and whether field-specific terminology, instruments, frameworks, and reported results are used correctly. The domain reviewer owns it. My editorial interest is that misrepresentation of prior work is the kind of defect that cannot be fixed by copy-editing and therefore propagates directly into the decision rule.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, shared between discourse-analysis and methodology roles).** Here the question is whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated claims actually follow from the evidence presented, whether the chain from data to interpretation to implication is free of gaps and equivocation, and whether there are reasoning defects severe enough that the main argument would collapse if named. This is not a stylistic judgement about persuasiveness; it is a structural judgement about entailment. Owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, with methodology eligible as a second scorer.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension tests whether a competent reader from a neighbouring field can enter the paper without insider tuition: whether framing and definitions are supplied rather than assumed, whether jargon is glossed at first use, and — critically — whether any claim the paper makes about relevance beyond its home discipline is actually backed by argument or evidence rather than merely asserted in a closing paragraph. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a block here forces major revision but cannot by itself trigger rejection. Owned by the perspective reviewer.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, mine to score).** This is my dimension. It covers whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can follow the argument's shape — sections that do the work their headings promise, a beginning that sets up what the middle delivers and the end resolves — together with clarity of prose at the sentence and paragraph level, the quality and self-sufficiency of any figures and tables, and conformity to the target venue's structural and citation conventions. It carries normal priority, meaning defects here are real and worth flagging but cannot alone escalate past minor revision unless they compound with problems elsewhere.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, mine to score).** Also mine, and this one carries mandatory weight. I must judge two joined questions: whether this manuscript belongs in this venue at all, given its scope, readership, and the kinds of work the venue publishes; and whether it advances knowledge enough to justify the space, which means asking what a reader knows after reading it that they did not know before, and whether that increment is genuinely new rather than a restatement of settled understanding. Because this dimension is mandatory, my finding here can drive the decision to major revision or, if the deficiency is fundamental and unfixable within the manuscript's design, to outright rejection.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Section architecture that matches the argument's logic, self-explanatory headings, prose clarity at paragraph and sentence level, figures and tables that stand alone with adequate captions and legible labelling, consistent and complete citation formatting, and adherence to the venue's structural conventions including abstract, keywords, and reference style.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition so deficient that the paper's contribution cannot be reliably extracted by an attentive reader — for example missing or empty core sections, figures or tables referenced but absent or uninterpretable, or prose ambiguity that makes central statements admit contradictory readings.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity or presentation defects that slow but do not defeat comprehension — uneven section proportions, undefined abbreviations, inconsistent citation or heading formatting, weak or absent captions, or a length that is visibly mismatched to the venue's expected article format.
### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of what is new, evidence that the claimed novelty is positioned against existing work rather than asserted in isolation, scope and readership alignment with the configured venue, an increment in knowledge proportionate to the space requested, and implications addressed to the audience the venue actually serves.
what_triggers_block: A substantive contribution shortfall or venue mismatch that is nonetheless remediable within the existing study — for example novelty asserted without positioning against prior work, findings presented without articulating what they add, or scope pitched to a neighbouring venue's readership and requiring reframing.
what_triggers_warn: Contribution present and venue-appropriate but under-argued or under-sold — a novelty claim that is real yet stated only implicitly, implications sketched too briefly for the readership, or a contribution whose significance is modest relative to the venue's typical bar but still defensible.
what_triggers_fatal: No identifiable original contribution at all, or a topic and treatment categorically outside the venue's remit, such that no revision short of conducting a different study or submitting elsewhere could establish fit and significance.
