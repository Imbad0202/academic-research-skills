## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** From an editorial standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's design and analytic machinery would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the manuscript's field: whether the sampling and data collection are described well enough to be judged, whether the handling of the data (cleaning, exclusions, missingness) is transparent, whether statistical procedures are appropriately chosen and completely reported, and whether a reader has enough material — code, data, or at minimum precise procedural detail — to attempt reproduction. It is owned by the methodology reviewer and is mandatory, meaning a fatal defect here alone drives the manuscript to rejection. I do not score it; my job as editor is to register how the methodology reviewer's verdict propagates through the failure conditions.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension governs factual fidelity to the substantive field: are the empirical claims consistent with what the current evidence base actually supports, is prior literature characterised faithfully rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and are field-specific terms, constructs, instruments, and reported results used correctly. Errors of this kind are not stylistic; they corrupt the manuscript's standing as a contribution to knowledge. Ownership sits with the domain reviewer, and because the dimension is mandatory, a fatal finding triggers rejection while a block-level finding forces major revision. It falls outside my scoring remit.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, devil's-advocate role with methodology eligibility).** Here the question is whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether stated conclusions actually follow from the evidence presented, whether the strength of the claims is calibrated to the strength of the support, and whether any reasoning defect — circularity, equivocation, causal overreach, unacknowledged confounds elevated to inference — undermines the spine of the argument. The devil's-advocate reviewer owns it and the methodology reviewer may also score it. As a mandatory dimension it carries the same escalation weight as D1 and D2. I do not score it.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension checks whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper: whether framing and definitions are supplied rather than assumed, whether jargon is glossed, whether the stated implications are meaningful beyond a narrow specialist audience, and — critically — whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually backed rather than asserted. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so it cannot on its own produce a rejection, but a block-level score here independently forces major revision under F4. The perspective reviewer owns it; I do not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role).** This is mine. It covers the manuscript as a piece of scholarly craft: whether the organisation supports the argument, whether exposition is clear and economical, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission observes the conventions of the target venue in structure, apparatus, and referencing. It is normal priority, so it has no fatal tier and cannot by itself force more than a minor revision under F5 — but a warn or worse here does put the manuscript into revision territory, so the judgement is not cosmetic.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role).** Also mine, and the heavier of my two. This dimension asks two linked questions: does this manuscript belong in this venue, addressing its readership on topics within its scope at the depth it expects; and does it advance something original and consequential rather than restating known results, reporting a bare descriptive exercise, or delivering an increment too small to justify the space. Because it is mandatory, my verdict here carries rejection power under F1 and major-revision power under F2, so I must hold the evidentiary bar high before assigning anything worse than pass and must reserve the fatal tier for genuinely irrecoverable mismatches.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the section architecture tracks the argument, whether methods and results are separable and locatable, whether prose is unambiguous at the sentence level, whether every figure and table is numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, and referenced in text, and whether reference formatting, abstract, and front/back matter follow standard venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken enough that a reader cannot reliably determine what was done or what was found — e.g. results reported without a corresponding methods description, tables or figures whose numbers cannot be reconciled with the text, or missing/unusable core apparatus such that substantive rewriting is required before assessment is possible.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity or presentation defects that do not obscure the study — awkward or redundant passages, an under-signposted section order, inconsistent terminology across sections, thin or poorly captioned exhibits, or reference/formatting irregularities that a copy-edit pass and modest restructuring would resolve.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit and defensible statement of what is new relative to existing work, evidence that the topic, framing, and depth match the configured venue's scope and readership, a contribution whose magnitude justifies publication space, and positioning that makes clear which audience gains what from the paper.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is materially under-argued or thin for the venue — the novelty claim is asserted rather than demonstrated against prior work, or the study reads as a single-site descriptive report whose stated significance outruns what it delivers — such that substantial reframing, added evidence, or repositioning is required, though a viable paper is recoverable.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are real but imperfectly established — the novelty statement is present yet vague, the intended readership is left implicit, or the implications section overclaims relative to the delivered evidence in ways that targeted revision to the framing and discussion would fix.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is categorically outside the configured venue's scope or offers no discernible original contribution at all — for example a restatement of already-established findings, or content whose subject matter and audience have no relationship to the venue — so that no revision within the paper's existing scope could make it publishable here.
