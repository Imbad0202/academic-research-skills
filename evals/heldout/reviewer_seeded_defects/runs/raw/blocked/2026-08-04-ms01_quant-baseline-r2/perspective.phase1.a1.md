## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility supports would survive scrutiny by specialists in the paper's own field. From where I sit as the cross-disciplinary reader, this matters because a method that cannot be reconstructed by an insider certainly cannot be evaluated or reused by someone from an adjacent discipline; still, ownership of this judgement sits with the methodology reviewer, and I will not score it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** Here the question is whether the manuscript's claims match the current state of evidence in its home domain, whether prior work is characterised fairly, and whether domain-specific terms and reported results are factually correct. My interest in this is indirect: misdescribed prior work or drifting terminology is exactly what misleads outside readers most efficiently, since they lack the local knowledge to detect it. The domain reviewer owns the call.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, da/methodology roles):** This concerns whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence presented actually bears on the claims made, and whether any reasoning defects undercut the main argument. Cross-disciplinary accessibility and argumentative soundness are adjacent but distinct: an argument can be perfectly legible to outsiders and still be invalid, or airtight yet impenetrable. The devil's-advocate and methodology roles adjudicate this one.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role — mine):** This is my dimension. It asks whether the framing, the operational definitions, and the stated implications can be understood and correctly applied by a competent reader from a neighbouring field, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home discipline is actually supported rather than merely asserted. Concretely I am asking: are constructs defined rather than assumed; is jargon either avoided or unpacked at first use; are the boundary conditions of the findings stated so an outsider knows what does and does not transfer; and when the paper claims relevance to other literatures, communities, or practice settings, does it supply evidence and citation rather than gesture? Overreach outward and opacity inward are the two failure modes I am hunting.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, eic role):** This covers organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's conventions. It overlaps with my remit at the edges, because poor structure and uninformative exhibits raise the entry cost for any non-specialist reader, but the formal judgement belongs to the editor-in-chief role and I will keep my comments on prose to whatever is strictly needed to explain a relevance finding.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, eic role):** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it offers an original and consequential contribution to that readership. A paper's breadth of appeal is related to but not the same as fit: a very accessible manuscript can be wrong for a venue, and a narrow one can be exactly right. The editor-in-chief owns this determination.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Explicit operational definitions of key constructs and outcome measures at first use; jargon and field-specific instruments glossed for adjacent-field readers; stated scope and boundary conditions telling an outsider what does and does not transfer; population, setting, and context described concretely enough to judge comparability; and every claim of relevance to another discipline, practice community, or literature backed by citation or presented evidence rather than assertion.
what_triggers_block: Core constructs or the outcome measure are never operationally defined, or the paper asserts substantive implications for other disciplines or for practice with no supporting evidence or citation, such that an adjacent-field reader cannot determine what was measured or what may legitimately be carried across.
what_triggers_warn: Definitions and framing are mostly recoverable but locally opaque — unglossed jargon, undefined instruments, missing scope or boundary conditions, or interdisciplinary implications stated more broadly than the presented evidence licenses — requiring guesswork from an outside reader without wholly blocking comprehension.
