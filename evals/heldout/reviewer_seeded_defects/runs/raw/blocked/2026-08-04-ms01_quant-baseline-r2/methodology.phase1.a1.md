## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** From a methods standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's inferential machinery can actually bear the weight of its conclusions. I read it as covering: whether the design (experimental, quasi-experimental, or observational) is stated explicitly and matches the causal or associational language used; whether the sampling frame, unit of analysis, and inclusion/exclusion rules are defined well enough that the population of inference is unambiguous; whether measures are operationalised with reported reliability or validity evidence rather than asserted; whether data handling (missingness, exclusions, transformations, outlier rules) is documented and not silently discretionary; whether statistical reporting includes the elements needed to evaluate a result rather than merely announce it (effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, model specification, assumption checks, degrees of freedom, and honest treatment of multiplicity); and whether reproducibility affordances exist at all — data availability, analysis code, instrument text, preregistration or its absence acknowledged, and ethics/consent approvals where human participants are involved. The bar is the field's own peer-review norm, not an idealised standard: I judge whether an independent methodologist in this field could, in principle, reconstruct and critique the analysis from what is written.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension concerns substantive correctness within the field: whether empirical claims are consistent with the current body of evidence, whether prior work is characterised faithfully rather than strawmanned or over-credited, and whether field-specific terminology, constructs, and reported quantities are used correctly. I do not score it; my methodological reading can surface facts that a domain specialist should verify, but the adjudication of domain truth is not mine and I will not encroach on it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer; I am also eligible).** From a methodology perspective this is the question of inferential validity in the narrative layer rather than the statistical layer: does the chain running from research question to design to results to conclusion hold without unsupported jumps? I look for consistency between the strength of the evidence produced and the strength of the claims made, for hypotheses that are not retrofitted to results, for absence of fallacies that would matter (post hoc causal attribution from correlational designs, confusing significance with importance, generalising beyond the sampled population, treating absence of evidence as evidence of absence, circular definitions), and for whether stated limitations are load-bearing corrections rather than decorative hedges that the abstract and conclusion then ignore.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This asks whether the framing, construct definitions, and stated implications can be understood and evaluated by readers in adjacent fields, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually supported rather than borrowed rhetorically. Not my dimension; methodological opacity can degrade accessibility, but I will report that as a methods observation rather than score it here.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** Organisation, expository clarity, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue formatting and reporting conventions. I do not score it, though I note that under-specified tables or figures without sample sizes and uncertainty are simultaneously a presentation issue and a methods issue, and I will raise the latter under D1 where warranted.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** Whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and offers an original, non-trivial advance for that readership. This is an editorial judgement of novelty and audience fit that I do not score; my only adjacent contribution is assessing whether the evidence base is strong enough for whatever contribution is claimed, which I handle under D1 and D3.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design statement and unit of analysis; sampling frame with inclusion/exclusion rules and final N with attrition accounting; operational definitions of key measures with reliability/validity evidence; complete model specification and covariate rationale; missing-data treatment; effect sizes with uncertainty intervals rather than bare p-values; assumption and robustness checks; multiplicity handling; confounding and selection-bias discussion appropriate to the design; ethics approval and consent; and reproducibility affordances such as data/code/instrument availability and any preregistration.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing inferential defect that invalidates the headline result as reported — for example causal or effectiveness conclusions drawn from a design with uncontrolled self-selection or no comparison condition, an analysis whose specification is undisclosed so the estimate cannot be interpreted, or outcome/sample definitions that shift between sections — where the fix requires new analysis or new data rather than new prose.
what_triggers_warn: Recoverable reporting and transparency gaps that leave the core inference plausible but under-verified — missing effect sizes or confidence intervals, unreported reliability for a constructed measure, silent handling of missing cases or exclusions, no assumption or sensitivity checks, absent data/code availability statement, or ethics approval not stated — all addressable by revising the manuscript with existing data.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported analysis cannot be trusted at all or was not performed as described — internally impossible statistics (numbers that contradict each other, percentages or degrees of freedom inconsistent with the stated N), results that cannot arise from the described procedure, apparent fabrication or duplication of data, or human-subjects work presented with no plausible ethical basis — such that no revision of the present dataset could rehabilitate the claims.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the research question, the design chosen to answer it, the results actually obtained, and the conclusions asserted form an unbroken chain; whether claim strength is calibrated to evidence strength across abstract, results, discussion, and conclusion; whether hypotheses appear specified rather than retrofitted; whether stated limitations genuinely constrain the conclusions instead of being asserted and then ignored; and whether alternative explanations for the observed pattern are engaged rather than dismissed.
what_triggers_block: The central thesis rests on an inferential leap the evidence cannot support and the leap is repeated in the abstract or conclusion — correlational findings narrated as causal effects, generalisation well beyond the sampled population or setting, a null result read as demonstrated equivalence, or a headline claim that a reader cannot trace back to any reported result.
what_triggers_warn: Localised overreach or internal friction that does not topple the thesis — occasional causal verbs in the discussion where the design licenses only association, a hedged limitation that the concluding paragraph quietly discounts, one unsupported implication or policy recommendation, or mismatched emphasis between what was measured and what is discussed.
what_triggers_fatal: The argument is self-defeating or non-recoverable — the stated conclusion directly contradicts the reported results, mutually exclusive claims are advanced as jointly true, or the thesis is unfalsifiable/circular by construction so that no revision short of a different study could make the paper's central assertion assessable.
