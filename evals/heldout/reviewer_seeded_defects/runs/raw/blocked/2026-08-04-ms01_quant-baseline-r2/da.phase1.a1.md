## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor.** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive a competent methodologist's scrutiny rather than merely appearing orderly. From an adversarial stance, the question I would press is not "did the authors describe a method?" but "does the described method license the inferences drawn from it, and could a skeptical reader reconstruct the analysis from what is on the page?" Ownership here sits with the methodology reviewer, so I am not scoring it; but I note that a design that cannot support causal or generalising language becomes my concern the moment such language appears in the argument. Silence about samples, attrition, model specification, or uncertainty is treated as an unmet burden of proof, not as a neutral omission.

**D2 — domain_accuracy.** This dimension demands that claims be consistent with current evidence in the manuscript's own field, that prior work be represented as it actually stands rather than as a convenient foil, and that field-specific terms and reported quantities be used correctly. The adversarial reading is that misrepresenting the literature is an argumentative move, not just a citation error: a strawman predecessor manufactures novelty that the manuscript has not earned. This dimension is owned by the domain reviewer and I will not score it, but any factual drift I encounter that props up the central thesis will be surfaced as a coherence liability rather than quietly deferred.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence.** This is my dimension. The contract requires that the core thesis be internally consistent, that the evidence actually presented supports the claims actually made, and that no fallacy undermines the central argument. Adversarially, I read the manuscript as a chain and hunt for the weakest link: definitions that shift between sections, conclusions whose modal strength exceeds what the evidence licenses, correlational findings narrated in causal grammar, selection or survivorship effects treated as results, abstract-versus-discussion mismatch, unstated auxiliary assumptions doing the real inferential work, and rival explanations that are named but never actually excluded. I also test the converse: whether the stated thesis is falsifiable at all, and whether any presented result could in principle have contradicted it. A manuscript passes only if I can restate its argument in premise-conclusion form and find the conclusion genuinely supported; it fails to the extent that the conclusion survives only because a gap has been papered over with hedging, rhetoric, or asymmetric emphasis.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance.** This dimension asks whether framing, definitions, and implications are intelligible and defensible to readers in adjacent fields, and whether interdisciplinary claims are substantiated rather than gestured at. The adversarial worry is borrowed authority: importing a construct or method from a neighbouring discipline and inheriting its prestige without inheriting its constraints. Ownership is with the perspective reviewer, so I do not score it; where an unsubstantiated cross-field claim is load-bearing for the central thesis, I treat it inside D3 as an unwarranted premise.

**D5 — writing_and_structure.** This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, figure and table quality, and conformity to venue conventions. Adversarially, prose problems matter to me only instrumentally: vagueness and disorganisation are frequently the mechanism by which an argumentative gap is concealed, and a figure that cannot be read independently of its caption's interpretation is a rhetorical device rather than evidence. The editor-in-chief role owns this dimension and I will not score it; I will only note presentational choices when they function as argument rather than as style.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution.** This dimension requires that the manuscript suit its configured venue and deliver an original, significant contribution to that readership. The adversarial pressure point is that "contribution" claims are argumentative claims with their own evidentiary burden: novelty asserted relative to an incompletely surveyed literature, or significance asserted from an effect too small or too fragile to matter, is a claim without support. This is owned by the editor-in-chief and I do not score it; I confine myself to whether the manuscript's own novelty and importance claims are internally consistent with the evidence it presents.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript's central thesis can be reconstructed as an explicit premise-conclusion chain in which stated evidence supports stated claims — checking definitional stability across abstract, methods, results, and discussion; alignment between the modal strength of conclusions and the inferential strength of the evidence; explicit treatment and exclusion of rival explanations, including selection, survivorship, confounding, and reverse-direction accounts; consistency between numbers or findings reported in different sections; and whether any presented result could in principle have falsified the thesis.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing inferential gap that the manuscript's own evidence cannot close — for example, the headline conclusion asserts or clearly implies a causal or generalising claim that the presented evidence can only support associationally, or an obvious rival explanation for the central finding is never addressed, or the abstract's claim materially exceeds what the results and discussion establish, such that the central argument fails as stated even though a narrower, honestly-scoped version could be recovered by substantial rewriting.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument survives scrutiny but shows localised coherence defects that do not by themselves overturn the thesis — for example, secondary claims overreach their evidence, hedging is applied inconsistently so that the same finding is cautious in one section and confident in another, a definition drifts in a non-load-bearing way, an alternative explanation is named but dismissed without argument, or emphasis is asymmetric between supportive and unsupportive results.
what_triggers_fatal: The thesis is incoherent or unfalsifiable at its core rather than merely under-supported — for example, the argument contains a contradiction between claims both of which are essential to it, the conclusion is presupposed by the analysis so no possible result could have contradicted it, the reported evidence points in the opposite direction from the stated conclusion, or the central claim is stated so that no evidence could bear on it, leaving nothing that revision of the argument could repair.
