## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor.** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive the ordinary scrutiny of a competent referee in this field. Read adversarially, my job is not to be satisfied that a method was named but to ask whether it was actually executed and reported in a way that a skeptic could check: are the units of analysis, sampling frame, exclusions, and analytic choices stated precisely enough that an independent party could re-run them and get the same numbers? Anything that reads as a methodological gesture — a design label without the accompanying parameters, an inference without its uncertainty, a dataset without its provenance — is a candidate defect rather than a stylistic preference. Ownership sits with the methodology reviewer, so I do not score it, but its condition constrains how much argumentative weight the paper's claims can legitimately bear.

**D2 — domain_accuracy.** Here the challenge is whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with what the domain's current evidence base actually supports, whether prior work is characterised as that work would recognise itself, and whether domain-specific terms and reported results are used correctly rather than loosely. An adversarial reading looks for straw-man renderings of predecessors, citations that are made to say more than they say, terminology drift where a technical construct is quietly redefined mid-paper, and numerical or definitional claims that contradict established findings without acknowledging the conflict. This is the domain reviewer's dimension; I do not score it, though any accuracy defect it surfaces is directly relevant to whether the paper's chain of reasoning still holds.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence.** This is my dimension. The question is whether the paper has one thesis rather than several incompatible ones, whether the evidence actually offered is the evidence the thesis requires, and whether the inferential steps from data to conclusion are valid rather than merely fluent. Adversarially, I will try to break the argument: I will look for the gap between what was measured and what is concluded, for correlational evidence recruited to support causal or prescriptive language, for conclusions in the abstract or discussion that the results section cannot underwrite, for premises that are asserted and then used as though established, for scope creep in which a narrow finding is generalised to populations, settings, or mechanisms never studied, and for internal contradictions between sections. Alternative explanations that the paper's own design cannot rule out are a central test: if a rival account fits the reported evidence at least as well and is not addressed, the argument is not coherent, it is merely unopposed. I will also check that hedging is load-bearing rather than decorative — a caveat buried in a limitations paragraph does not repair an overclaim made everywhere else.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance.** This dimension asks whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper, understand what is being claimed, and evaluate why it matters, and whether any claim to reach beyond the home discipline is actually substantiated rather than asserted. The adversarial posture is to distrust interdisciplinary framing that functions as marketing: does the paper genuinely engage the neighbouring literature it invokes, define its constructs in terms a non-specialist could operationalise, and state implications that follow from its evidence rather than from its ambition? This belongs to the perspective reviewer; I do not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure.** This concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's conventions. Read sceptically, poor structure is rarely only cosmetic: buried methods, results that appear first in the discussion, figures whose axes or denominators are unlabelled, and tables that do not reconcile with the text are often where substantive problems hide. The Editor-in-Chief role owns this dimension; I do not score it, but I treat presentational opacity as a reason to probe harder rather than to defer.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution.** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and significant for that readership rather than restating known results in a new setting. Adversarially, the test is what the field would lose if this paper did not exist: is there a delta over prior work that is stated explicitly and defended, or is novelty claimed only by the absence of an identical predecessor? Scale, single-site scope, and confirmatory findings are not automatically disqualifying, but they must be matched by correspondingly modest claims. This is the Editor-in-Chief's dimension; I do not score it.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A single explicit thesis whose supporting chain is traceable end to end — stated claim, the specific evidence adduced for it, the inferential warrant linking them, the named rival explanations the design can and cannot exclude, and consistent claim strength across abstract, results, discussion, and conclusion, with hedges that actually constrain the conclusions rather than sitting decoratively in a limitations paragraph.
what_triggers_block: The central conclusion does not follow from the reported evidence — for example, causal, mechanistic, or prescriptive conclusions are drawn from evidence that cannot license them, a plainly available alternative explanation that fits the data equally well is never addressed, the abstract or conclusion asserts materially more than the results section supports, or the paper's own sections contradict one another on a load-bearing claim.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument holds but has repairable weaknesses — undefended premises used as established, generalisation modestly beyond the studied population or setting, an alternative explanation acknowledged but dismissed without argument, unevenly calibrated claim strength across sections, or a thesis that must be reconstructed by the reader because it is never stated in one place.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript has no recoverable argument to evaluate — the stated thesis is incoherent or shifts to an incompatible claim mid-paper, the conclusions are asserted with no evidentiary chain offered at all, or the reasoning is circular or self-refuting such that no revision short of a different paper could repair it.
