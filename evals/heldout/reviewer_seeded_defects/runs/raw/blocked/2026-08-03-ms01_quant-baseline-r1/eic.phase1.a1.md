## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of data would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in this field. That covers how the sample was obtained and bounded, whether the analytic approach matches the questions asked, whether statistics are reported completely enough for a reader to judge them rather than merely trust them, and whether enough procedural detail exists that another team could attempt the same analysis. It is owned by the methodology reviewer, and because it is mandatory, a serious defect here can by itself determine the manuscript's fate regardless of how attractive the topic is.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** This is the substantive-truth check. The domain reviewer is asked whether what the manuscript asserts about its subject matter is consistent with what the field currently knows, whether the literature it invokes is described faithfully rather than conveniently, and whether technical vocabulary, instruments, measures, and reported quantities are used correctly. Misattribution of prior findings, stale or superseded evidence presented as current, and terminological slippage all live here. Like D1, it is mandatory, so an uncorrected factual error of consequence carries decision-level weight.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, DA role with methodology as a second eligible voice):** Independently of whether the methods are sound and the facts are right, the manuscript has to hold together as an argument. The question is whether the central thesis stays the same thesis from introduction to conclusion, whether the evidence actually presented bears the weight the conclusions place on it, and whether any inferential step commits an error — overreach from association to causation, equivocation, selective reading of one's own results — that would collapse the main claim. The discourse-analysis reviewer owns it; it is mandatory.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role):** This dimension protects the readership beyond the authors' immediate specialty. The perspective reviewer judges whether framing and definitions let an intelligent reader from an adjacent field follow the work without insider knowledge, and — more demandingly — whether any claim the manuscript makes about relevance to other disciplines is actually supported rather than merely asserted in a closing paragraph. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a block here escalates the decision but cannot by itself trigger rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role, mine to score):** This is the craft-and-presentation dimension I own directly. It asks whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can find the argument, whether exposition is clear rather than merely present, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission observes the conventions the venue expects of its authors. Its normal priority means problems here shape revision requests rather than acceptance in principle, but chronic opacity can still make a manuscript unpublishable in its current form.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role, mine to score):** The second dimension I own is the gatekeeping judgement proper: does this manuscript belong here, and does it add anything. Fit means the topic, method, and register match what the configured venue publishes and what its readers come for. Contribution means the work advances beyond what is already available — not necessarily dramatically, but discernibly and in a way the manuscript itself makes explicit and defensible. Because this is mandatory, a manuscript that is merely competent but adds nothing, or that is solid but plainly addressed to a different community, faces a decision-level consequence.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A discoverable structure with a stated purpose, a section order that tracks the argument, self-contained and correctly labelled figures/tables whose captions and axes match the text, defined terms and abbreviations at first use, prose that states rather than gestures, and conformance to venue conventions for length, sectioning, reference formatting, and reporting of tables and statistics.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken badly enough that a competent reader cannot reliably reconstruct what was done or what is claimed — for example missing or unnumbered results reporting, figures/tables that contradict or are unsupported by the text, absent method or results sections, or pervasive ambiguity that would require rewriting rather than editing.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but presentation degrades it — unlabelled or under-captioned exhibits, undefined jargon, misordered or padded sections, redundancy between narrative and tables, inconsistent citation or reference formatting, or a length/format mismatch with venue conventions that copyediting alone would not resolve.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of what is new and for whom, positioned against the nearest existing work; a topic, method, and register that match what the configured venue publishes; implications addressed to that venue's actual readership; and a contribution whose claimed significance is proportionate to the evidence and scope of the study rather than inflated in the framing.
what_triggers_block: The contribution as presented is not discernibly additive — the manuscript restates known results, reports a single local deployment with no generalisable insight or transferable design lesson, or fails to position itself against prior work at all — or the topic and treatment are plainly aimed at a different community than the configured venue's readership.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and novelty are present but underargued — the "what is new" claim is implicit, the positioning against nearest prior work is thin or one-sided, the significance asserted outruns the study's scope, or the implications section speaks past the venue's readership while the core material still belongs there.
what_triggers_fatal: The submission is disqualifying on its face for this venue — it is out of scope in kind rather than degree, duplicates or substantially overlaps previously published work by the same authors without disclosure, or presents no identifiable original contribution whatsoever such that no revision short of a different study could remedy it.
