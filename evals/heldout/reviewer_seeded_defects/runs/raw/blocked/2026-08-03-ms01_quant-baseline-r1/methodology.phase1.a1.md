## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** From a methodology standpoint this dimension asks whether the study's inferential machinery can actually bear the weight of its conclusions: whether the design is stated explicitly enough to be classified (experimental, quasi-experimental, observational), whether the sampling frame, unit of analysis, and outcome definition are unambiguous, whether data provenance and exclusions are documented, whether the statistical models match the data structure and the reported estimates carry uncertainty, and whether enough procedural detail exists for an independent analyst to attempt reproduction. The bar is the field's ordinary peer-review bar, not perfection — but design–inference mismatches and undocumented analytic choices are the core failure modes I am charged with detecting.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension covers whether substantive claims are consistent with the current evidence base in the field, whether prior literature is represented faithfully rather than strawmanned or over-credited, and whether domain terminology and reported results are factually correct. I am not scoring this dimension; my only relationship to it is that a methodological artefact and a domain factual error can look similar on the surface, so I should keep the boundary clean and leave substantive domain adjudication to its owner.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, methodology eligible).** Here the question is whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence presented actually licenses the claims made, and whether any reasoning defects — equivocation, circularity, unwarranted generalisation, or a leap from association to mechanism — undercut the argument. My angle on this is narrower than the owner's: I judge coherence as the fit between what the design and analysis can support and what the manuscript asserts, i.e. whether inferential scope is honoured in the framing, abstract, and conclusions.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension concerns whether framing, definitions, and stated implications are legible and useful to readers in adjacent fields, and whether any interdisciplinary claim is backed rather than asserted. I do not score it and will not encroach on it; if I notice methodological jargon that impedes comprehension, that observation belongs to its owner, not to my scoring lines.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This covers manuscript organisation, expositional clarity, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue conventions. It is not mine to score. Methodology sometimes shares a border with it — an under-labelled table can be either a presentation defect or a reporting defect — and where that happens I will confine myself to the reporting-completeness reading that falls under D1.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it offers an original and significant contribution for that readership. I do not score it. Whether a contribution is *sound* is my business under D1; whether a sound contribution is *sufficient and appropriately placed* is the editor's, and I will not substitute my judgement for theirs.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design statement and unit of analysis; sampling frame, cohort size, attrition and exclusion accounting; operational definitions of the predictor and outcome measures including how each was instrumented and over what window; confounder handling and any selection-bias mitigation; model specification matched to the data structure with effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, and exact test statistics rather than bare significance labels; treatment of missing data and multiple comparisons; ethics/consent and data-access statements; and enough procedural specificity (instruments, code, derived-variable rules, software) for an independent analyst to attempt reproduction.
what_triggers_block: A core inferential claim rests on an analysis whose specification, sample, or outcome definition is not recoverable from the text — for example no reported denominators or comparison basis, no uncertainty estimates for the headline effect, or an outcome variable never operationally defined — so the reported result cannot be evaluated or reconstructed even in principle.
what_triggers_warn: The design and analysis are recoverable and broadly appropriate, but rigor is thinned by identifiable, fixable gaps: incomplete attrition or exclusion accounting, confounders acknowledged but unmodelled, missing-data handling unstated, effect sizes reported without intervals, no correction or acknowledgement for multiple testing, or reproducibility affordances (instrument details, code/data availability) absent while the substantive analysis remains legible.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence of a validity-destroying defect that no revision of the present dataset can repair — such as an outcome measure that is definitionally contained in or computed from the predictor, an analysis conducted on a sample selected on the outcome, or internally contradictory reported numbers indicating the analysis as described could not have produced the stated results.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the thesis asserted in title, abstract, and conclusions is the same thesis the design and analysis can support; whether each substantive claim is traceable to a specific reported result; whether correlational evidence is described in correlational language and any causal, mechanistic, or policy claim is separately licensed; whether hedges introduced in the results survive into the discussion; whether limitations named are actually carried through to constrain the stated implications; and whether generalisation targets (population, setting, platform, time horizon) are consistent across sections rather than silently widening.
what_triggers_block: The central argument depends on an inferential step the evidence does not license and that appears in the paper's own headline framing — for instance an observational association presented as a causal or effect-producing relationship, or conclusions and recommendations addressed to a population or setting materially broader than the one studied, such that the thesis as stated is not the thesis demonstrated.
what_triggers_warn: The overall argument holds, but there are localised coherence slips that a revision can tighten: occasional causal verb choice in otherwise correlational discussion, a limitation acknowledged in one section yet not reflected in the implications, an unsupported ancillary claim not load-bearing for the thesis, or asymmetric emphasis on confirming results while discordant or null findings are mentioned but not reconciled.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript contains mutually incompatible core claims — a stated conclusion that directly contradicts its own reported evidence, or a thesis whose supporting reasoning is circular or presupposes what it purports to establish — so that no version of the argument built on the presented evidence can be made coherent.
