## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role owns).** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of data would survive scrutiny by a competent specialist referee in the manuscript's own field. That covers how the sample or corpus was assembled, whether the analytic procedure actually answers the stated question, whether statistics or qualitative coding are reported with enough detail to be checked, and whether another researcher could in principle repeat the work from what is written. It is mandatory, so a fatal defect here is decisive for the whole submission regardless of how attractive the topic is. My oversight role is to respect the methodology reviewer's verdict on this rather than substitute my own reading.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role owns).** This dimension governs factual fidelity to the field: whether the substantive claims are consistent with what the current evidence base supports, whether cited or paraphrased prior work is represented as its authors would recognise, and whether field-specific vocabulary, instruments, frameworks, and reported results are used correctly rather than loosely. Misattribution, invented findings, or terminology used in a sense the field does not accept all fall here. Because it is mandatory, an uncorrectable accuracy failure ends the manuscript's path to publication.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, discourse-analysis role owns, methodology may also score).** Here the question is whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together on its own terms: does the chain from premises through evidence to conclusion actually connect, do the stated findings support the strength of the claims made from them, and are there reasoning defects — overgeneralisation, circularity, conflation of correlation with cause, unstated leaps — serious enough to hollow out the main argument. This is distinct from whether the methods were sound and from whether the facts were right; a technically clean study can still be argued badly. Mandatory status means a collapsed central argument is disqualifying.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role owns).** This dimension checks whether a reader from a neighbouring discipline can enter the paper: are the framing and motivation intelligible outside the author's immediate specialism, are key constructs defined rather than assumed, and are the implications drawn for other fields actually supported rather than asserted as decoration. It is high priority but not mandatory, so a blocking judgement here drives revision rather than outright rejection under the configured failure conditions. I read the perspective reviewer's finding as the authority on this axis.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, my own dimension).** This is squarely editorial: whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow it, whether sections do the work their headings promise, whether exposition is clear at sentence and paragraph level, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than ornamental, and whether the submission observes the venue's conventions on structure, length, referencing, and apparatus. Normal priority means defects here are ordinarily fixable in revision; my task is to record their severity honestly rather than either indulging poor craft or inflating stylistic taste into a substantive objection.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, my own dimension).** This is the gatekeeping judgement: does this manuscript belong in this venue, addressed to this readership, and does it add something that the readership does not already have. Fit concerns scope, genre, and audience expectations. Contribution concerns originality and significance — whether the work advances knowledge, method, or practice enough to justify the space it asks for, or merely restates what is already established. Because this dimension is mandatory, a fatal failure here is grounds for rejection even if every other dimension is clean, so I must apply it with a clear evidentiary basis rather than on impression.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A discernible and appropriate section architecture; a title, abstract, and introduction that state the question and the claim; prose that is unambiguous and free of undefined shorthand; figures and tables that are numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, and referenced in text; consistent and complete referencing apparatus; and length, formatting, and conventions matching what the configured venue expects for this genre of submission.
what_to_look_for_note_unused: none
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is defective enough that a competent reader cannot reliably determine what was done or what is being claimed without guessing — for example missing or non-functional core sections, figures or tables presented without interpretable labels or data, or a citation apparatus so incomplete that claims cannot be traced.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but its craft falls below venue standard in localised, repairable ways — redundant or misordered passages, an abstract that omits method or findings, undefined abbreviations, inconsistent reference formatting, figures that duplicate rather than extend the text, or drift from venue conventions on headings and length.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of what is new and why it matters, positioned against the existing literature so the increment is visible; a topic, method, and register that match the venue's stated scope and its readership's interests; scale of evidence and depth of treatment proportionate to the claims and to the space requested; and implications addressed to the audience this venue actually serves rather than to a generic reader.
what_triggers_block: The contribution as presented is too thin or too weakly differentiated from established work to warrant publication in this venue at this length — for instance a descriptive report with no articulated advance, or an evidence base far too slight to support the significance claimed — such that substantial additional work, not editing, would be needed.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are real but under-argued or partially misaligned — the novelty claim is implied rather than stated, positioning against prior work is sketchy, the framing addresses a different audience than the venue's, or the significance asserted outruns what the presented evidence carries, all correctable by rewriting and sharper positioning.
what_triggers_fatal: The submission does not belong in this venue at all or offers no genuine contribution to publish — wholly outside the venue's scope or genre, or duplicative of already-published work including the authors' own, or so devoid of new content that no amount of revision within this manuscript could produce a publishable increment.
