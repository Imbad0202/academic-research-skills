## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** From a methods standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's inferential machinery actually holds together: whether the design chosen is capable of answering the stated research question, whether sampling and recruitment are described well enough to judge who the findings apply to, whether data collection instruments and analytic procedures are specified rather than gestured at, whether whatever statistical or qualitative-analytic reporting appears is complete and internally consistent, and whether an independent researcher could plausibly repeat the work from what is written. It is the dimension where I am obliged to be least charitable, because everything downstream — the domain claims, the argument, the contribution — inherits whatever defects live in the design and the data handling.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain reviewer's ownership).** This dimension governs whether the manuscript's substantive assertions are correct against the current state of evidence in its field, whether the literature it cites is represented faithfully rather than conveniently, and whether field-specific terminology, instruments, frameworks, and reported prior results are used accurately. I do not score it; my only legitimate contact with it is that a methodological defect can occasionally be the mechanism by which a domain error arises, and in that case I report the defect on my own dimension rather than trespassing on this one.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, discourse-analysis reviewer owns, I am eligible).** Here the question is whether the central thesis is stable across the manuscript, whether the evidence actually adduced supports the strength and scope of the claims made from it, and whether any reasoning steps are fallacious or non-sequitur in ways that would collapse the paper's core argument. My angle on this is specifically the evidence-to-claim link: whether inferences drawn are licensed by the design and data the authors possess, whether causal language rests on a design capable of supporting causation, whether generalisation exceeds the sampling frame, and whether limitations acknowledged in one section are quietly forgotten when conclusions are stated.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective reviewer's ownership).** This concerns whether an intelligent reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper: whether framing, key definitions, and stated implications are intelligible outside the immediate specialty, and whether claims that reach across disciplinary boundaries are actually substantiated rather than asserted for reach. Not mine to score.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, editor-in-chief's ownership).** This covers manuscript organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's conventions of presentation. Not mine to score, though I note that unreadable methods reporting registers on my dimension as an inability to verify, independently of prose quality.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, editor-in-chief's ownership).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it contributes something original and consequential for that readership. Not mine to score; methodological adequacy is a necessary but not sufficient input to that editorial judgement.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: An explicitly stated design matched to the research question; sampling frame, recruitment route, eligibility criteria, sample size and non-response or attrition accounting; instrument provenance and full wording or availability; a specified analysis procedure (coding scheme, inter-coder agreement, or model specification and assumption checks as applicable); complete reporting of estimates with uncertainty and exact tests rather than bare significance verdicts; ethics approval and consent handling; and concrete reproducibility affordances such as data, instrument, or codebook availability statements.
what_triggers_block: Core analytic procedure is unreportable from the text — e.g. the sample and its selection cannot be reconstructed, the analysis method is named only in the abstract with no operational detail, or reported results are numerically inconsistent or unsupported by any described procedure — such that the stated findings cannot be verified or repeated in principle.
what_triggers_warn: Design and analysis are recoverable and defensible in outline, but specific rigor affordances are thin or missing — e.g. absent effect sizes or uncertainty intervals, no inter-coder reliability for qualitative coding, unaddressed selection or non-response bias, missing-data handling unstated, or no data/instrument availability statement — leaving conclusions weaker than claimed but repairable in revision.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported empirical basis does not exist or cannot have produced the stated results — for instance results presented with no corresponding data collection, an analysis logically impossible given the described sample, absent required ethics clearance for identifiable human-subject data, or reporting internally contradictory to the point of indicating fabrication — a defect no revision could repair.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each substantive conclusion is traceable to a specific piece of the paper's own evidence at the strength claimed; whether causal, predictive, or prescriptive language is licensed by the design actually used; whether generalisation stays inside the sampling frame and study context; whether the thesis stated in the introduction is the one defended in the discussion; and whether acknowledged limitations are honoured in the concluding claims rather than dropped.
what_triggers_block: The paper's central conclusion is not entailed by its own evidence — causal or effectiveness claims asserted from a design incapable of supporting them, generalisation to populations or settings never sampled, or a conclusion that contradicts the results section — such that the main argument fails on the authors' own materials.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument survives but individual inferential steps overreach — hedged findings restated as settled in the abstract or implications, a secondary claim resting on evidence not presented, or limitations acknowledged then ignored in one or two conclusions — fixable by rescoping the claims.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's core thesis is incoherent or self-refuting at the level of its own logic — mutually exclusive positions maintained as jointly true, or the argument's key premise circularly derived from the conclusion it is meant to establish — so that no revision short of a different paper could rescue the argument.
