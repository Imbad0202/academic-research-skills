## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its handling of data would survive an informed attempt to break it. Adversarially, I would ask: does the design actually license the inferences drawn, or does it rest on a convenience sample, an uncontrolled comparison, or an outcome measured after the fact? Are the statistical procedures reported completely enough that a hostile reader could recompute them — effect sizes, uncertainty, sample attrition, model specification, handling of missing data — or is the reporting selective in ways that flatter the conclusion? Are reproducibility affordances (data availability, code, instrument details, preregistration or its absence) present, or must the reader take the analysis on trust? A manuscript passes only if the method is strong enough that the burden of doubt shifts to the challenger.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** Here the challenge is whether the manuscript's factual and terminological claims hold against what the field already knows. An adversarial reading looks for prior work that is misdescribed, cited in support of something it did not find, or omitted precisely where it would complicate the argument; for domain terms used loosely or in a sense the field does not accept; and for empirical assertions presented as settled when the evidence base is contested. Novelty claims are a particular target: asserting that something is new is an implicit claim about the literature, and a false one is a domain error, not a rhetorical flourish.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by devil's advocate):** This is my dimension. The question is not whether the conclusions are appealing but whether the chain from premise to claim actually closes. I will test whether the central thesis is stated with enough precision to be wrong; whether each load-bearing claim is supported by evidence the manuscript actually presents rather than by assertion, citation-by-gesture, or appeal to plausibility; whether the strength of the conclusion is calibrated to the strength of the support; and whether any inferential move commits a fallacy that the argument cannot survive — treating association as causation, confusing a selected subgroup for the population, hidden circularity, equivocation on a key term across sections, or an unfalsifiable framing that absorbs any result. I will also check internal consistency: whether the abstract, results, and discussion make compatible claims, and whether limitations are acknowledged in the text and then quietly abandoned when conclusions are drawn.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, perspective role):** The adversarial question is whether a competent reader from a neighbouring field can follow the argument without reconstructing missing definitions, and whether claims that reach across disciplinary boundaries are earned rather than borrowed. Framing that assumes in-group vocabulary, implications asserted for domains the study did not sample, or generalisations stated at a scope wider than the design supports would all be problems under this dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, EiC role):** The challenge is whether the manuscript's organisation helps or hinders verification. Sections that bury the design, results that appear only in prose, figures or tables that cannot be read independently of a favourable narrative, inconsistent numbers between text and exhibits, or departures from venue conventions all raise the cost of scrutiny and count against the paper here.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role):** This dimension resists the assumption that a competent study is automatically publishable in this venue. An adversarial reading asks whether the contribution is genuinely additive relative to existing work or merely a local replication reported as discovery; whether its significance is argued or merely claimed; and whether its scope, length, and audience match what the configured venue and its readership expect.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A precisely stated central thesis; explicit identification of each load-bearing claim and the specific evidence offered for it; calibration between conclusion strength and evidential strength; consistency of the thesis across abstract, results, discussion, and any stated limitations; and absence of inferential fallacies (causal leaps from associational designs, selection artefacts treated as population facts, equivocation on key constructs, circular support, unfalsifiable framing).
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing claim on which the paper's headline conclusion depends is unsupported by any evidence the manuscript presents, or rests on an inferential fallacy that cannot be repaired without collecting new data or retracting the conclusion.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument survives but is overstated at the margins — hedged findings restated as firm ones in abstract or discussion, a secondary claim asserted without support, an ambiguous key term, or acknowledged limitations that are not carried through into the stated conclusions.
what_triggers_fatal: No coherent falsifiable thesis can be identified at all, or the manuscript's own reported results contradict its stated conclusion and the contradiction is left unaddressed, so that the central argument fails irrecoverably as written.
