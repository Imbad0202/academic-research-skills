## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** As editor, I read this dimension as the demand that the study's design and its evidentiary machinery would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the manuscript's own field. That covers how the sample or corpus was constituted, how data were collected and cleaned, whether the analytic procedures match the stated question, whether statistical reporting is complete enough to be checked rather than merely believed, and whether a reader is given the materials, code, or procedural detail needed to attempt reproduction. Ownership sits with the methodology reviewer, and because it is mandatory, deficiencies here can drive the whole editorial outcome rather than being absorbed as revision notes.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** This is the substantive-correctness check. The domain specialist is asked to confirm that the manuscript's assertions are consistent with the current state of evidence in the field, that prior literature is characterised fairly rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and that specialised terminology, constructs, instruments, and reported results are used in their accepted senses. Misstatements about what earlier work found, or misuse of field-specific vocabulary, count against this dimension even when the paper's own analysis is internally tidy. Its mandatory status means factual error in the domain is not a cosmetic matter.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with methodology as a second eligible role):** Here the question is whether the paper's central argument actually holds together once assembled. The chain from stated question through evidence to conclusion must be traceable, the strength of the conclusion must not exceed the strength of what was shown, and there must be no reasoning defects — circularity, equivocation, causal overreach, selective appeal to convenient evidence — that would collapse the thesis if pressed. Coherence is separable from both method and domain fact: a technically clean study can still argue for something its data do not license.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role):** This dimension asks whether a well-informed reader from a neighbouring field can enter the paper without a private glossary. Framing should make the problem legible outside the immediate specialty, key definitions should be stated rather than presumed, and implications drawn for other disciplines should be backed by argument rather than asserted as rhetorical reach. It is high priority but not mandatory, so a failure here escalates the decision without being able to trigger outright rejection on its own.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role, mine to score):** This is the presentational dimension I own directly. It concerns whether the manuscript is organised so that its sections do the work their headings promise, whether exposition is clear enough that meaning does not depend on charitable reading, whether figures and tables are self-supporting, adequately labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission observes the venue's conventions for structure, length, referencing, and reporting. It carries normal priority: problems here shape revision requirements rather than acceptance in principle.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role, mine to score):** This is the gatekeeping judgement proper. I must decide whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue at all — whether its topic, scope, and intended readership match what the venue publishes — and whether it advances something original and consequential enough to justify the space it asks for. A competent but derivative report, or a solid piece aimed at the wrong readership, fails this dimension. Because it is mandatory, a fatal judgement here is sufficient on its own to end the manuscript's path at this venue.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether sections follow a logical and conventional arc with each doing the work its heading promises; whether prose is unambiguous without charitable reading; whether figures and tables are labelled, captioned, self-contained, and actually necessary; whether length, referencing, and reporting format match venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is defective enough that the paper's procedure or results cannot be reconstructed by a careful reader — for example, results presented without identifiable methods, figures or tables that are unlabelled or contradict the text, or missing sections the venue requires.
what_triggers_warn: The paper is followable end to end but presentation degrades it — redundant or misordered passages, vague or overloaded sentences at key claims, under-captioned or low-quality exhibits, inconsistent citation or reporting formatting.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, framing, and target readership match the configured venue's scope; whether the paper states a clear claim to novelty; whether that novelty is substantiated against existing work rather than asserted; whether the contribution's significance is proportionate to the venue's standards and to the manuscript's length.
what_triggers_block: A contribution claim that is real but substantially under-substantiated or overstated relative to what is delivered — thin novelty positioning, missing comparison to the nearest prior work, or scope that only partially matches the venue's readership and would need repositioning.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are adequate but imprecisely argued — the significance statement is generic, the intended readership is left implicit, or the relation to adjacent published work is gestured at rather than specified.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is out of scope for the configured venue, or offers no discernible original contribution — restating known results, reporting a routine exercise with no advance, or duplicating prior work without acknowledged increment — such that no revision within the current scope could make it publishable here.
