## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by me).** This dimension asks whether the study's inferential machinery is trustworthy: whether the design can actually support the causal or associational claim being made, whether sampling, assignment, and attrition are documented well enough to judge bias, whether measures are defined operationally and consistently, whether the statistical reporting includes the quantities needed to evaluate it (effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, denominators, model specifications, handling of missing data and clustering), and whether an independent reader could in principle reproduce the analysis from the described data, code, and procedures. From a rigor standpoint I treat unstated design choices as defects, not as neutral omissions, because an undocumented choice cannot be audited.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension governs whether the substantive content is correct against the current state of knowledge in the field: whether cited prior work is characterised accurately rather than conveniently, whether technical vocabulary is used the way the field uses it, and whether reported quantities and interpretations of others' results are factually right. It is a truth-of-content check rather than a validity-of-inference check, and it is not mine to score.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer; I am also eligible).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated conclusion is the conclusion the presented evidence actually licenses, whether each intermediate step follows from the previous one, whether scope conditions stated early are respected later, and whether the argument avoids fallacious moves — most pertinently for me, sliding from association to causation, generalising beyond the sampled population, treating absence of significance as evidence of absence, or reframing a secondary finding as the headline claim. My reading of this dimension is deliberately narrow and evidentiary: I evaluate whether claim strength matches evidence strength, and I leave rhetorical and framing analysis to the owner role.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension concerns whether a competent reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper: whether constructs are defined rather than assumed, whether jargon is glossed, whether the stated implications travel beyond the immediate subfield, and whether any claim of interdisciplinary reach is backed by argument rather than asserted. Not mine to score.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension covers craft and presentation: logical section ordering, clarity of prose, whether figures and tables are self-explanatory and correctly labelled, and conformity to the venue's formatting and reporting conventions. Not mine to score.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge enough to warrant the space: originality relative to existing literature, significance for the venue's readership, and appropriateness of scope and depth. Not mine to score.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design type and unit of analysis; population definition, sampling frame, and enrolment/attrition accounting; operational definitions and measurement windows for the exposure and outcome variables; identification strategy including how self-selection into the exposure is addressed; full statistical reporting (model specification, denominators, effect sizes with uncertainty intervals, treatment of missing data and of nesting within courses/instructors); pre-specification versus post-hoc status of analyses; and reproducibility affordances such as data availability, code availability, instrument text, and ethics/consent documentation.
what_triggers_block: A design or analysis defect that invalidates the reported inference as stated and cannot be repaired by clarification — for example an observational exposure compared without any confounding adjustment while the paper reports a causal effect, an outcome measured in a way that mechanically includes the predictor, undisclosed exclusion of cases that changes the analysed sample, or statistical results reported without the quantities needed to evaluate them at all.
what_triggers_warn: The core inference remains defensible but specific rigor affordances are missing or thin — for example effect sizes reported without confidence intervals, clustering or repeated measures acknowledged but not modelled, attrition reported without comparing leavers to stayers, no data or code availability statement, or measurement instruments described only in summary form.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported analysis could not have produced the reported numbers, or that the empirical basis is not what the paper claims — internally contradictory sample sizes or degrees of freedom that no reading reconciles, statistics that are arithmetically impossible given the stated design, an analysis presented as confirmatory that the text elsewhere reveals to be selected from many unreported alternatives, or human-subjects data used with no ethics approval or consent basis stated.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the abstract, results, discussion, and conclusion assert the same claim at the same strength; whether the language of the conclusion (causal, predictive, or associational) matches what the design and estimates support; whether stated limitations are carried into the conclusions rather than quarantined in a single paragraph; whether the generalisation target matches the sampled population; and whether alternative explanations for the observed pattern are named and addressed with evidence rather than dismissed.
what_triggers_block: The central thesis depends on an inferential leap the presented evidence does not license and that no reordering of the existing material would repair — for example a headline causal or policy recommendation resting on an unadjusted association, or a conclusion that directly contradicts the paper's own reported estimates or acknowledged limitations.
what_triggers_warn: The thesis survives but is overstated or unevenly supported in places — hedged in the results and unhedged in the abstract, generalised somewhat past the sampled cohort, or resting on a plausible but unexamined alternative explanation that is mentioned without being addressed.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript contains mutually exclusive core claims presented simultaneously as findings, such that no consistent reading of the thesis exists, or the stated conclusion is asserted about a quantity the study never measured.
