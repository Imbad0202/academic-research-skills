## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my dimension).** From a methodology standpoint this asks whether the study's design can actually bear the weight of the inferences drawn from it: whether the sampling frame, unit of analysis, comparison structure, and outcome definition are stated precisely enough for another researcher to reconstruct them; whether data provenance, exclusions, and missingness are documented rather than assumed away; whether the statistical treatment reports effect magnitudes with uncertainty rather than bare significance verdicts, and whether the model assumptions and identification logic are made explicit; and whether reproducibility affordances (analysis code, data availability statement, instrument details, ethics/consent provenance) meet the ordinary peer-review bar for the field rather than an aspirational one. My yardstick is not novelty but auditability: could a competent reader retrace the path from raw records to reported numbers and arrive at the same place.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension governs whether substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence in the field, whether prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than strategically, and whether field-specific terminology, constructs, and reported prior results are used correctly. I do not score it. Methodologically I note only that domain accuracy and methodological rigor can fail independently — a technically clean analysis can misattribute prior findings, and a well-read paper can be analytically unsound — so I will not treat my own findings as evidence about this dimension.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, methodology eligible).** Here the question is whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together internally: whether each headline claim is actually entailed by the evidence presented, whether the strength of language matches the strength of the design, and whether there are inferential leaps — most importantly for me, causal or policy claims resting on associational or observational evidence, selection or survivorship reasoning presented as effect, or conclusions that quietly widen beyond the population and time window studied. I assess it strictly through the evidence-to-claim link, leaving rhetorical structure and framing to the owning role.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This concerns whether framing, definitions, and stated implications are intelligible and useful to readers in adjacent fields, and whether any claim of interdisciplinary reach is substantiated rather than asserted. It is outside my dispatch and I will not score it; if I encounter definitional ambiguity that also degrades measurement validity, I will record that under my own dimensions rather than importing it here.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This covers organisation, clarity of exposition, figure and table craft, and conformity to venue conventions. I do not score it. Where a table or figure omits information required to verify a reported quantity, I treat that as a reporting-completeness issue inside methodology rigor rather than a presentation issue, and I will say so explicitly to avoid double-counting.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether its contribution is original and significant for that readership. Not my dimension, and not something I will infer from methodological findings: the sufficiency of a design for a claim is a separate judgement from whether the claim would matter to this audience.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design type and unit of analysis; sampling frame and cohort construction with inclusion/exclusion counts; operational definitions of the exposure/engagement measure and the retention outcome including measurement window; documented handling of missing data, attrition, and confounders; statistical models with stated assumptions, effect estimates, uncertainty intervals, and any multiplicity or clustering adjustment; ethics/consent and data-provenance statements; and reproducibility affordances such as code, analysis scripts, variable definitions, or a data availability statement.
what_triggers_block: A core reported inference cannot be verified or reconstructed because a load-bearing methodological element is absent or internally inconsistent — for example an undefined or shifting outcome/exposure operationalisation, unreported sample size or attrition at the analysed stage, a statistical procedure named without the estimates and uncertainty needed to evaluate it, or numbers in text and tables that disagree with no reconciliation.
what_triggers_warn: The design and analysis are reconstructable and the reported estimates stand, but rigor is thinned by specific remediable gaps — for example missing confidence intervals or effect sizes alongside reported p-values, unstated handling of a plausible confounder or of clustering, absent code/data availability statement, or ethics and consent provenance mentioned too vaguely to audit.
what_triggers_fatal: The stated design is structurally incapable of supporting the reported estimand at all, or the reported analysis is invalidated at its foundation — for example an outcome measured before or contemporaneously with the exposure it is claimed to follow, an analysis population that is defined by the outcome itself, or evidence of undisclosed post hoc redefinition of outcomes or subgroups such that no reported quantity can be trusted.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A traceable chain from each headline claim back to a specific reported quantity; alignment between the verbal strength of conclusions and the inferential strength of the design; consistency of the thesis across abstract, results, discussion, and any implications or recommendations; explicit acknowledgement of alternative explanations, including selection and reverse-causation accounts; and conclusions bounded to the studied population, setting, and time window.
what_triggers_block: A central conclusion is not supported by the evidence actually presented — causal, effectiveness, or policy-prescriptive language resting on associational evidence without the required identification argument; a headline claim whose supporting quantity is never reported; or a discussion/abstract claim that contradicts the results it cites.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument survives scrutiny but contains local overreach or friction — hedged findings restated more confidently in the abstract or implications, an obvious rival explanation left unaddressed, generalisation beyond the sampled setting stated without qualification, or a secondary claim whose evidentiary support is weaker than its phrasing implies.
what_triggers_fatal: The thesis is self-defeating as argued: the reported evidence, taken at face value, points against the paper's own conclusion, or the argument depends on mutually exclusive premises such that no revision short of a different study could rescue the stated claim.
