## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer):** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and its execution would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the field: whether the sampling and assignment logic supports the inferences drawn, whether data cleaning and exclusions are disclosed, whether statistical procedures are correctly chosen and fully reported (effect sizes, uncertainty, model specification), and whether enough materials or detail exist for another team to reproduce the analysis. I do not score it, but a fatal finding here from the methodology reviewer drives the decision under F1.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer):** This dimension covers factual fidelity to the field: whether the manuscript's claims are consistent with current evidence, whether cited prior work is characterised as its authors would recognise, and whether domain terminology, instruments, and reported quantities are used correctly. My oversight role is to register the domain reviewer's verdict rather than to substitute my own reading of the literature.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, also open to methodology):** Here the question is internal logic: does the central thesis hold together across sections, do the presented data actually license the conclusions asserted, and are there inferential leaps, equivocations, or circularities that would collapse the argument if named. Editorially, a coherence block matters because it usually cannot be repaired by copy-editing.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer):** This dimension tests whether a reader from an adjacent field can follow the framing, whether key constructs are defined rather than assumed, and whether any claim about relevance beyond the home discipline is actually backed by evidence rather than asserted in the discussion. Its high priority means a block here alone is enough to route the manuscript to major revision under F4.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as EiC):** I assess the manuscript as a communicative artefact: whether sections are ordered so the argument accumulates, whether exposition is clear and free of ambiguity that changes meaning, whether figures and tables are legible, self-labelled, and non-redundant with the text, and whether the manuscript observes the venue's conventions for length, structure, abstract, and reference formatting. This is a normal-priority dimension, so problems here escalate the decision only via the general F5 warn rule, never through a fatal pathway.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as EiC):** I judge whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge enough to warrant the space it asks for: is the topic within scope for the readership, is the contribution original relative to existing work rather than a restatement or a thin increment, and is the significance proportionate to the claims made in the title and abstract. Because this dimension is mandatory, a fatal determination triggers rejection under F1 and a block triggers major revision under F2, so I must hold the bar carefully and state my reasoning explicitly.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether section ordering builds the argument, prose is unambiguous, figures and tables are legible, captioned, and non-redundant, and venue conventions for length, abstract, headings, and referencing are observed.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is disordered enough that the study's design, results, or conclusions cannot be reliably reconstructed by a careful reader, or essential figures/tables are missing, unlabelled, or uninterpretable.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity or presentation defects — imprecise wording, thin or misplaced captions, uneven section balance, inconsistent reference formatting, or minor departures from venue conventions — that impede but do not prevent comprehension.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic and audience match the configured venue's scope, and whether the stated contribution is original, adequately positioned against prior work, and significant enough for the readership given the claims made in title and abstract.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is substantially thinner than advertised or its novelty relative to existing work is not established, so that scope or significance must be re-argued and re-evidenced before the manuscript can be assessed as a contribution.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but under-articulated — for example novelty is implied rather than stated against named prior work, or implications for the venue's readership are asserted only in passing.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript falls outside the venue's scope altogether, or offers no identifiable contribution beyond duplicating already-published work, leaving no revision path that would make it publishable here.
