## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive scrutiny by a competent peer reviewer in the field. Read adversarially, the question is not "did the authors describe a method?" but "could a skeptic reconstruct what was done and reach the same numbers?" A rigor claim collapses if sampling and assignment procedures are vague, if the analytic units shift silently, if uncertainty is unreported or reported without the assumptions it rests on, or if the data and code disclosures are gestures rather than affordances. I do not score this dimension, but I must remain alert to the boundary case where a methodological weakness is simultaneously a logical defect in the argument, since that is where my own dimension overlaps.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** Here the challenge is whether the manuscript's substantive assertions match what the domain's current evidence base actually supports, and whether cited prior work is represented as its authors would recognise. Adversarially, the failure modes are citation-as-decoration, mis-stated technical terminology, results attributed to sources that reported something narrower, and confident statements about a literature that is in fact contested. I do not score this dimension; my interest in it is limited to noticing when a domain misstatement is load-bearing for the central argument I am charged with testing.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, my owned dimension).** This is the dimension I am accountable for. The question is whether the paper's core thesis holds together on its own terms: whether each claim is entailed by the evidence actually presented rather than by evidence the reader is invited to imagine, whether the chain from data to conclusion is free of gaps that get bridged by rhetoric, and whether the paper's own stated limitations are honoured in its conclusions rather than quarantined in a paragraph and then ignored. My adversarial posture is to attack the strongest reading of the thesis, not a strawman: I will identify the load-bearing inferential steps, ask what would have to be true for each to hold, and test whether the manuscript establishes those conditions. Particular targets include unearned causal language over associational evidence, selection or survivorship effects treated as noise rather than as rival explanations, conflation of a measured proxy with the construct being argued about, circularity where the outcome is partly definitionally entailed by the predictor, unexamined rival hypotheses that the presented data cannot discriminate against, and abstract-versus-body claim inflation where the conclusion is broader than what the results section licenses. Internal consistency also matters: numbers, framings, and hedges must not contradict each other across sections.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** The adversarial reading here asks whether an intelligent reader from an adjacent field could follow the framing, take the definitions at face value without being misled, and evaluate the stated implications — or whether the paper's accessibility is achieved by leaving key terms undefined and its interdisciplinary reach asserted rather than substantiated. I do not score this dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformance to venue conventions. Read adversarially, poor structure is not merely cosmetic: it is often where argumentative gaps hide, since a claim that never appears in the same place as its evidence is hard to falsify. I do not score this dimension.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and significant for that readership, as opposed to restating known results with new local data. The adversarial question is what a reader would be able to do after this paper that they could not do before, and whether that increment is real or asserted. I do not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: The explicit chain from stated evidence to central thesis — each load-bearing inferential step identified, its required background assumptions named, and a check of whether the manuscript establishes those assumptions or merely presumes them; plus internal consistency of claims, hedges, and reported quantities across abstract, results, discussion, and stated limitations, and whether rival explanations for the reported pattern are enumerated and discriminated against by the presented evidence rather than dismissed rhetorically.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing inferential step in the central argument is unsupported by the evidence the manuscript itself presents — for example a causal or effectiveness conclusion resting on evidence that cannot exclude an obvious rival explanation, a proxy silently substituted for the construct the thesis is about, partial circularity between predictor and outcome, or conclusions that contradict the paper's own stated limitations — such that the thesis as stated does not follow even under its most charitable reading, and repair requires new analysis or a materially narrowed claim.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument survives its strongest challenge but is overstated or under-defended at identifiable points — scope inflation between abstract and results, hedges dropped in the discussion, a rival explanation acknowledged but not addressed, or a minor internal inconsistency in framing or reported figures — such that the defects are correctable by rewriting and re-scoping without new evidence.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's core thesis is incoherent or unfalsifiable as constructed — mutually contradictory central claims with no reading that reconciles them, a conclusion that is definitionally entailed by its own setup and therefore vacuous, or a thesis whose key evidentiary support is absent from the manuscript entirely — so that no revision short of a different paper could rescue the argument.
