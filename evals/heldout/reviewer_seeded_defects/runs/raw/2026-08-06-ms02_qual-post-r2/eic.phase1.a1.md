## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** From the chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study was actually built to support the inferences it draws: whether the design matches the research question, whether data collection and handling are described with enough precision that a competent peer could interrogate them, whether statistical or analytic reporting is complete and appropriately caveated, and whether the manuscript provides the affordances — instruments, coding schemes, sampling frames, availability statements — that let another investigator reproduce or at least audit the work. It is owned by the methodology reviewer and carries mandatory weight, so a fatal defect here ends the manuscript's run rather than merely delaying it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension governs whether the manuscript is truthful about its own field. It covers correct use of domain-specific terminology, accurate characterisation of prior literature and the positions of cited authors, and consistency between the claims made and the current weight of evidence in the discipline. Misrepresenting an antecedent study, inverting a known finding, or deploying a technical term in a sense the field does not recognise all belong here. The domain reviewer owns it, and because it is mandatory, factual corruption at this level is a decision-forcing event rather than a copy-edit note.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, devil's-advocate role with methodology eligibility).** Here the question is internal: does the paper's central thesis hold together on its own terms? The chain from premises through evidence to conclusion must be traceable, the evidence actually adduced must be capable of bearing the weight the conclusion places on it, and the manuscript must be free of reasoning errors — circularity, equivocation, unwarranted generalisation, causal claims from correlational material — that would collapse the core argument if named aloud. The devil's advocate owns it, methodology may also score it, and its mandatory status means incoherence at the thesis level is not survivable through polishing.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension tests whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper and leave with something usable. It asks whether framing and definitions are pitched so that assumed-shared vocabulary is actually made explicit, whether stated implications for neighbouring disciplines are substantiated rather than asserted as courtesy gestures, and whether interdisciplinary borrowings are handled with the same care the home field would demand. It is high priority rather than mandatory: a block here forces major revision under F4 but cannot on its own trigger rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, editor-in-chief role).** This is my own dimension in the ordinary editorial sense: how the manuscript is organised, whether the exposition is clear at sentence and section level, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission observes the conventions of the venue it is aimed at — length, sectioning, referencing, abstract structure. It carries normal priority, so deficiencies here shape the revision letter and can trigger the minor-revision condition, but they do not by themselves compel major revision or rejection.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, editor-in-chief role).** This is the gatekeeping judgement the chair cannot delegate: does this manuscript belong in this venue, addressed to this readership, and does it add something the readership does not already have? It combines topical and methodological fit with an assessment of originality and significance — whether the stated contribution is genuinely new relative to the existing record, whether it is consequential enough to warrant the venue's space, and whether the manuscript's scale and depth match what the venue's readers expect. It is mandatory, so a fatal verdict here is a rejection irrespective of how strong the other dimensions are.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript has a coherent and signposted structure (clear problem statement, method, evidence, discussion), prose that states its points without ambiguity or padding, figures and tables that are self-contained and correctly captioned, consistent and complete referencing, and conformance to the configured venue's length, sectioning, and formatting conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is disordered enough that the reader cannot reliably determine what was done, what was found, and what is being claimed — for example missing or unlabelled core sections, figures and tables that cannot be interpreted from their captions and callouts, or referencing so incomplete that cited support cannot be located.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but its presentation materially impedes efficient reading — redundant or misplaced material, sections whose stated purpose diverges from their content, under-specified figure or table labelling, inconsistent citation formatting, or noticeable departures from venue conventions that a revision round could correct.
### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, framing, and readership address the configured venue's scope; whether the manuscript states an explicit contribution and positions it against the existing record; and whether that contribution is both original and significant enough — in scale, depth, and consequence — to justify the venue's space for its stated audience.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is stated but substantially under-delivered or under-positioned for this venue — for instance an incremental restatement of established knowledge, a contribution claim not distinguished from clearly antecedent work, or scope and depth that fall short of what the venue's readership requires, such that acceptance would need substantive additional work rather than presentation changes.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but imprecisely established — the novelty claim is asserted rather than positioned against comparable work, the target readership or implications are left implicit, or the significance is real but oversold relative to what the manuscript actually delivers.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is outside the configured venue's scope altogether, or offers no identifiable original contribution to its readership — including cases where the substance duplicates prior published work or where the stated contribution dissolves entirely once the actual content is examined, leaving nothing that revision could recover.
