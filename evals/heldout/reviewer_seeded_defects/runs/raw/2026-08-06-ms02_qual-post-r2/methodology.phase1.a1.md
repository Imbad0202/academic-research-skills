## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership):** This dimension asks whether the study's design and execution would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in higher education policy research. From a rigor standpoint that means: the research question and design are matched (a qualitative interview design must not be asked to carry inferential or causal weight it cannot bear); the sampling frame, recruitment route, and unit of analysis are stated well enough that a reader can judge who is and is not represented; data collection instruments and their administration are documented; analysis is described as an auditable procedure rather than asserted as a conclusion; any quantitative reporting carries denominators, dispersion, and uncertainty rather than bare point estimates; and enough material (protocol, codebook, ethics approval, data availability statement) is disclosed that another researcher could in principle repeat or contest the work. Silence on these affordances is itself a finding, not a neutral omission.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain reviewer's ownership):** This dimension covers whether the manuscript's substantive claims match the current state of evidence in quality assurance and higher education policy, whether cited prior work is represented faithfully rather than conscripted, and whether field-specific vocabulary — accreditation cycles, internal versus external quality assurance, quality culture constructs — is used with its accepted technical meaning. I am not scoring this dimension; if I notice something in the intersection of measurement and domain fact (for instance, an instrument claimed to operationalise a named construct in a way that construct does not support), I will surface it as observation and defer the verdict to the domain reviewer.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, DA owns, I am eligible):** This dimension asks whether the paper's central thesis holds together internally and whether the evidence actually presented is the evidence the conclusions require. My methodological angle on it is the inference chain: does each claim's strength match the strength of its warrant; are causal, directional, or generalising conclusions drawn from designs that can only support description or association; are counter-explanations and negative cases acknowledged; do the discussion and abstract restate what the data showed, or do they quietly escalate it. Fallacies that matter here are the ones with a measurement root — sampling-based overreach, treating self-report as behaviour, confirmation-only evidence selection, and conclusions that contradict the study's own stated limitations.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, perspective reviewer's ownership):** This dimension concerns whether a reader from an adjacent field — organisational studies, public administration, education management — can follow the framing, decode the definitions, and evaluate any claim the paper makes about relevance beyond its own subfield. Interdisciplinary assertions must be substantiated rather than gestured at. I am not scoring this dimension and will not encroach on it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, EiC's ownership):** This dimension covers organisation, expositional clarity, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's formatting and reporting conventions. I am not scoring it. Where poor presentation actively prevents methodological verification — an unlabelled table whose numbers cannot be reconciled, for example — I will record that as a D1 evidentiary gap rather than a D5 style complaint, and say so explicitly.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC's ownership):** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and consequential for that readership. That is an editorial judgement I do not own. My only contribution to it is factual: if the design can only support a narrow, local, descriptive claim, I will state that scope limit plainly so the EiC can weigh contribution against what the evidence can actually carry.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design statement matched to the research question; sampling frame, recruitment, participant count and institutional diversity; interview or survey instrument and its administration; a stated, auditable analytic procedure (coding scheme, coder count, agreement or trustworthiness strategy); ethics approval and consent; complete reporting of any numbers with denominators and dispersion; limitations that name the design's actual inferential ceiling; and reproducibility affordances such as protocol, codebook, or data availability.
what_triggers_block: A structural defect that invalidates the reported findings as evidence for the paper's own conclusions — for example, findings and themes presented with no recoverable account of how data were collected or analysed, a sample or design that cannot address the stated research question, or reported statistics that are internally inconsistent or arithmetically impossible.
what_triggers_warn: The design is sound and traceable but under-documented in ways a revision can repair — thin instrument description, analytic procedure asserted rather than specified, missing reflexivity or trustworthiness detail, absent ethics or data availability statement, or incomplete descriptive reporting that does not overturn the findings.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the empirical basis is not credible as presented — data or participants that cannot be reconciled across sections of the manuscript, results that could not have been produced by the described procedure, or an explicit absence of ethical clearance for human-participant research such that no revision could rehabilitate the study.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A thesis stated once and held consistently across abstract, results, and discussion; each claim carrying a warrant of matching strength; hedging that tracks the design's evidentiary reach; explicit treatment of disconfirming cases and rival explanations; and a discussion that neither exceeds nor contradicts the paper's own stated limitations.
what_triggers_block: The central argument depends on an inference the presented evidence cannot license — causal or generalising conclusions drawn from a descriptive or non-representative design, or a headline claim that the paper's own reported data do not support.
what_triggers_warn: The core thesis survives but is weakened by localised overreach or slippage — abstract or conclusion phrased more strongly than the results section, unaddressed alternative explanations, or evidence selected only where it confirms the argument, all correctable by rewording and added qualification.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's conclusions are mutually contradictory or directly negated by its own reported findings, so that no consistent thesis can be recovered from the text at all.
