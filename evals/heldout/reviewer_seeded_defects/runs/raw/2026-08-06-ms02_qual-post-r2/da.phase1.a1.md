## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical or qualitative analysis reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive a hostile methodologist's reading. Adversarially, I read it as an invitation to assume the reported procedure is insufficient until the manuscript proves otherwise: sampling and recruitment must be specified rather than gestured at, analytic decisions must be traceable, and any inferential claim must be backed by a stated procedure a third party could re-run. Vagueness is not neutral here; it is a defect, because an unspecified method cannot be checked and therefore cannot be trusted. I am not the owner of this dimension and will not score it, but its failure modes overlap with mine wherever a methodological gap is what actually breaks an argumentative chain.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** The challenge is whether every substantive claim about the field is true and whether prior work is represented as the prior work actually says, not as the manuscript needs it to say. Adversarially, the risks are misused technical vocabulary, citations recruited to support propositions they do not support, stale evidence presented as current, and confident assertions about domain consensus that no cited source establishes. A manuscript can be internally tidy and still be wrong about the world; that is precisely what this dimension is for. I do not score it, but I flag that a domain error can masquerade as a coherence problem and vice versa, so I will keep my own findings anchored in reasoning structure rather than field fact.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, my dimension).** Here I am the adversary of record. The question is whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the claims stated in the abstract and conclusion are the claims the body actually earns, whether the evidence presented is the right *kind* of evidence for the inference drawn, and whether the reasoning chain from data to conclusion has any missing link that the prose merely paints over. My job is to attempt to break the argument, not to summarise it. Specific hostile probes: does a descriptive or perceptual dataset get silently upgraded into a causal or prescriptive conclusion; are alternative explanations for the same pattern acknowledged and ruled out or simply unmentioned; do stated scope limits get honoured in the conclusion or quietly abandoned; are key constructs defined once and used consistently, or does a term drift so that the argument only works because two meanings are being conflated; is any premise doing heavy load-bearing work while resting on assertion alone; is the conclusion falsifiable in principle given the design, or would any result have been read as confirming it. Circularity, question-begging framing, hasty generalisation from a narrow base, appeals to authority in place of evidence, and conclusions whose confidence exceeds their support are the failure patterns I will hunt.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, owned by the perspective reviewer).** The adversarial reading is whether an intelligent reader from an adjacent field could follow the framing, the definitions, and the stated implications without being an insider, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually substantiated rather than asserted for reach. Borrowed concepts must be used as their home discipline uses them, and implications claimed for other fields must be supported, not decorative. I do not score this dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** The challenge is whether the manuscript's organisation, exposition, figures, tables, and conformance to venue conventions let a reader locate and evaluate the argument efficiently. Adversarially, disorder is a substantive risk and not merely cosmetic: a claim buried away from its evidence, an unreadable or uninterpretable exhibit, or a structure that obscures where the contribution is stated makes verification harder and can conceal weaker reasoning. I do not score this dimension.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** The challenge is whether the work belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something the venue's readership does not already possess. Adversarially, the hard questions are what specifically is new, how the increment is distinguished from existing work, and whether the significance claimed is proportionate to what was actually done. Restatement of received wisdom with fresh packaging fails here even if everything else is sound. I do not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the thesis stated in the abstract, body, and conclusion is one and the same claim; whether each inferential step from evidence to conclusion is explicit and licensed by the kind of evidence actually presented; whether key constructs are defined once and used consistently without semantic drift; whether alternative explanations and stated scope limits are addressed in the conclusion rather than abandoned; and whether load-bearing premises are supported rather than asserted.
what_triggers_block: A central conclusion the presented evidence structurally cannot support — for example a causal, prescriptive, or generalising claim drawn from evidence that can only license description or association — or a load-bearing premise that is pure assertion, or a reasoning chain with a missing link that would have to be repaired by new argument or new data rather than by rewording.
what_triggers_warn: The core thesis survives scrutiny but has localised reasoning defects: overreach confined to isolated sentences, an unaddressed rival explanation that plausibly coexists with the authors' account, mild inconsistency in how a construct is used across sections, or confidence language that outruns the stated support while the substantive claim remains defensible.
what_triggers_fatal: The central argument is self-defeating or unfalsifiable as constructed — mutually contradictory core claims that cannot both be true, circular reasoning in which the conclusion is smuggled into a premise, or a thesis framed so that no possible finding could have counted against it — such that no revision short of rebuilding the argument could rescue it.
