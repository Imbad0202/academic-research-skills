## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by methodology).** This dimension asks whether the study's design actually licenses the inferences it draws. From a methodology-rigor standpoint that means: an explicit and appropriate research design for the stated question; a sampling and recruitment procedure that is described well enough to judge selection bias; instruments (interview protocols, surveys, coding frames) that are documented rather than merely named; data handling that is traceable from raw material to reported result; analysis that is matched to the data type, with uncertainty, effect magnitudes, or qualitative trustworthiness checks reported rather than asserted; and enough procedural detail — ethics approval, consent, anonymisation, materials availability — that an independent researcher could in principle repeat the work. Failure here is not about whether the findings are interesting but about whether the evidentiary chain holds.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by domain).** This dimension concerns fidelity to the substantive literature and terminology of the field: whether claims are consistent with current evidence, whether prior work is characterised accurately rather than strawmanned or over-credited, and whether domain-specific constructs, frameworks, and reported results are used in their established senses. It is a content-truth check rather than a procedure check, and it is owned by the domain reviewer; I am not scoring it.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by da, methodology eligible).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's central thesis hangs together internally and whether the evidence presented is of the right kind and strength to carry the conclusions drawn. From the methodology seat, my angle is the inference chain: do the stated findings actually follow from the reported data and analysis; are causal, generalising, or normative claims warranted by a design that can support them; are scope conditions and limitations acknowledged where the evidence thins; and are there equivocations, circularities, or unstated assumptions that would collapse the argument if made explicit. Contradictions between what the methods section can deliver and what the discussion asserts fall squarely here.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, owned by perspective).** This dimension evaluates whether framing, definitions, and stated implications are intelligible and useful to readers in adjacent fields, and whether any claim reaching across disciplinary boundaries is genuinely substantiated rather than rhetorically borrowed. It is owned by the perspective reviewer; I am not scoring it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, owned by eic).** This dimension covers manuscript organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue formatting and reporting conventions. It is owned by the editor-in-chief role; I am not scoring it.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by eic).** This dimension judges whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it makes an original contribution of significance appropriate to that readership. It is owned by the editor-in-chief role; I am not scoring it.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of research design and unit of analysis; sampling frame, recruitment, sample size and non-response or saturation rationale; documented instruments and data-collection procedure; a stated analytic method matched to the data with coding/inter-rater or statistical detail; reporting of uncertainty, effect sizes, or qualitative trustworthiness checks; ethics approval, consent and anonymisation; and reproducibility affordances such as available protocols, codebooks, or data statements.
what_triggers_block: A core reported result rests on a procedure that is not described at all or is described so vaguely that its validity cannot be assessed — e.g. unspecified sampling, undisclosed analytic method, missing denominators, or numbers that cannot be reconciled with any stated data-collection process.
what_triggers_warn: The design and analysis are identifiable and defensible in outline, but specific reproducibility or reporting elements are thin — e.g. missing instrument text, absent inter-coder agreement, unreported response rate, no uncertainty estimates, or no ethics/consent statement — leaving results checkable in principle but not verifiable as reported.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported methodology is internally impossible or fabricated — results that contradict the stated design, statistics that cannot arise from the described sample, undisclosed data alteration, or ethical violations of a kind that would invalidate the dataset regardless of revision.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A clearly stated thesis; explicit linkage from each reported finding to the claim it is offered to support; inference types (descriptive, causal, generalising, prescriptive) that stay within what the design can license; consistency between the limitations acknowledged in the methods and the strength of language used in the discussion and abstract; and stable use of key constructs across the manuscript.
what_triggers_block: The central conclusion does not follow from the presented evidence — for example causal or population-level generalisations drawn from a design incapable of supporting them, or a headline claim contradicted by the paper's own reported data.
what_triggers_warn: The main argument holds, but secondary claims are over-stated relative to the evidence, scope conditions are left implicit, or terminology drifts between sections in ways that weaken rather than break the inference chain.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's argument is self-defeating at its core — mutually contradictory central claims that cannot both be true, or a thesis sustained only by circular reasoning or by evidence the paper itself elsewhere disavows, such that no revision short of a different study could repair it.
