## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study was actually built to support what it claims: whether the design fits the research question, whether data collection and handling are described in enough detail that a competent peer could interrogate them, whether any statistical or analytic reporting is complete and correctly applied, and whether a reader has the affordances (instruments, sampling logic, coding procedures, availability statements) needed to reproduce or at least audit the work. It is owned by the methodology reviewer, and it is mandatory — a fatal defect here ends the manuscript's path to publication regardless of how attractive the topic is.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension checks that the manuscript is factually sound within its own field: that the substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence, that prior scholarship is represented accurately rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and that field-specific terminology, frameworks, and reported results are used correctly. Editorially, this is the reputational-risk dimension — errors here survive publication and get cited by others — so it too carries mandatory weight and is assigned to the domain specialist rather than to me.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate, also open to methodology).** Here the question is whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated conclusions actually follow from the evidence presented, whether there are internal contradictions between sections, and whether any logical fallacy or unearned inferential leap sits at a load-bearing point in the argument. A paper can be methodologically clean and factually accurate yet still fail this dimension by overclaiming, and because it is mandatory, an unsalvageable break in the argument's spine is grounds for the harshest outcomes.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension evaluates whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper without being blocked by unexplained jargon, parochial framing, or implicit assumptions, and whether any claims the paper makes about relevance beyond its home discipline are actually substantiated rather than asserted. It is high priority rather than mandatory: failure here is serious enough to force a major revision but is not by itself a rejection trigger. It belongs to the perspective reviewer.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, my dimension as EiC).** This is my assessment of the manuscript as a piece of scholarly communication: whether the organisation moves the reader logically from problem to method to evidence to conclusion, whether the exposition is clear and free of ambiguity at points that matter, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission observes the venue's structural and formatting conventions. Its normal priority means deficiencies here shape revision requests rather than acceptance in principle, but severe presentational failure can still make the work unusable to readers.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, my dimension as EiC).** This is the gatekeeping judgement proper: does this manuscript belong in this venue, addressed to this readership, and does it advance the field with something original and consequential rather than restating settled knowledge or reporting an increment too small to warrant a journal slot? Scope mismatch and insufficient contribution are the two classic desk-level grounds for refusal, and because this dimension is mandatory, a fundamental failure of fit or novelty is decision-determinative for the panel regardless of the manuscript's other virtues.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A discernible and conventional organisational arc (problem, prior work, method, evidence, discussion, limitations, conclusion) with sections that are proportionate to their function; prose that states claims unambiguously and defines terms at first use; figures and tables that are numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, referenced in the text, and carrying information the prose does not already duplicate; consistent citation and reference formatting; and adherence to the length, abstract, and structural conventions the configured venue expects.
what_triggers_block: Structure or exposition is broken badly enough that the reader cannot reliably determine what was done or what is being claimed — for example missing or unrecognisable core sections, results presented without any interpretive framing, figures or tables that are uncaptioned, unreferenced, or contradict the text they illustrate, or a reference apparatus so incomplete that claims cannot be traced.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but presentationally deficient — noticeable redundancy or disproportion between sections, passages where imprecise or undefined wording leaves a claim open to more than one reading, figures or tables that are legible but poorly labelled or under-explained, inconsistent formatting or citation style, or minor departures from venue conventions that a copy-editing and restructuring pass would resolve.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit and defensible statement of what is new — a gap identified in the existing literature and a claim about how this work closes it; evidence that the contribution is of a type and scale the configured venue publishes and that its intended audience is that venue's readership; substantive engagement with the relevant prior scholarship sufficient to demonstrate that the increment is real rather than assumed; and implications developed far enough that a reader of this venue could act on or build from them.
what_triggers_block: The stated contribution is materially insufficient or unsubstantiated for the venue — the work restates established knowledge without a defensible increment, the novelty claim is asserted but not positioned against relevant prior work, the scope is a poor match for the venue's remit such that the wrong readership is being addressed, or the substantive payload is too thin to support a full research article at this outlet.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real and the venue is broadly appropriate, but the case is undersold or imprecisely framed — the novelty claim is stated vaguely rather than sharply differentiated from the nearest prior work, the implications for the venue's readership are gestured at rather than developed, or the scope drifts toward an adjacent outlet in ways a reframed introduction and discussion could correct.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript has no admissible contribution to this or any venue — it falls entirely outside the venue's subject remit, or its central claimed contribution is void because the work is duplicative of already-published material, is a repackaging of the authors' own prior output without new substance, or rests on a premise that removes any basis for original claims.
