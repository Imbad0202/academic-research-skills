## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor.** As an adversarial reader I take this dimension to mean the manuscript must survive an attempt to break its design: whether the sampling frame, instrument, and analysis plan could plausibly produce the numbers reported, whether statistics are reported completely enough that a skeptic could recompute them, and whether the materials and data-handling description would let an independent party attempt replication. The challenge posture is to ask what a hostile methodologist could exploit — undisclosed exclusions, unreported assumption checks, analyses that drift from any stated plan — and to treat silence on such matters as a defect rather than a neutral omission. This dimension is owned by the methodology role, so my job is not to score it but to avoid contaminating my own reasoning with methodological complaints dressed up as logic problems.

**D2 — domain_accuracy.** Read adversarially, this asks whether the manuscript's substantive claims would survive contact with someone who knows the literature cold: are cited prior findings characterised as their authors would recognise them, are domain constructs used with their accepted meanings rather than idiosyncratic ones, and are numerical or terminological statements about the field actually correct. The attack surface includes citations that are directionally reversed, contested findings presented as settled, and terminology borrowed from one tradition while being interpreted under another. Ownership sits with the domain role; my responsibility is to flag only where an accuracy problem is simultaneously an argumentative dependency, and even then to defer the accuracy judgement itself.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence.** This is the dimension I own, and adversarially it means the paper's central thesis must hold together under deliberate pressure. I will attempt to construct the strongest available counter-reading: does the conclusion actually follow from the evidence offered, or does it require an unstated bridging premise the authors never defend; do claims made in the abstract, results, and discussion say the same thing or quietly escalate; does the design licence the causal, directional, or generalising language used; are rival explanations for the reported pattern acknowledged and dispatched, or simply unmentioned. Common failure shapes I will hunt for include circularity between construct definition and finding, affirming the consequent from a correlational pattern, conflation of a measured proxy with the theoretical target, selective engagement with only the friendly interpretations, and conclusions whose scope exceeds the population, instrument, or timeframe that produced them. The standard is not that the paper be uninteresting or hedged into vacuity, but that every load-bearing inferential step be visible and defensible.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance.** Adversarially framed, this asks whether a competent reader from an adjacent field could follow the framing without insider tacit knowledge, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually earned rather than asserted. The hostile question is whether interdisciplinary language is doing rhetorical work — invoking another field's authority to inflate significance — while the substantive engagement with that field is thin or absent, and whether key constructs are defined once, clearly, rather than assumed. This dimension belongs to the perspective role, so I will not score it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure.** The adversarial reading here is that organisation and exposition are not cosmetic: a reader should not have to reconstruct the argument's skeleton themselves, figures and tables should be self-sufficient and consistent with the text, and venue conventions should be met rather than approximated. The attack is to look for structural choices that obscure weakness — results distributed so that no single place shows the full picture, tables whose numbers do not reconcile with the prose, sections that promise analyses never delivered. Ownership rests with the EiC role; I will note structural problems only insofar as they are indistinguishable from argumentative gaps, and will not score the dimension.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution.** Read as a challenge, this asks whether the manuscript would be worth the readership's attention at the configured venue, and whether the claimed contribution is genuinely additive rather than a restatement of established results in new packaging. The adversarial probes are whether the stated novelty survives comparison with the nearest prior work, whether the significance claim is proportionate to the evidence, and whether the topic and treatment match what this venue's readers need. This is an EiC-owned dimension and outside my scoring remit.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the central thesis is stated once and consistently across abstract, results, and discussion; whether each load-bearing inference from evidence to conclusion is explicit and licensed by the reported design; whether alternative explanations for the reported pattern are named and addressed; and whether the scope of concluding claims stays inside the population, instrument, and timeframe actually studied.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing inferential defect that the current evidence cannot repair — for example the headline conclusion asserts a causal or directional relationship that the reported evidence cannot support, or the thesis stated in the discussion contradicts the thesis or findings stated earlier, or the argument depends on an unstated premise that, once surfaced, is implausible or begs the question.
what_triggers_warn: Localised coherence weaknesses that leave the main argument standing but require repair — for example a secondary claim overreaches its evidence, an obvious rival explanation goes unmentioned, hedging is inconsistent between sections, or a construct is used with two slightly different senses without the argument itself collapsing.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript has no recoverable argument to revise — the central claim is circular or unfalsifiable as constructed, or the stated conclusion is logically incompatible with the evidence presented such that no rewriting short of a different study could reconcile them, or the paper simultaneously advances mutually exclusive theses as its main finding.
