## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my ownership).** This is the dimension I own: whether the study's design actually licenses the inferences drawn from it. From a methodology-rigor standpoint I read this as covering the alignment between research question and design, the sampling frame and recruitment procedure, how the instrument was constructed and validated, how missing and excluded data were handled, whether the statistical analyses are appropriate to the measurement level and dependence structure of the data, whether estimates are reported with uncertainty rather than as bare point values or bare p-values, and whether a competent independent analyst could re-run the analysis from what is disclosed (instrument items, data availability, analysis code or fully specified procedures, ethics/consent). A manuscript passes this dimension not by being ambitious but by being auditable: every quantitative claim must be traceable to a described procedure applied to a described sample.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension asks whether the substantive content is correct with respect to the field's current evidence base: constructs named accurately, prior findings represented as their authors actually reported them, terminology used in its standard technical sense, and no misstatement of established results. It is not mine to score, but it constrains me indirectly — a measurement instrument can be procedurally clean yet operationalise a construct in a way the domain literature would not recognise, and if I notice such a mismatch I should surface it as evidence for the domain reviewer rather than score it myself.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by discourse analysis, methodology eligible).** Here the question is whether the chain from evidence to conclusion holds together: does the stated thesis remain the same thesis throughout, do the reported results actually entail the claims made about them, and are there inferential leaps or fallacies that would collapse the central argument. My methodology-eligible angle on this is narrower and more specific than the discourse owner's: I test whether the strength and causal direction of the conclusions is licensed by the design that produced the numbers, whether qualifiers survive from the results section into the abstract and discussion, and whether limitations acknowledged in one place are quietly ignored where the headline claim is made.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, perspective role).** This concerns whether a reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper: are field-specific constructs defined rather than assumed, are implications stated in terms that travel beyond the immediate subfield, and are any claims about relevance to other disciplines actually supported rather than merely asserted. Not scored by me.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, editor role).** This covers organisation, expositional clarity, quality and self-sufficiency of tables and figures, and conformity to the venue's conventions for sectioning, reporting, and length. Not scored by me; where poor exposition makes a procedure impossible to evaluate, I will record that as a methodology-rigor reporting gap rather than as a writing complaint.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, editor role).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge in a way that matters to that readership — originality and significance, not merely correctness. Not scored by me; a study can be methodologically flawless and still fail this dimension, and the reverse.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: An explicit design statement matched to the research questions; sampling frame, recruitment channel, invitation and completion counts with response rate; instrument provenance including full item wording, response scale, and any reliability or validity evidence; operational definitions of each variable and how composite scores were formed; handling of missing, incomplete, or excluded responses with counts; analyses appropriate to the measurement level and to the fact that the design is observational; effect estimates with confidence intervals and exact test statistics rather than p-values alone; a stated power or precision rationale for the sample size; ethics approval and consent procedure; and reproducibility affordances such as data availability, instrument appendix, and specified software and analysis steps.
what_triggers_block: A quantitative conclusion rests on a procedure that is not recoverable or not defensible as reported — for example central statistics presented with no sample size, no denominator, or no test specified; the analytic sample never reconciled against those invited or those excluded; a self-report instrument whose items and scale are nowhere given so its scores cannot be interpreted; or an inferential model whose assumptions are plainly violated by the described data with no acknowledgement or alternative.
what_triggers_warn: The core procedure is recoverable and defensible but has identifiable gaps that weaken confidence without invalidating it — for instance a response rate reported without a nonresponse comparison, reliability given as a single coefficient with no validity evidence, effect sizes or intervals omitted while test statistics are present, no stated sample-size rationale, or ethics and data-availability statements missing while the analysis itself is fully specified.
what_triggers_fatal: The reported methods and the reported results cannot both be true — an analysis described as impossible on the stated data, numbers that fail internal arithmetic reconciliation across text, tables, and abstract, evidence of undisclosed post hoc redefinition of outcomes or groups to obtain the reported finding, or human-subjects data collected with no ethical authorisation of any kind.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the thesis stated in the introduction is the thesis defended in the discussion; whether each claim in the abstract and conclusion maps onto a specific reported result of adequate strength; whether the verbs describing relationships stay within what a cross-sectional self-report design supports; whether qualifiers and limitations acknowledged in one section are honoured wherever the headline claim is restated; and whether the interpretation considers and addresses obvious rival explanations such as common-method variance, reverse direction of association, or selection into the sample.
what_triggers_block: The central argument depends on an inference the reported evidence does not license — a causal, predictive, or effectiveness claim built from a single-timepoint associational design; a conclusion generalised to a population the sampling procedure never reached; or a headline finding asserted in abstract and conclusion that no reported analysis in the body actually tests.
what_triggers_warn: The main argument holds but is locally overreached or under-defended — softened wording in results hardening into stronger wording in the abstract, a plausible rival explanation left unaddressed, an unqualified practical recommendation drawn from a modest association, or limitations listed as a detached paragraph that never revises any stated conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's conclusion contradicts its own reported evidence — the direction, sign, or significance of a stated finding is the opposite of what the tables show, or two load-bearing claims required by the thesis are mutually exclusive and the paper cannot be repaired by weakening either without dissolving its contribution.
