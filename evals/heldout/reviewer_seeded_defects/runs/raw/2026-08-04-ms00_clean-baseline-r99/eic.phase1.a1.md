## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** From the editor's chair, this dimension asks whether the study could survive a competent methods referee: whether the design actually supports the question asked, whether the data were collected and handled defensibly, whether statistical procedures are reported completely enough for a reader to judge them, and whether enough is disclosed (instruments, sampling, analysis steps, materials) that another team could in principle repeat the work. It is owned by the methodology reviewer and is mandatory, meaning a fatal finding here forces outright rejection rather than revision.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension covers factual correctness within the subject area: are the substantive claims consistent with what the current evidence base actually supports, is prior literature described faithfully rather than conveniently, and are field-specific terms, constructs, and reported results used correctly. Editorially, this is the dimension that protects the journal from publishing statements a specialist reader would recognise as wrong or as a misreading of the cited sources. Only the domain reviewer scores it, and it carries mandatory weight.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, da and methodology roles).** Here the question is whether the paper's reasoning holds together end to end: does the central thesis stay consistent from introduction to conclusion, does the evidence presented actually license the conclusions drawn, and are there logical defects — overreach, equivocation, unsupported causal leaps, circularity — serious enough to undermine the main argument rather than merely blemish it. The devil's-advocate reviewer owns it, with the methodology reviewer also eligible, and it too is mandatory.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension tests whether a reader from a neighbouring field can enter the paper without insider knowledge: whether framing and definitions are explained rather than assumed, whether implications are stated in terms an adjacent audience can evaluate, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually backed by evidence rather than asserted as rhetoric. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a blocking finding drives major revision instead of rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, eic role).** This is the editor's own craft dimension: how the manuscript is organised, how clearly the exposition carries the reader through the argument, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative, and whether the submission observes the conventions expected by the target venue. It is normal priority, so problems here shape the revision request but cannot by themselves force rejection.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, eic role).** This is the editor's gatekeeping judgement on whether the work belongs in this journal at all and whether it adds anything: does the topic, scope, and treatment match what the configured venue publishes and what its readership expects, and does the manuscript deliver an original and non-trivial contribution rather than a restatement of settled knowledge or an under-powered fragment. Despite being an editorial judgement rather than a technical one, it is mandatory, so a fatal verdict here triggers rejection.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A logical section progression with a stated aim, methods, results, and discussion that map onto each other; prose that defines terms before using them; tables and figures that are self-explanatory, correctly captioned, referenced in text, and non-redundant with the prose; consistent reporting conventions and complete, correctly formatted references.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken enough that a reader cannot reconstruct what was done or what was found — for example missing or unlabelled results displays, numbers in text that contradict numbers in tables, or sections whose content does not match their headings.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but degraded by fixable defects such as verbose or repetitive passages, undefined jargon, inconsistent terminology, poorly formatted or hard-to-read tables, or deviations from venue formatting conventions.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of the gap the study addresses and what it adds beyond existing work; a topic and treatment consistent with what the configured venue publishes for its stated readership; a contribution whose scope and depth are proportionate to the manuscript's length and claims; and implications framed for that readership rather than asserted generically.
what_triggers_block: The stated contribution is largely a restatement of already well-established findings, or the scope is too slight to sustain a research article at this venue, such that substantial reframing or additional work would be required before the paper offers publishable value.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real but under-articulated or oversold — the novelty claim is vague, the positioning against prior work is thin, or the significance for the venue's readership is asserted rather than argued — yet is recoverable by rewriting the framing.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript falls outside the configured venue's subject scope altogether, or presents no identifiable original contribution whatsoever, so that no revision within the paper's existing content could make it appropriate for this journal.
