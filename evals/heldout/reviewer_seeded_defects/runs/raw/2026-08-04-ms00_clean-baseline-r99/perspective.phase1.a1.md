## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, and statistical reporting would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the paper's home field, and whether enough is disclosed that another team could repeat the work. Read from my seat as the cross-disciplinary reader, methodological rigor matters indirectly but sharply: a design whose limits are not stated cannot be safely borrowed by an adjacent field, because outside readers lack the tacit norms to know what the design can and cannot license. I do not score this dimension; I only note, where relevant, when methodological choices are described in ways that an adjacent-field reader would systematically misread.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension covers whether the manuscript's substantive claims match the current state of evidence in its own field, whether prior work is characterised faithfully rather than strawmanned or over-credited, and whether field-specific terms and reported results are used correctly. My cross-disciplinary interest here is narrow and different: terminology that is accurate inside the field can still be a false friend outside it, since the same word often carries a different technical meaning one discipline over. Correctness itself is the domain reviewer's call, not mine.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, also open to methodology).** This dimension tests whether the central thesis holds together from premises to conclusion, whether the evidence actually bears the weight of the claims placed on it, and whether any reasoning defect is severe enough to undermine the paper's core argument. For an outside reader, coherence and accessibility are entangled: an argument with unstated intermediate steps reads as coherent to insiders who fill the gaps automatically and as a non-sequitur to everyone else. I flag such gaps as accessibility problems under my own dimension rather than scoring coherence here.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, my dimension).** This is the dimension I own. It asks whether the paper's framing, its definitions of key constructs, and its stated implications are genuinely usable by readers in adjacent fields, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home discipline is actually substantiated rather than merely asserted. Two failure directions concern me symmetrically: the paper may be hermetically sealed, using jargon, instruments, and background assumptions without enough scaffolding for a neighbouring specialist to evaluate anything; or it may over-reach, exporting conclusions to populations, settings, institutional contexts, or disciplines that the study never touched. The healthy middle is a paper that states its constructs operationally, names its boundary conditions, and calibrates its implications to the scope of what it examined.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief).** This dimension covers manuscript organisation, clarity of prose, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's structural conventions. It overlaps with mine at the edges, since poor signposting harms outside readers first, but the two are distinct: a beautifully written paper can still be unusable across disciplines, and a clumsily written one can still define its terms precisely. Scoring belongs to the EiC.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief).** This dimension judges whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge in a way that is original and significant for that readership. My perspective touches it because interdisciplinary breadth is sometimes offered as a substitute for depth of contribution, and an outside-facing framing does not by itself create novelty. Whether the contribution clears the venue's bar is nonetheless the EiC's determination, not mine.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Explicit operational definitions of key constructs and instruments; a stated scope covering population, setting, and time frame; whether stated implications for other fields, institutions, or practice are tied to evidence actually presented; whether jargon, abbreviations, and inherited theoretical assumptions are unpacked enough that an adjacent-field specialist could independently assess the claims.
what_triggers_block: Central constructs are used without any operational definition, or headline implications are exported to populations, settings, or disciplines the study never examined, such that an adjacent-field reader would be led to a conclusion the evidence does not support.
what_triggers_warn: Definitions and scope are present but thin or scattered, some field-specific terms or measures go unexplained, or a few implication statements are broader than the presented evidence while the paper's main claim remains recoverable to an outside reader.
