## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** The contract asks whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, statistical reporting, and reproducibility affordances would survive scrutiny from a competent methodologist in the paper's own field. Read adversarially, this means the burden is on the manuscript to show that its inferential machinery is licensed by its design rather than assumed: that the sampling frame supports the population claims, that measures are defensible, that reported statistics are complete enough to be recomputed or at least audited, and that materials, instruments, and analytic decisions are documented well enough for another team to attempt replication. Absence of such documentation is not neutral; it is a gap the authors must close.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** Here the challenge is whether the manuscript's substantive claims are true and current with respect to the literature it inhabits, and whether it represents prior work honestly rather than conveniently. An adversarial reading looks for mischaracterised citations, constructs used with non-standard or drifting definitions, terminology deployed loosely, and results asserted as established when they are contested or superseded. A single confidently stated but factually wrong domain claim damages credibility disproportionately, because it signals that the authors are not embedded in the evidence base they invoke.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, my dimension as devil's advocate).** This dimension asks whether the paper's central thesis holds together on its own terms: whether the stated claims are the claims the evidence can bear, whether the chain from data to conclusion has no missing links, and whether no reasoning defect quietly does the work that the evidence fails to do. My job is to attack the argument's spine rather than its details — to hunt for conclusions that outrun the design, conflations between association and causation or between perception and behaviour, conclusions restated in the abstract at a strength never earned in the results, scope creep in the implications, unfalsifiable framing, circular support where a construct is validated by the very measure it explains, and internal contradictions between sections. Coherence failures are not stylistic; they mean the paper's headline claim is not the claim it has demonstrated.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, perspective role).** The contract requires that framing, definitions, and implications be legible and defensible to readers in adjacent fields, and that any claim reaching across disciplinary boundaries be substantiated rather than gestured at. Adversarially, this means checking whether jargon is unpacked, whether constructs borrowed from neighbouring literatures are used as those literatures use them, and whether the paper's stated significance for other communities is backed by argument rather than asserted in a final paragraph.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, EiC role).** This concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue conventions. Read as a challenge, the question is whether a reader can reconstruct what was done and what was found without reverse-engineering the text — whether sections carry their intended load, whether tables report what they claim to report with units, denominators, and uncertainty, and whether structural sprawl or compression hides analytic weakness.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role).** The contract asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge in a way that matters to that readership. The adversarial test is whether the contribution is genuinely novel and non-trivial rather than a restatement of well-established findings in a new sample, and whether the scale and depth of the work are commensurate with what the venue publishes. A study can be internally clean and still fail here if it adds nothing the field did not already know.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the stated thesis, the evidence actually presented, and the conclusions drawn in abstract, discussion, and implications describe the same claim at the same strength — checking specifically for inferential leaps beyond the design (e.g., causal or effectiveness language resting on correlational or single-timepoint evidence), conflation of self-report or perception with observed behaviour or outcomes, circular or tautological support where the explanatory construct and the outcome measure share content, generalisation past the sampled population, internal contradictions between sections, unfalsifiable or post-hoc framing of hypotheses, and conclusions that survive only because a rival explanation was never stated.
what_triggers_block: The manuscript's headline conclusion cannot be derived from the evidence presented even granting the authors' own data — e.g., a causal, effectiveness, or predictive claim asserted where the design supports only association; or the core construct is validated by the very measure it purports to explain, so the central finding is circular and could not have come out otherwise.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument is derivable in principle but overstated or under-defended at identifiable points — hedged claims escalating into unhedged language in abstract or implications, an unstated alternative explanation left unaddressed, scope creep in recommendations beyond the sampled setting, or a local inconsistency between sections that weakens without dismantling the thesis.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's argument is self-defeating or unrecoverable by revision — the stated conclusion is directly contradicted by the authors' own reported evidence, mutually exclusive core claims are asserted as jointly true, or the thesis is constructed so that no possible result could disconfirm it, such that no rewrite short of a different study could repair the reasoning.
