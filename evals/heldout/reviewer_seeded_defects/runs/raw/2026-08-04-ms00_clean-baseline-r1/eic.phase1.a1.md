## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and execution would survive scrutiny from a competent methodologist in the manuscript's field. It covers how the research was set up, how data were collected and cleaned, whether the analytic choices are defensible and reported with enough precision (effect sizes, uncertainty, assumptions, sample provenance), and whether another team could plausibly repeat the work from what is written. As an editorial matter, it is a gate condition: no amount of interesting framing compensates for an evidential base that cannot bear the weight placed on it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** Here the concern is factual fidelity to the substantive field. Do the claims sit correctly against the current state of evidence, is prior literature characterised as its authors would recognise, and are field-specific terms, constructs, instruments, and reported results used correctly rather than loosely? Misattribution, stale or superseded evidence presented as current, and terminological slippage all fall here. This too is mandatory, because a paper that misstates its own domain corrupts the record regardless of how carefully its statistics were run.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, devil's-advocate role with methodology as secondary).** This tests whether the paper's central line of reasoning holds together on its own terms: does the thesis stay the same from abstract to conclusion, does the evidence actually license the inferences drawn from it, and are there logical gaps — overreach from association to causation, equivocation between constructs, unacknowledged alternative explanations — that would break the argument if a hostile but fair reader pressed on them? Mandatory status reflects that an incoherent argument is not repairable by adding data.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role).** This dimension concerns legibility and warrant beyond the author's home specialism. Framing, definitions, and stated implications should be followable by an informed reader from an adjacent field without insider decoding, and any claim that reaches into another discipline must be supported rather than asserted by analogy. It is high priority rather than mandatory: failure here degrades reach and can force major revision, but it does not by itself invalidate the work.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, editor-in-chief role, my responsibility).** This is the editorial craft dimension: is the manuscript organised so that a reader can find the argument, are sections proportionate and non-redundant, is the prose clear enough that meaning does not depend on charitable reading, do figures and tables carry interpretable content with adequate captions and labelling, and does the submission observe the venue's structural and formatting conventions (abstract, reporting sections, references, limitations)? Normal priority means problems here typically generate revision requests rather than rejection, but sustained opacity can still obscure whether the science is sound.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, editor-in-chief role, my responsibility).** This is the question every editor must answer before sending a paper forward: does this manuscript belong in this venue, and does it add something its readership does not already have? It combines topical and methodological fit with the configured outlet, appropriateness of scope and depth for that readership, and genuine originality and significance — a defensible increment on what is already published rather than a restatement, a fragment of a larger study, or an exercise too slight to justify the space. Its mandatory status means an unfit or non-contributory paper is a decision-determining failure regardless of its internal tidiness.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Clear IMRaD-equivalent organisation, proportionate sections, unambiguous prose, self-contained and correctly labelled figures/tables with reported units and denominators, complete and consistent referencing, and adherence to the venue's structural conventions including abstract, limitations, and reporting standards.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition so deficient that the reader cannot reconstruct what was done or what is claimed — e.g. missing or unusable methods/results sections, figures and tables whose numbers cannot be tied to any described analysis, or pervasive internal inconsistency between text and displayed data.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity and presentation defects that impede but do not prevent comprehension — e.g. under-captioned or redundant tables, disproportionate or misplaced sections, undefined abbreviations, inconsistent citation formatting, or a missing limitations discussion.
what_triggers_fatal: NOT_APPLICABLE

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of the gap addressed and what is new, scope and depth matched to the configured venue's readership, positioning against the specific prior literature it claims to extend, and evidence that the increment is substantive enough to warrant publication space.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is stated but thin or insufficiently differentiated for this venue — the study largely reproduces well-established findings, or its scope and depth fall materially below what the readership expects, such that acceptance would require a substantially strengthened or reframed contribution claim.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and novelty are plausible but under-argued — the gap statement is vague, the positioning against prior work is generic, or the implications for the venue's readership are asserted rather than developed, requiring targeted revision rather than new work.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is categorically outside the venue's remit or offers no identifiable original contribution at all — for example a topical or disciplinary mismatch that no revision could reconcile, a restatement of existing published work, or a fragment with no standalone scholarly claim.
