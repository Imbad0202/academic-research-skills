## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role):** From an editorial oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study was actually built to support the inferences drawn from it: whether the design suits the question, whether the data were collected and handled in a defensible way, whether statistics are reported completely enough for a reader to judge them, and whether another researcher could plausibly reproduce or re-analyse the work. It is a gate-level requirement, meaning a serious failure here cannot be traded away against strengths elsewhere. The methodology reviewer owns it, so my editorial job is to receive that verdict rather than substitute my own.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role):** This dimension covers factual fidelity to the field: are the claims consistent with what the current evidence base actually supports, is prior literature described as it really is rather than as a convenient foil, and are field-specific terms, instruments, and reported results used correctly. Misstatement of an established finding or misuse of a technical construct is not a stylistic blemish here; it is a correctness failure. It is mandatory and owned by the domain reviewer, and as editor I treat their assessment as authoritative on questions of substance.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, da and methodology roles):** Here the question is whether the manuscript's central argument holds together on its own terms — whether the thesis stated up front is the thesis actually defended, whether the evidence presented is the kind of evidence that could bear the weight of the conclusion, and whether any reasoning steps smuggle in unsupported leaps. Two roles are eligible, with the devil's-advocate reviewer owning it, which signals that adversarial pressure-testing of the logic is expected. It is mandatory, so incoherence at the core of the argument is disqualifying regardless of polish.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high, perspective role):** This dimension asks whether a competent reader from an adjacent field could follow the framing, understand the definitions without insider knowledge, and see why the implications matter beyond a narrow subspecialty — and, where the manuscript itself reaches across disciplines, whether those reaching claims are actually backed rather than gestured at. It carries high rather than mandatory priority, so weakness here escalates the editorial decision without automatically ending it. The perspective reviewer owns it.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal, eic role):** This is mine to score. It concerns craft-level presentation: whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can navigate from problem to method to result to interpretation without backtracking, whether exposition is clear and unambiguous, whether figures and tables are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative rather than decorative, and whether the submission observes the conventions the venue expects. Priority is normal, so problems here shape revision requests rather than acceptance in principle — but they still register, because unreadable work cannot be evaluated fairly by readers.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, eic role):** Also mine to score, and mandatory. Two linked judgements: whether this manuscript belongs in this venue given its stated scope and readership, and whether it advances something original and consequential enough to justify occupying space there. Incremental restatement of settled knowledge, or solid work aimed at an entirely different audience, both fail this dimension even when everything else is competent. Because it is mandatory, a severe verdict here drives the editorial decision directly through the failure conditions.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether sections follow a logical problem-method-result-interpretation progression, whether prose is unambiguous at the sentence level, whether every figure and table is self-explanatory with complete captions, units, and sample sizes, and whether length, abstract, headings, and reference formatting match venue conventions.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken badly enough that a qualified reader cannot reliably determine what was done or what was found — for example results presented without any accompanying method description, unlabelled or uninterpretable displays carrying the main evidence, or a structure so disordered that claims cannot be matched to support.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but has localised presentation defects — vague or overloaded passages, missing detail in captions or table headers, duplicated or misplaced material, inconsistent terminology, or departures from venue formatting conventions that a targeted revision pass would fix.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the research question and framing address the venue's stated scope and readership, what specifically is claimed as new relative to existing work, whether the contribution's significance is argued rather than asserted, and whether the scale and depth of the study are commensurate with what the venue publishes.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is too thin or too derivative to warrant publication here as submitted — the novelty claim rests on restating known results, or the work targets an audience materially different from the venue's readership — yet a defensible contribution could plausibly be established through substantial revision, reframing, or additional evidence.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are real but underspecified or oversold — the novelty relative to prior work is stated imprecisely, implications for the venue's readership are asserted without development, or the significance argument needs sharpening while the underlying contribution stands.
what_triggers_fatal: There is no recoverable contribution or venue relationship at all — the manuscript falls wholly outside the venue's subject scope, or its central offering is already established knowledge presented as new, such that no revision within the scope of this submission could make it publishable here.
