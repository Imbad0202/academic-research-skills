## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** This dimension asks whether the study was designed and executed well enough to survive scrutiny by peers in its own field: whether sampling, measurement instruments, data handling, and statistical reporting are sound and transparent enough that another researcher could in principle repeat the work. From where I sit as the cross-disciplinary reader, I care about this mainly because a design flaw travels: if the inferential machinery is weak, any implication offered to neighbouring fields inherits that weakness. But the call itself belongs to the methodology reviewer, and I will not score it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** Here the question is whether the manuscript's substantive claims match what the field currently knows, whether prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than conveniently, and whether domain-specific terms and reported results are used correctly. My interest is adjacent: terminology that is accurate inside the home field can still be exported misleadingly to other fields. Still, adjudicating factual correctness against the domain evidence base is the domain reviewer's charge, and I leave the score to them.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, DA role with methodology as secondary).** This covers whether the central thesis holds together on its own terms — whether the evidence actually bears the weight the conclusions place on it, and whether the chain of reasoning is free of fallacies serious enough to collapse the main argument. A cross-disciplinary reader is often the first to notice when a leap has occurred, because we lack the tacit background that lets insiders fill gaps silently. Even so, this dimension is not in my dispatch, so I record no score for it.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role — mine).** This is the dimension I own. The test is whether a competent reader from an adjacent discipline can enter the paper and leave with a correct understanding: are the framing and motivation legible without insider training, are the key constructs and operational definitions stated rather than assumed, are units, instruments, and outcome measures explained, and are the implications drawn for other fields actually supported by what the study did rather than asserted as decoration? Two failure modes matter symmetrically. One is opacity — jargon, unexplained shorthand, or an unstated theoretical frame that locks out neighbouring readers. The other is overreach — borrowing vocabulary, methods, or authority from another discipline, or claiming relevance to it, without the evidence or engagement to justify the claim. I will judge accessibility and substantiation together, since a paper can be perfectly readable and still make interdisciplinary promises it cannot keep.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, EiC role).** This dimension concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the venue's conventions. It overlaps with my own dimension at the edges — poor structure impedes outside readers — but the two are distinct: D5 asks whether the manuscript is well made as a document, whereas mine asks whether its conceptual content crosses disciplinary boundaries intact. The Editor-in-Chief scores D5; I do not.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, EiC role).** The question is whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it offers something original and significant enough to matter to that readership. This is adjacent to my remit in that breadth of relevance can bear on fit, but the judgement about scope, novelty, and significance relative to the venue's standards sits with the Editor-in-Chief, and I will not score it.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether an adjacent-field reader can follow the paper unaided — explicit motivation and framing, defined constructs and operationalisations, explained instruments/measures/units, glossed field-specific terminology and acronyms, self-contained figures and tables, and implications for other disciplines that are traced back to what this study actually measured rather than merely asserted.
what_triggers_block: Central constructs or the outcome measure are never operationally defined, or the paper stakes an explicit claim of relevance/applicability to another discipline with no supporting evidence or engagement with that discipline's literature, such that an adjacent-field reader would likely misunderstand or over-apply the findings.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument remains recoverable, but accessibility gaps recur — undefined acronyms or insider shorthand, a theoretical frame left implicit, figures or tables that cannot be read without body text, or generalisations stated more broadly than the study design supports.
