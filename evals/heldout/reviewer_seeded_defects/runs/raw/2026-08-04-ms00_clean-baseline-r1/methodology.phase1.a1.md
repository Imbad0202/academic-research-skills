## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, my owned dimension).** From a methods-audit standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study's design can actually bear the weight of the inferences drawn from it: whether the sampling frame and recruitment procedure are specified and defensible for the target population, whether the response rate and any non-response or attrition are quantified, whether instruments have documented provenance and reliability/validity evidence, whether measures and constructs are operationalised unambiguously, whether the analysis chosen matches the measurement level and design, and whether statistical reporting includes the quantities needed for verification (effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, exact test statistics, degrees of freedom, denominators for every percentage). It also covers reproducibility affordances — data, instrument, code, or protocol availability, ethics and consent documentation, and enough procedural detail that an independent team could repeat the study. The bar is the field's ordinary peer-review standard, not perfection: gaps matter in proportion to how much the paper's conclusions depend on them.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension concerns substantive correctness within the subject area: whether the claims are consistent with the current state of evidence, whether cited prior work is characterised accurately rather than stretched to fit, whether domain terminology and reported findings from the literature are used correctly, and whether the paper's positioning against established results is factually sound. I am not scoring this dimension; where a methodological observation bears on it (for instance, a measurement choice that misrepresents an established construct), I will surface the observation as narrative input to the domain reviewer rather than as a score.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, eligible for me as a secondary scorer).** This dimension tests the chain from data to conclusion: whether the central thesis stays internally consistent across abstract, results, and discussion; whether every claim is backed by evidence that is actually capable of supporting it; and whether the paper avoids reasoning defects that would collapse the argument. My contribution is specifically the inferential-validity slice — design-to-claim mismatch (for example, correlational or self-report evidence carrying causal or behavioural conclusions), conclusions that outrun the sample or the measured constructs, unacknowledged confounding or common-method concerns treated as settled, and results-to-discussion drift where the stated finding changes strength between sections. The dimension owner is the discourse-analysis reviewer; I score it from the methodology angle and defer to the owner on rhetorical structure.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension asks whether a competent reader from an adjacent field can enter the paper: whether framing, key definitions, and implications are made explicit rather than assumed, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home discipline is actually substantiated rather than asserted. I do not score it. If methodological terminology or a statistical claim is presented in a way that would mislead a non-specialist reader, I will note it descriptively for the owning reviewer.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension covers manuscript craft: organisation and section logic, clarity of exposition, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue formatting and reporting conventions. I do not score it. Where a presentation defect is inseparable from a methodological one — an unreadable table that hides the numbers needed to verify an analysis, for example — I will report the methodological consequence under my own dimensions and leave the presentation judgement to the owner.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** This dimension judges whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances knowledge in a way that matters to that readership, including originality relative to existing work and the significance of the increment. I do not score it. My only adjacent input is factual: whether the evidence base is strong enough that the claimed contribution could be sustained, stated as an observation rather than a contribution verdict.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit sampling frame and recruitment procedure, reported sample size with response rate and non-response handling, instrument provenance with reliability and validity evidence, unambiguous operationalisation of each construct, analyses matched to measurement level and design, complete statistical reporting (test statistics, degrees of freedom, exact p-values, effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, denominators for all percentages), stated handling of missing data, ethics/consent documentation, and reproducibility affordances such as available instrument, data, or analysis code.
what_triggers_block: Core reported results cannot be verified or reproduced from what is provided — for example, an analysis whose sample size, denominators, or test statistics are absent or mutually inconsistent, an undocumented or unvalidated instrument carrying the paper's primary measures, or an inferential procedure that is inappropriate for the data structure and is not remediable without new analysis.
what_triggers_warn: Design and analysis are broadly appropriate and the numbers hang together, but specific rigor elements are thin or missing in ways fixable by revision — incomplete effect-size or uncertainty reporting, unstated missing-data treatment, response rate not given, reliability coefficients omitted, ethics approval unmentioned, or reproducibility materials not referenced.
what_triggers_fatal: Evidence that the reported methodology could not have produced the reported results at all — internally impossible statistics, sample or measurement descriptions that contradict each other irreconcilably across the manuscript, analyses reported on data the stated design cannot have collected, or absence of any identifiable method for the paper's central empirical claim.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the strength and scope of the central claim stay constant from abstract through results to conclusions; whether each conclusion is licensed by the design that produced it; whether the inferential distance between what was measured and what is asserted is acknowledged; whether alternative explanations, confounding, and shared-method or shared-instrument concerns are addressed rather than dismissed; and whether stated limitations actually constrain the conclusions instead of being listed and then ignored.
what_triggers_block: The paper's headline conclusion requires an inference the design cannot support and this is not qualified anywhere — for instance, causal, longitudinal, or objective-behaviour claims resting on evidence incapable of establishing them, or conclusions generalised to populations the sampling procedure never reached, such that the central argument fails as written.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument holds but shows local slippage — findings stated more strongly in the abstract or discussion than in the results, a limitation acknowledged in one section yet contradicted by a claim elsewhere, or a plausible rival explanation left unaddressed without changing the overall verdict.
what_triggers_fatal: The thesis is self-defeating or evidentially empty — conclusions that directly contradict the reported results, an argument that presupposes what it sets out to demonstrate at its core, or a central claim for which no supporting evidence is presented anywhere in the manuscript.
