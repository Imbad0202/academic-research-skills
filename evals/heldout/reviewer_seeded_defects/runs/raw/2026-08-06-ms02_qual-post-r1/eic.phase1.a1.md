## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer):** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design and its execution would survive scrutiny by a competent specialist in the field: was the sampling or case-selection logic defensible, was the data collection and handling described well enough for a reader to judge it, are analytic and statistical claims reported with the detail the field expects, and has the author supplied enough procedural transparency that another researcher could in principle repeat or audit the work. I do not score it, but I record that a fatal defect here is decision-determining for the panel.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer):** This dimension covers whether the substantive content is factually and conceptually correct against the current state of the field: technical terms used in their accepted sense, cited prior work characterised faithfully rather than conveniently, reported results and figures internally and externally accurate, and no misattribution or overstatement of what earlier scholarship established. The domain reviewer owns this judgement; my role is to accept their finding and route it through the failure conditions.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, with the methodology reviewer also eligible):** The question is whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together on its own terms — whether stated evidence actually bears on the claims it is offered for, whether conclusions stay within the reach of the data, and whether the chain of reasoning is free of fallacies, equivocations, or unacknowledged leaps grave enough to hollow out the main argument. This is a mandatory dimension, so a block or fatal finding by its owner constrains the final decision regardless of how the paper reads.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer):** Here the concern is reachability across disciplinary boundaries: whether framing, key definitions, and stated implications can be followed by a serious reader from an adjacent field without insider vocabulary, and whether any claim that reaches beyond the home discipline is actually supported rather than asserted. It is high priority rather than mandatory, so a block here drives revision but cannot on its own force rejection.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as Editor-in-Chief):** This is my own dimension and concerns craft rather than substance: whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can locate the argument, whether exposition is clear and economical, whether figures, tables, and any apparatus are legible, correctly labelled, and genuinely informative, and whether the submission observes the target venue's conventions for structure, length, and presentation. Priority is normal, so defects here generate revision signals rather than existential ones.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as Editor-in-Chief):** This is the other dimension I score, and it is mandatory. I must judge whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue at all — topic, scope, and readership match — and whether it makes a contribution that is both original relative to existing literature and significant enough to justify the space it asks for. A paper can be clean, correct, and well written and still fail here; because the dimension is mandatory, a fatal finding on it is grounds for rejection under F1.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the manuscript has a discernible and signposted structure, whether the abstract and introduction accurately preview what follows, whether prose is clear and free of ambiguity that obscures meaning, whether figures and tables are numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, and referenced in text, and whether length and presentation conventions of the configured venue are observed.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken badly enough that a competent reader cannot reliably reconstruct what was done or what is being claimed — for example missing or unlabelled sections, figures or tables cited but absent or uninterpretable, or prose so unclear that key statements admit contradictory readings.
what_triggers_warn: The argument remains followable but presentation imposes avoidable friction — repetitive or padded passages, inconsistent terminology or heading levels, weak or overlong captions, misordered material, sloppy referencing format, or noticeable deviation from venue length and formatting conventions.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the topic, scope, and intended audience match the configured venue's stated remit; whether the manuscript states clearly what is new relative to existing literature; whether that novelty is more than incremental restatement; and whether the significance claimed is proportionate to the evidence and scope actually presented, including whether the manuscript's length and depth are adequate to sustain the contribution it advertises.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is real but too thin or too narrowly bounded for this venue's readership as submitted, or the novelty claim is asserted without positioning against the closest existing work, such that acceptance would require substantial added scope, comparison, or reframing.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are defensible but underargued — the novelty statement is vague or buried, the intended readership is left implicit, implications for the venue's audience are gestured at rather than developed, or the significance claims are somewhat inflated relative to what the study delivers.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript is outside the venue's remit altogether, or offers no identifiable original contribution — for example it merely restates established knowledge, duplicates prior published work without acknowledged advance, or presents opinion where the venue requires substantiated scholarly contribution.
