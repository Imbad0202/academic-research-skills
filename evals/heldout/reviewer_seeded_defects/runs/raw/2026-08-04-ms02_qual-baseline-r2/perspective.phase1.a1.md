## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, methodology role).** This dimension asks whether the study's design, data handling, statistical or qualitative analysis reporting, and reproducibility supports would survive scrutiny by competent reviewers in the paper's own field. From my vantage point as the cross-disciplinary reader, methodological rigour matters chiefly because readers from adjacent fields cannot independently reconstruct what was done unless sampling, instrumentation, and analytic steps are stated plainly; but ownership of this judgement sits with the methodology reviewer, and I do not score it.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, domain role).** This dimension covers whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with current evidence in its home domain, whether prior literature is represented faithfully rather than strawmanned or over-credited, and whether domain-specific terminology, instruments, and reported results are factually correct. My interest is adjacent: mis-stated domain terms propagate confusion outward to other fields. Scoring authority nonetheless rests with the domain reviewer, so I offer no score here.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, da/methodology roles).** Here the question is whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence actually bears the weight the conclusions place on it, and whether any fallacies, equivocations, or non-sequiturs hollow out the main argument. Coherence is the substrate on which cross-field transferability rests, since an argument that shifts its own terms mid-course cannot be exported anywhere. Even so, this dimension is owned by the devil's-advocate reviewer, and I do not score it.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, perspective role — mine).** This is my assigned dimension. I must judge whether the framing, key definitions, constructs, and stated implications are intelligible and usable by readers who work one field over rather than only by insiders of the paper's own specialty; and, separately, whether any claim the manuscript makes *about* other disciplines, other national or sectoral contexts, or general applicability is actually substantiated rather than asserted. Two distinct failure modes live here: opacity (jargon, undefined constructs, unstated scope conditions, implications addressed only to insiders) and overreach (borrowing a framework or claiming generality without the evidence or citation to license it). Passing requires that an informed outsider can state what was studied, in what setting, under what boundary conditions, and how far the conclusions travel.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, eic role).** This dimension concerns organisation, clarity of exposition, quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to the target venue's conventions. Poor structure and weak exhibits are also the most common practical barrier to outside readers, so my findings may overlap with this dimension in substance; scoring, however, belongs to the editor-in-chief role and I will not issue a score for it.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, eic role).** This asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it delivers an original contribution of significance appropriate to that readership. Cross-disciplinary reach and venue fit are related but not identical: a paper can be perfectly legible to outsiders yet contribute little, or be a genuine advance yet be addressed to the wrong audience. Because this dimension is owned by the editor-in-chief role, I record no score.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether core constructs (e.g., "quality culture", "quality assurance implementation", administrator role categories) are explicitly defined rather than assumed; whether the institutional, national, and regulatory context is stated so an outsider can locate the study's scope conditions; whether the abstract, introduction, and implications sections address readers beyond the home specialty; and whether every claim about other disciplines, other systems, or general applicability is backed by cited evidence or clearly labelled as conjecture.
what_triggers_block: Central constructs are used as load-bearing terms without definition, or the study's setting and scope conditions are so unspecified that an adjacent-field reader cannot determine what population, system, or level of analysis the conclusions apply to; or a broad interdisciplinary/generalisability claim is asserted with no supporting evidence or citation at all.
what_triggers_warn: Definitions and context are present but thin or scattered — insider jargon left unglossed in places, borrowed frameworks cited without explaining what is imported, implications written mainly for specialists, or transferability claims hedged inconsistently — such that an outsider can follow the paper only with extra effort.
