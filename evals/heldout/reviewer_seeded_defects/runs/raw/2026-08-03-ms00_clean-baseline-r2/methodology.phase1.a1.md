## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by me).** This dimension asks whether the study's design and its execution would survive a competent methodologist's audit in the manuscript's field. From a rigor standpoint that means: the design is fit for the causal or descriptive question actually being asked; the sampling frame, recruitment path, and eligibility rules are stated well enough that I could reconstruct who is and is not in the analysed dataset; measurement instruments have documented provenance and some evidence of reliability or validity; data cleaning, exclusions, and missing-data handling are declared rather than implied; the statistical or analytic procedures match the measurement scale and dependency structure of the data; uncertainty is reported (intervals, dispersion, exact test statistics) rather than replaced by bare point estimates or unqualified significance verdicts; and enough procedural detail, materials, or data-availability information exists that an independent team could attempt a replication. Ethics and consent affordances count here too when human participants are involved. I treat inference language as part of methodology: a design that cannot license a causal or generalising claim but is written as though it can is a rigor defect, not merely a wording defect.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This dimension concerns whether the manuscript's substantive claims are consistent with the current evidence base of its field, whether prior literature is represented faithfully rather than strawmanned or miscited, and whether field-specific constructs, instruments, and reported results are named and characterised correctly. I am not eligible to score this dimension and will not attempt to. My only legitimate interaction with it is indirect: if a methodological defect I document happens to bear on a factual claim, I should describe the methodological defect in my own terms and leave the evidentiary adjudication to the owner.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, co-eligible for me).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together internally and whether the evidence presented actually bears the weight the argument places on it. Read through a methodology lens, coherence is chiefly a question of inferential chain integrity: does each claim follow from the results as measured, or does the argument quietly upgrade an association into a mechanism, a self-report into a behaviour, a sample into a population, or a null result into evidence of equivalence? I will also check whether the stated research questions, the analyses actually run, and the conclusions drawn are the same three things, and whether limitations sections retract in fine print what the abstract and discussion assert in bold. Fallacies that matter for my scoring are those that, if corrected, would change the paper's headline conclusion — not stylistic overreach in a single sentence.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This dimension evaluates whether the framing, terminology, and stated implications are intelligible and useful to readers in adjacent fields, and whether any claims of interdisciplinary reach are actually substantiated rather than asserted. I am not eligible to score it and will not produce a score for it. If I encounter methodological under-specification that would also impede an outside reader's comprehension, I will record it under my own dimensions rather than trespassing into this one.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the EiC role).** This dimension covers manuscript organisation, clarity of exposition, the quality and self-sufficiency of figures and tables, and conformity to venue formatting and reporting conventions. I am not eligible to score it. There is a boundary I must respect carefully here: an undocumented analytic step is a D1 problem even though it manifests as a gap in prose, whereas an awkwardly sequenced section or an unlabelled axis with otherwise complete reporting is a D5 problem. I will keep my findings on the rigor side of that line.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the EiC role).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue and whether it advances something original and consequential for that readership. Novelty and significance judgements are not mine to make under this contract, and I will not smuggle a contribution verdict into a rigor score — a methodologically clean study that is uninteresting still passes D1, and a highly novel study with a broken design still fails it. I acknowledge the dimension exists and defer entirely to its owner.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit design statement matched to the research question; sampling frame, recruitment channel, eligibility rules, response/completion rate and final analysed N; instrument provenance with item wording or citation plus reliability/validity evidence; declared handling of missing data, exclusions, and any data transformation; analytic procedures appropriate to the measurement scale and dependency structure, with effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, and exact test statistics rather than bare p-thresholds; pre-specification versus exploratory status of each analysis; ethics approval and informed-consent statement; and reproducibility affordances such as available instruments, data, or analysis code.
what_triggers_block: One or more load-bearing methodological elements are absent or non-reconstructible — for example no stated sampling frame or analysed sample size, an undescribed or unvalidated measurement instrument carrying the main constructs, undisclosed exclusions or missing-data handling that could move the reported estimates, or inferential statistics reported without the test statistics, degrees of freedom, effect sizes, or uncertainty needed to verify them — such that a reader cannot judge whether the reported results follow from the data.
what_triggers_warn: The core design, sample, measures, and analyses are reconstructible and defensible, but rigor is degraded by recoverable omissions that a revision could supply: incomplete reliability evidence, thin justification for a modelling or scoring choice, missing data-availability or ethics statement, absent response-rate reporting, single-source or common-method measurement acknowledged only in passing, or uncertainty reported inconsistently across results.
what_triggers_fatal: The methodology as described cannot support any of the manuscript's substantive conclusions and no revision short of collecting new data could repair it — for instance the reported analyses are internally impossible given the stated design or sample, results are presented without any identifiable data source or procedure, apparent duplication or fabrication indicators appear in the reported numbers, or human-participant research is reported with no ethical basis at all.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A traceable chain from stated research questions through the analyses actually performed to the conclusions asserted, checked for construct slippage (self-report treated as behaviour, proxy treated as target), inferential upgrade (association narrated as causation or mechanism, sample narrated as population, null narrated as equivalence), consistency between abstract, results, discussion, and limitations, absence of unreported analyses implicitly relied upon, and explicit consideration of the most obvious rival explanation for the reported pattern.
what_triggers_block: The headline conclusion does not follow from the evidence presented — a causal, directional, mechanistic, or generalising claim is asserted on a design that cannot license it; the discussion rests on comparisons or analyses never reported; or the abstract and results contradict each other on the paper's central finding — so that correcting the inference would materially change what the paper claims.
what_triggers_warn: The central thesis survives scrutiny but the argument is locally overstated or under-defended: occasional causal phrasing in a correlational report, a plausible confound or alternative explanation left unaddressed, implications extended modestly beyond the studied population, or limitations that concede a constraint the discussion elsewhere ignores.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript has no coherent recoverable thesis at all — the stated aims, the reported results, and the conclusions describe mutually incompatible studies, or the central claim is contradicted by the paper's own reported data, so that no revision of the argument could be built on the evidence actually presented.
