## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** From the editorial chair's seat, this dimension asks whether the study's design, sampling, data handling, analysis, and statistical reporting would survive scrutiny by a competent methodologist in the manuscript's field, and whether the manuscript gives readers enough procedural detail to reproduce or at least audit what was done. It is a mandatory dimension, so a failure here has decision-level consequences for the whole panel. I do not score it: the methodology reviewer is the sole eligible role, and my job as EiC is to receive that verdict, weigh it against the failure-condition table, and avoid substituting my own methodological judgement for the specialist's.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** This covers whether the substantive claims are consistent with the current state of evidence in the paper's own field, whether prior literature is characterised faithfully rather than strawmanned or misattributed, and whether field-specific terminology, constructs, instruments, and reported results are used correctly. It is mandatory, so an error class here can drive rejection or major revision. Only the domain reviewer may score it; my editorial role is to ensure the domain verdict is recorded and propagated into the decision logic, not to second-guess the domain expert's factual reading.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by the discourse-analysis reviewer, with methodology also eligible).** The question is whether the manuscript's central thesis holds together internally, whether the evidence actually presented does the inferential work the claims require, and whether any reasoning defects — overreach from correlational data, equivocation between constructs, circularity, unearned generalisation — undercut the core argument. It is mandatory. Two roles are eligible, which means the panel may produce more than one reading; as EiC I do not add a score of my own but must reconcile eligible reviewers' verdicts under the cross-reviewer quantifiers when the failure conditions are evaluated.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** This asks whether the framing, operational definitions, and stated implications are legible and useful to readers from adjacent fields, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplinary boundaries is actually substantiated rather than asserted. It is high priority rather than mandatory, which means a `block` here triggers major revision under F4 but cannot on its own trigger the rejection pathway. The perspective reviewer alone scores it; I consume the result.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by me as EiC).** This is my dimension. It concerns whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow the argument from problem statement through method, results, and interpretation; whether the exposition is clear and free of ambiguity that obscures meaning; whether figures, tables, and captions are self-contained, correctly labelled, and consistent with the text; and whether the submission observes the configured venue's structural and formatting conventions, including abstract, reporting, and reference practices. Priority is `normal`, so it carries no fatal pathway: the worst it can do alone is push the decision to minor revision under F5, and it contributes to no mandatory-dimension tally.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by me as EiC).** This is my second and weightier dimension. It asks two linked questions: does this manuscript belong in the configured venue given its scope, audience, and typical article type; and does it advance something original and consequential enough to justify the space it would occupy in front of that readership. Because it is mandatory, my verdict here feeds F1, F2, and F3 directly — a fatal finding routes to rejection, a `block` routes to major revision, and a `warn` combines with other mandatory warnings to do the same. I must therefore hold this judgement to a defensible evidentiary standard rather than treating it as taste, and I must be explicit about whether a shortfall is about fit, about contribution, or about both.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A discernible and conventional section architecture; a title and abstract that accurately preview the actual study; clear operational definitions introduced before they are used; figures and tables that are numbered, captioned, self-explanatory, and numerically consistent with the narrative text; complete and internally consistent citation and reference apparatus; and prose whose sentences resolve to unambiguous claims at the length and depth the venue expects for this article type.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition is broken badly enough that a competent reader cannot reconstruct what was done or what was found — for example, results reported with no locatable method for producing them, figures or tables that contradict the text or are referenced but absent, an abstract that describes a different study than the body, or missing structural components the venue treats as non-optional.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but degraded — uneven section proportions, terminology introduced inconsistently, tables lacking units or denominators, imprecise or hedged sentences that force re-reading, incomplete reference entries, or formatting departures from venue convention that a copy-editing pass and modest restructuring would fix.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit statement of scope and audience that matches the configured venue; a stated gap or question the field would recognise as open; a contribution — empirical, conceptual, methodological, or synthetic — that is articulated and then actually delivered by the body of the paper; evidence that the work is positioned against, and adds to, existing literature rather than restating it; and a scale and depth of treatment proportionate to what the venue's readership expects.
what_triggers_block: The contribution as delivered falls materially short of what would warrant publication in this venue — the study is a narrow replication or descriptive exercise presented without new insight, the stated novelty is not supported by the work actually reported, or the topic sits outside the venue's scope such that substantial reframing, additional data, or redirection to a different outlet would be required.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are real but under-argued — the novelty claim is asserted rather than demonstrated against named prior work, the implications for the venue's readership are left implicit, the scope is broader or narrower than the venue's typical article, or the contribution is genuine but modest and needs sharper positioning to justify itself.
what_triggers_fatal: There is no defensible contribution at all for any venue — for instance the manuscript's central claim is already established and merely restated, the work is a duplicate or salami slice of prior publication, or the submission is so far outside the configured venue's remit and so thin in substance that no revision within the authors' control could make it publishable here.
