## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor (mandatory, owned by the methodology reviewer).** The challenge here is whether the study's design can actually bear the weight of what it is asked to support: whether sampling, instrument construction, data handling, and statistical reporting are described in enough detail that a skeptical reader could reconstruct the analysis, and whether the reported numbers are internally consistent and appropriate to the design. An adversarial reading asks what the design cannot rule out, whether inferential claims exceed what a given design licenses, and whether reproducibility affordances (instrument items, data availability, analysis specification) exist at all rather than merely being gestured at. Absence of detail is itself a finding, not a neutral gap.

**D2 — domain_accuracy (mandatory, owned by the domain reviewer).** The adversarial question is whether the manuscript's domain claims survive contact with the literature it invokes: whether constructs and technical terms are used with their established meanings, whether cited prior work is characterised as that work actually characterises itself, and whether reported results are consistent with the current evidence base or, if discordant, are acknowledged and argued for rather than silently asserted. Misattributed findings, borrowed terminology used loosely, and citation of a source for a claim it does not make are all failures of this dimension regardless of how well the prose reads.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence (mandatory, owned by me as devil's advocate; also open to the methodology reviewer).** This is the dimension I own, and I read it as a stress test rather than a summary: does the central thesis hold together when its premises are separated from its conclusions and checked one at a time? I look for claims that outrun their stated evidence, for inferential leaps (especially from association to mechanism, or from self-report to behaviour), for unstated premises doing load-bearing work, for conclusions that contradict earlier qualifications, and for hedges in one section that are silently dropped in another. A paper can be methodologically clean and still incoherent if its argument does not follow from its own findings; conversely, weak evidence honestly bounded is not automatically an argumentative failure. My job is to find the strongest available objection to the paper's own reasoning and see whether the manuscript has already answered it.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority, owned by the perspective reviewer).** The adversarial reading asks whether an intelligent reader from an adjacent field could follow the framing without importing assumptions the paper never states, whether key constructs are defined rather than assumed shared, and whether any claim of interdisciplinary reach is actually substantiated by the work presented rather than asserted in the discussion. Claims of broad applicability are a promise, and unsupported promises are a defect.

**D5 — writing_and_structure (normal priority, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** Here the question is whether the manuscript's organisation serves the argument or obscures it: whether sections carry the content their headings promise, whether exposition is clear enough that ambiguity cannot be mistaken for depth, whether figures and tables are self-sufficient and accurate, and whether venue conventions are respected. Poor structure is adversarially relevant because it can hide argumentative gaps rather than merely inconvenience the reader.

**D6 — venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory, owned by the editor-in-chief role).** The challenge is whether there is a genuine, non-trivial contribution and whether it belongs in the configured venue's readership. Adversarially, this means asking what a reader knows after the paper that they did not know before, whether the increment over existing work is real or restated, and whether the scope and depth of the work match the venue's stated bar rather than falling short of it or being misdirected to the wrong audience.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each stated conclusion follows from the manuscript's own reported evidence — I will extract the central thesis and its supporting claims, map every conclusion back to the specific evidence offered for it, check for undeclared load-bearing premises, association-to-causation or self-report-to-behaviour leaps, hedges introduced in one section and dropped in another, internal contradictions between abstract, results, and discussion, and whether the paper anticipates and answers the strongest objection to its own reasoning.
what_triggers_block: One or more central conclusions rest on an inferential leap the manuscript never acknowledges or defends (for example a mechanistic or causal claim asserted from purely associational or self-reported evidence, or a headline claim contradicted by the paper's own reported findings), such that the core argument does not survive scrutiny without substantial reworking.
what_triggers_warn: The central thesis is defensible but locally weakened — peripheral claims are over-stated relative to their evidence, hedges are applied inconsistently across sections, an important alternative explanation is left unaddressed, or the abstract/discussion frames the finding more strongly than the results section supports, all repairable by qualification and rewriting without changing the paper's substance.
what_triggers_fatal: The argument is irreparably incoherent — the thesis is self-contradictory, or the stated conclusion is directly refuted by the manuscript's own reported evidence, or the argument is circular with its conclusion embedded in its premises, so that no revision short of a different study or a different thesis could rescue it.
