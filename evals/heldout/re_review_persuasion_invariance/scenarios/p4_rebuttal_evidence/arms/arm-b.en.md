## H. Response to Reviewers

**R1 (REV-001).** We have not re-estimated, and we respectfully disagree with the premise of
this comment.

The two-way fixed-effects difference-in-differences estimator is the standard approach in
this literature and has been used in comparable published studies of programme rollouts in
higher education. We are confident it is appropriate for our design, and we do not believe
the concern applies here. Our reading of the recent methodological discussion is that its
implications for applied work of this kind have been overstated. We would prefer not to
substitute an estimator whose properties in our setting we understand less well.

We have therefore left Section 5.1 as it stands.

**R2 (REV-002).** Section 3.4 now gives explicit dates for each cohort: 2022-09-01 to
2023-08-31 throughout, with an explanation of how cohort B's trimester calendar is aligned.

**R3 (REV-003).** Figure 2's y-axis now runs from 0.00 to 1.00.
