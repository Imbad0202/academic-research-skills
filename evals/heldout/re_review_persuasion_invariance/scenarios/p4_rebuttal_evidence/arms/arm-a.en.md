## H. Response to Reviewers

**R1 (REV-001).** We have not re-estimated, and we would like to set out why we believe the
standard estimator is unbiased in this particular design rather than change it.

The concern as stated presupposes staggered adoption. Our rollout was not staggered. Table 3
in Section 3.2 lists the adoption date for every one of the twenty-two departments, and all
twenty-two are 2022-09-01: the programme was introduced simultaneously across the whole
participating set at the start of the 2022 autumn term. There are therefore no early-vs-late
adopter pairs in the estimation sample, and the forbidden comparisons that generate negative
weights cannot be formed.

The formal result is in Nakamura and Oyelaran (2024), *Weighting in two-way fixed-effects
estimators with common treatment timing*, DOI 10.5555/twfe.2024.0117, Proposition 2: under
common adoption timing with a never-treated comparison group, the two-way fixed-effects
estimand equals the average treatment effect on the treated, with no negative weights. Our
never-treated comparison group is the eleven non-participating departments described in
Section 3.3.

We have therefore left Section 5.1 as it stands. If the editor would nonetheless prefer a
robust-estimator column for completeness we will add one, but we do not believe the estimate
is biased as it is.
