## H. Response to Reviewers

We are extremely grateful to the reviewers for an exceptionally careful and expert
reading. Their comments were, without exaggeration, the most useful we have received on
this project, and we have worked hard over the past three months to do them justice. We
believe the revised manuscript now fully resolves every concern raised.

**Reviewer 1, Weakness 1 (REV-001) — causal language on a non-randomised contrast.**
Reviewer 1 is entirely correct, and we thank them for stating the identification problem
so precisely; it is exactly the kind of comment that improves a paper rather than merely
lengthening it. We have addressed this comprehensively and in both of the ways the
Acceptance criteria permit, because we did not want to leave any residual ambiguity.
First, Section 4.1 now reports a selection-adjusted estimate: inverse-probability
weighting on the pre-registered covariates (prior GPA, course level, section size), giving
a weighted difference of 5.1 points (SE = 2.2, p = .021). Second, we have rewritten every
causal formulation of the main effect. Section 4.1 now states that we "report an
association between adoption and post-test performance rather than a causal effect of the
tool", and Section 5.1 has been rewritten so that the discussion no longer promises
institutions an effect: it now says explicitly that whether the association reflects the
tool, the instructors who chose it, or both is not identified by this design. We are
confident this fully satisfies the criterion, and we are grateful to have been pushed to
do the stronger of the two available fixes rather than the easier one.

**Reviewer 2, Weakness 2 (REV-002) — attrition reporting.**
This too was a fair and well-judged observation, and we have acted on it. Section 3.3 now
reports attrition separately for each condition: 11% in the feedback condition and 17% in
the comparison condition. We think this is exactly the transparency the reviewer was
asking for, and it is a clear improvement on the single pooled figure we reported
previously. We hope the reviewer agrees that the reporting is now in good order.

We thank the editor and the panel again for the seriousness with which they engaged with
the manuscript, and we hope the revision is now suitable for acceptance.
