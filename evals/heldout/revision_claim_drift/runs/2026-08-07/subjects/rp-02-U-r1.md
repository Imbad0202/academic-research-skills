Students in the guided reflection condition scored 0.24 SD higher than comparison students on the delayed concept measure (95% CI [0.07, 0.41], p = .006). The Lumen Classroom Study enrolled 624 students across 18 secondary schools, 52.6% of whom participated in guided reflection. This result indicates that structured reflection supports durable conceptual organization, particularly when prompts require students to explain relationships rather than recall definitions (Adebayo & Lin, 2022).

Prior achievement moderated this benefit: students with lower prior achievement gained more from reflection (β = -0.18, SE = 0.07, p = .012), whereas the interaction with classroom size was smaller and imprecise (β = 0.06, 95% CI [-0.03, 0.15]). The pattern supports the interpretation that guided explanation is most valuable for students with less developed prior knowledge, although replication across a broader range of classrooms is needed before generalizing this conclusion (Mireles, 2019).
