In this sample, students who participated in guided reflection scored 0.24 SD higher on the delayed concept measure than comparison students (95% CI [0.07, 0.41], p = .006). The Lumen Classroom Study enrolled 624 students across 18 secondary schools, of whom 52.6% participated in the guided reflection condition. This result suggests that structured reflection may support durable conceptual organization, particularly when prompts require students to explain relationships rather than recall definitions (Adebayo & Lin, 2022).

In the moderation analysis, prior achievement was negatively associated with the incremental benefit of reflection (β = -0.18, SE = 0.07, p = .012), whereas the interaction with classroom size was smaller and imprecise (β = 0.06, 95% CI [-0.03, 0.15]). We regard these moderation results as preliminary: the pattern is consistent with the possibility that students with less developed prior knowledge may benefit more from guided explanation, and replication in a broader range of classrooms is needed before drawing a general conclusion (Mireles, 2019).
