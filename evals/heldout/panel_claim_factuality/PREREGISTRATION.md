# Eval preregistration: panel claim factuality (#1002)

Frozen with `plan.json`, `rubric.md`, `prompts/`, `scripts/extract_panel_claims.py`,
and `scripts/dispatch_panel_claim_audit.py` in one commit, before any judge call
on a claim. A change to any of them after that call is a new plan
version, logged as an amendment; the frozen files are never edited in place.

## Bars

- contrast_detection: per judge, at least 0.95 of the false contrast sentences are judged `false` by both replicates. A miss, or a missing verdict on any contrast sentence, voids that judge's claim verdicts.
- contrast_false_flag: per judge, at most 0.05 of the accurate contrast sentences are judged `false` by either replicate. A miss voids that judge's claim verdicts.
- With one valid judge the run is exploratory (per-judge counts only, no decision); with none it is void.
- false_claim_rate: adjudicated false claims divided by all 805 claims, overall and by seat. At least 0.03, with both judges past the contrast gate, means plan the second part of #1002 (a check between the panel and the synthesizer).
- panels_with_false_claim: the share of the 17 panels with at least one adjudicated false claim. At least 0.30, with both judges past the contrast gate, also means plan the second part. Below both decision bars, the second part is not planned on this evidence.

## Arms

Each arm is a judge, a generator, or a verifier, not a treatment. The codex and agy calls run through a subscription CLI, so they carry no per-call charge; their settled cost is `unknown` and the binding limits are the subscription usage limits and time. From version 4 the Google judge runs on the paid Gemini API, and its cost is settled from the `usageMetadata` of each response.

- judge `gpt-6-astra` (OpenAI family): `codex exec --json`, model_reasoning_effort=xhigh, `skills.include_instructions=false`, read-only, empty working directory, prompt on stdin. The read-only sandbox still lets the model read files, so a reply whose event stream shows any item other than a message or reasoning (a command, a file read, a tool) is a transport failure and stops the run. Price-table estimate at 10/50 USD per MTok: about 0.14 USD per item at 4,000 in and 2,000 out tokens; a batch is about 7,000 in tokens.
- judge `gemini-4-argon-medium` (Google family; `gemini-4-argon-high` in versions 3 to 6): the Gemini API `generateContent` method, model `gemini-4-argon`, `thinkingLevel` medium from version 7 (high in versions 4 to 6), `maxOutputTokens` 32,768 (the most one non-streaming request returns, thinking included; 65,536 in version 6), no tools, no system instruction, and one user message that holds the batch prompt. `scripts/dispatch_panel_claim_audit.py gemini-api-call` sends it from an empty working directory. The key is read from `GEMINI_API_KEY`, passed only to this helper (never to the codex or agy processes), and sent only in the request header; the helper follows no redirect, prints a response body only for an HTTP 200, and on any other outcome saves no response body and writes only the status code or the exception type, so no error text that could echo the key reaches a file. An error body, a non-200 status, a model version other than exactly `gemini-4-argon`, a finish reason other than `STOP`, or a reply part other than text (a function call, code) is a transport failure and stops the run, with one exception from version 5: a call that ends with finish reason `MAX_TOKENS` (thinking spent the whole output limit), or from version 8 `MALFORMED_RESPONSE` (a provider-side generation failure), is sent once more, unchanged, under its call id plus `-r`, and a second failure of that call stops the run. Safety and every other finish reason still stop the run. Each logged call records its tokens in, its tokens out (the total minus the prompt, so thinking is included), and its cost. Price: 2 USD per MTok in and 10 USD per MTok out, thinking included (Google's pricing page, rates through 2026-11-30); a batch is about 7,000 in tokens, and the pilot measures the output. In v1 and v2 this judge was `gemini-3.8-flash-high` and in v3 "Gemini 4 Argon (High)", both through `agy -p` (Amendments).

(Through version 3 the Google judge used the same agy flags and step rule.)
- verifier `gpt-6-astra`: the codex settings above, one call per manuscript over its mechanically valid pairs.
- Context the agy transport adds. From version 4 only the generator runs through agy, and its pairs were generated before version 4. ## Decoys

- False contrast sentences: up to 20 per manuscript (60 in all), each written by the generator from one explicit manuscript statement, changed in one to four tokens so that the manuscript contradicts it. They enter the batches as ordinary items with neutral ids, so the judge cannot tell them from real claims by id or position. A judge must rate at least 0.95 of them `false` in both replicates; a pass shows that the judge, with this prompt and these batches, finds a contradiction that the manuscript states.
- Accurate contrast sentences: the matched accurate twin of each false sentence, in a different batch from its twin. A judge must rate at most 0.05 of them `false` in either replicate; a pass shows that the judge does not call supported text false.
- A pair enters only when it passes the four mechanical checks in `scripts/dispatch_panel_claim_audit.py` (one sentence each, 8 to 50 whitespace-delimited words, one to four changed tokens, a verbatim evidence quote) and the verifier gives one answer for it, with all eight questions in `prompts/contrast_verify.md` true. The script's one-sentence check never rejects a period after a lone letter (an initial, "U.S."); the verifier's eighth question, single_sentences, covers the cases characters cannot settle, such as "group A. The". Two known limits of the script's check, accepted before the freeze: an initial written with a separate combining accent (E followed by U+0301) is still counted as a sentence end, so such a pair is rejected; and a period after a non-letter character such as "½" is not counted as an end, so the verifier's single_sentences answer is the only check there. The build reads the pairs back from the logged generator and verifier outputs and runs the mechanical checks again. Fewer than 15 accepted pairs for a manuscript stops the run.
- Limit: a contrast sentence is one plain sentence, while a real claim is a paragraph, so the decoys test contradiction-finding, not the full reading of a long claim. The verifier is the same model as one judge, so the accepted pairs can favour what that model finds; the second judge is the cross-check.

## Stop rules

- A judge that misses either contrast bar is void; when both judges miss, the measurement is void and no claim rate is published.
- A claim inventory other than 17 bundles and 805 claims, or any extraction problem: stop before any judge call.
- Fewer than 15 or more than 20 accepted contrast pairs for any manuscript, a repeated pair or sentence, or a pair with any check not true: stop before any judge call on claims.
- A batch whose output cannot be read after its one retry: stop the run.
- A transport failure, decided from the CLI's event stream or the API response fields and never from searching its text: a nonzero exit, a failed turn or error event, no completed turn or successful result, a finish reason other than `STOP`, an empty reply, or any step other than the reply (a command, a file read, a tool, a skill, a subagent, a function call): stop the run without a retry. The only exception is the Gemini judge's one resend after `MAX_TOKENS` or `MALFORMED_RESPONSE` (Arms, versions 5 and 8). No replacement model or transport without a new plan version.
- An interrupted call, an output that does not match its logged call, a decisions file or seat key from another build, or any input changed since the build: stop.
- A reply that uses a tool, a skill, or a subagent: stop, as a transport failure.
- `budget_exhausted`: stop. `run --judge gemini` requires `--cap-usd`, and a new Gemini call starts only when the cost of every Gemini call logged under the runs directory (the pilot included), plus the new call's worst case (one token per prompt byte and the full output limit), fits under the cap. A logged call without usage, or with usage that cannot be priced (the prompt and total counts missing, not integers, out of order, or above 10 million), counts at its worst case. Only one Gemini run at a time may use a runs directory (a lock file), so the two replicates run one after the other. The Gemini pilot has a 5 USD cap. The maintainer sets the full-run cap when approving the projection, at most 100 USD under this plan version; the script refuses a cap that is not above 0 and at most 100. A new paid call after 2026-11-30, when the price table changes, stops until the table is updated; reusing a logged output does not.
- The full run starts only after the maintainer approves the pilot projection.

## Results (version 8 run, 2026-10-24)

- **Run.** Both replicates of both judges finished all 104 batches; every one of the 805 claims has a readable verdict from each judge in each replicate. The Gemini judge cost 4.19 USD. Finish reason `MALFORMED_RESPONSE` occurred twice (replicate 1 `b079`, replicate 2 `b006`), and each batch answered on its one resend. The codex judge's outputs are the version 3 outputs, reused by prompt hash (907 of 907 calls in each replicate).
- **Contrast gate.** Both judges pass: each judge marked all 51 false contrast sentences `false` and marked none of the 51 accurate ones `false`. The run status is `decision_relevant`.
- **Agreement lists.** Agreed false 12, agreed not false 701, cross-judge divergence 54, replicate instability 38, missing 0. Raw `false` verdicts per replicate: codex 78 and 86, Gemini 34 and 30.
- **Decision rule.** The 12 agreed-false claims fall in 6 of the 17 panels (0.35). Agreed-false claims count without adjudication, and adjudication covers only the other 92 claims, so it can add false claims but cannot remove these. Under every adjudication outcome, panels_with_false_claim is therefore at least 0.35, above the 0.30 bar, and both judges pass the contrast gate: the decision rule is met, and the second part of #1002 is planned.
- **Adjudication deferred.** On 2026-10-24 the maintainer deferred the 92 adjudication decisions, because they cannot change the decision. The headline false_claim_rate is not computed; it lies between 12/805 (0.015) and 104/805 (0.129). Per-seat rates are not computed. If adjudication resumes, two facts apply: the maintainer knew the decision was already settled, and item `i0003` was discussed with the assistant that ran the study before any decision was recorded, so its decision is not blind to that discussion.
- **Limits.** The 12 agreed-false claims rest on the two judges' agreement and the mechanical evidence check; no person verified them. Adjudication has one maintainer, so no inter-rater agreement exists.
