How to match:
- Match items by content and reviewer, not by number. The response may number its items differently from the list above, for example R2-1 for Reviewer 2's positive remark and R2-2 for comment 2-1.
- Splitting one comment into several items is fine; the coach is told to split a comment that makes more than one request.
- An extra item that is not one of the nine, such as a row for a positive remark or a note that two items share one source paragraph, does not fail this check.

Pass only if all nine comments pass.
