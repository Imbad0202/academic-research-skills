Subject: Decision on Manuscript JHE-2026-0417 — Major Revision

Dear Dr. Author,

Thank you for submitting "Peer Feedback and Revision Behaviour in First-Year Writing Courses" to the Journal of Higher Education Pedagogy. Both reviewers see merit in the work, but they also raise substantive concerns. I am therefore inviting a major revision, due within 60 days.

Reviewer 1 writes: The literature review stops at 2019 and misses the recent wave of work on AI-assisted feedback, so the authors should position their contribution against that literature. I also found the coding scheme for the interview data underspecified: how many coders were involved, what was the inter-coder agreement, and how were disagreements resolved? Finally, the paper would benefit from a clearer statement of practical implications for instructors, which currently appears only in the last paragraph and reads as an afterthought.

Reviewer 2 writes: The authors employ a randomized controlled design but never report the randomization procedure or allocation concealment; this is a serious omission for an RCT and must be addressed before publication. The survey instrument's reliability is reported but the items themselves are not included, so please add them as an appendix. I also think the sample of 14 interviewees is too small to support the strong claims in section 5.2, so either soften those claims or explain the saturation logic.

From my side, please also shorten the abstract to 200 words in line with journal policy.

Kind regards,
Handling Editor
